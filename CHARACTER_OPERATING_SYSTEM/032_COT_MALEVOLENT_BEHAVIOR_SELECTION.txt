=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

TRAIT_GATE (at least one):
- NARCISSISM HIGH or VERY_HIGH.
- MACHIAVELLIANISM HIGH or VERY_HIGH.
- PSYCHOPATHY HIGH or VERY_HIGH.

CUE_GATE (at least one, paired with trait gate):
- Narrative input presents dominance, manipulation, exploitation, or thrill-seeking opportunity.
- Relational target presents vulnerability, dependency, or asymmetric-power cue.
- Short-term gain cue includes deception or concealment context.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:

=== SECTION: FIRING_DISCIPLINE ===

DISCIPLINE_RULES:
- If no trait reaches HIGH, emit only EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM.
- Trait gate alone is insufficient; cue gate must be satisfied.
- VERY_HIGH lowers cue threshold but does not waive explicit narrative cue.
- IMPULSE_STRENGTH calibrates to cue strength, not trait strength alone.

=== SECTION: TRAIT_GATING ===

- Enable dominance and entitlement impulse candidates.

ON_HIGH_MACHIAVELLIANISM:
- Enable strategic manipulation candidates when risk-reward cue aligns.

ON_HIGH_PSYCHOPATHY:
- Enable callous and thrill-seeking candidates under trigger cues.

THRESHOLD_RULE:
- Generate impulses only with VERY_HIGH trait or HIGH trait plus strong cue alignment.
