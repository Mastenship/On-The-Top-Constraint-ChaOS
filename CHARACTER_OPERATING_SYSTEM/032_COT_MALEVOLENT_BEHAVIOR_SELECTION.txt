=== SECTION: COT_MALEVOLENT_BEHAVIOR_SELECTION ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT defines how Dark Triad-gated tendencies may generate manipulative, exploitative, or callous behavioral impulses under cue alignment. It is a bounded impulse generator, not a direct state mutator.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- DECISION synthesis via malevolent impulse candidates
- relational strategy overlays via proposal-only flow
Is Informed By:
- SCHEME_DARK_TRIAD
- SCHEME_HEXACO
- [STATE_RELATIONAL]
- stress and context cues
- narrative input

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when both a trait gate and a cue gate are satisfied in the current cycle.

TRAIT_GATE (at least one):
- NARCISSISM at HIGH or VERY_HIGH percentile.
- MACHIAVELLIANISM at HIGH or VERY_HIGH percentile.
- PSYCHOPATHY at HIGH or VERY_HIGH percentile.

CUE_GATE (at least one, paired with the above):
- Narrative input presents a dominance, manipulation, exploitation, or thrill-seeking opportunity.
- A relational target presents a vulnerability, dependency, or asymmetric-power cue.
- A short-term gain cue with deception or concealment context.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not generate exploit/manipulate/dominate/mask impulses.
- Do not propose deception-leakage-control overlays.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: FIRING_DISCIPLINE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: For characters whose Dark Triad percentiles are all AVERAGE or below, this COT is effectively dormant.

DISCIPLINE_RULES:
- If no trait reaches HIGH percentile, this COT MUST emit only the EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry every cycle.
- Trait at HIGH percentile alone is insufficient; the cue gate above must also be satisfied per cycle.
- Trait at VERY_HIGH percentile lowers the cue threshold but does not waive it; an explicit narrative cue MUST still be present.
- IMPULSE_STRENGTH MUST be calibrated to cue strength, not trait strength alone.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: TRAIT_GATING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Gate malevolent options by trait threshold and cue alignment, respecting DYNAMICS_BODY.CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE for dominant-trait selection.

ON_HIGH_NARCISSISM:
- Enable dominance and entitlement impulse candidates.

ON_HIGH_MACHIAVELLIANISM:
- Enable strategic manipulation candidates when risk-reward cue aligns.

ON_HIGH_PSYCHOPATHY:
- Enable callous and thrill-seeking candidates under trigger cues.

THRESHOLD_RULE:
- Impulses generated only if:
  - relevant trait is VERY_HIGH percentile, or
  - HIGH percentile plus strong cue alignment.
