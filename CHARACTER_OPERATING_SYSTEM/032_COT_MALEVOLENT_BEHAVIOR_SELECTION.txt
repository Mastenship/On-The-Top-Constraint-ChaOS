=== SECTION: HONESTY_HUMILITY_MODULATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: HONESTY_HUMILITY is sincerity, fairness, greed avoidance, and modesty per SCHEME_HEXACO. Its low pole is the core the dark traits share, so it moves with them; its high pole opposes them. Resolve it against each enabling dark trait per DYNAMICS_BODY.CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE.

SAME_DIRECTION:
- VERY_LOW HONESTY_HUMILITY lowers the cue threshold for an enabling dark trait. It opens no gate by itself.
- Same-direction traits do not stack. Impulse strength follows the higher-precedence trait and cue strength.

OPPOSING:
- VERY_HIGH HONESTY_HUMILITY prevails over a HIGH dark trait. That trait enables no candidates.
- A VERY_HIGH dark trait prevails over HONESTY_HUMILITY at any band.
- HIGH HONESTY_HUMILITY yields to a HIGH dark trait.

JUSTIFICATION_NOTE:
- Cite the dominant trait in JUSTIFICATION per DYNAMICS_BODY.CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE.VERIFIER_NOTE.
