=== SECTION: UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Defines which COTs and governance invocations execute, in what order, what they propose into [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY], and the canonical proposal and impulse schemas. System-wide API spec for the proposal write surface.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: All COT templates and STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT tie-break behavior.
Is Informed By: STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY and CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.

=== SECTION: TWO_PHASE_GOVERNANCE_ORDER ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT executes twice per ordinary cycle as the same single-writer authority operating in two modes.

PRIMARY_PASS:
- Executes at PRIORITY_16.
- Consumes ordinary proposals generated before verification.
- Commits lawful character-state, temporary-modifier, and permitted governance-corrective proposals available at primary intake.
- Writes primary commit audit closure.

VERIFICATION:
- Executes at PRIORITY_17.
- Reads primary commit audit, scratchpad outputs, committed state, and governance runtime.
- Emits verification trace.
- May append governance-runtime corrective proposals to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].
- Does not directly mutate persistent state.

POST_VERIFICATION_PASS:
- Executes at PRIORITY_18.
- Consumes only verifier-originated governance-runtime proposals generated at PRIORITY_17.
- Commits or suppresses verification-result, quarantine-engage, quarantine-clear, and reason-code updates before scratchpad wipe.
- Emits post-verification commit audit closure and trace.
- Suppresses all non-governance-runtime proposals with POST_VERIFICATION_TARGET_FORBIDDEN.
- Suppresses governance-runtime proposals not generated by COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION with POST_VERIFICATION_SOURCE_INVALID.

SCRATCHPAD_WIPE_POINT:
- COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD is wiped only after PRIORITY_18 completes.

=== SECTION: SPECIAL_CASE_EXECUTION ===

COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS:
- Outside ordinary cycle execution order.
- Invoked only during first-time character seeding or explicit instantiation workflow.
- Does not participate in normal runtime tie-break precedence unless the system is in instantiation mode.

=== SECTION: SYSTEM_WIDE_WRITE_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: All COTs follow this policy.

POLICY:
- COTs do not write directly into persistent STATE_ modules.
- COTs write intended persistent changes only as proposals into [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].
- COTs may write impulses into [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][ACTIVE_IMPULSE_REGISTRY] when their template permits.
- COTs append trace entries to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][CASCADE_TRACE].
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT is the only writer to persistent STATE_*, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, and STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME.
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_PRIMARY_PASS commits ordinary proposals.
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS commits only verifier-originated STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME proposals.
- All proposals MUST use canonical bracket paths with canonical module roots.
- Character-cognition COTs MUST NOT read GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL modules. Read-boundary violations are raised by COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION as READ_BOUNDARY_VIOLATION.

=== SECTION: PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every proposal entry uses this schema.

REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- PROPOSAL_ID: PUP_<unique>
- SOURCE_COT: canonical COT name
- TARGET_MODULE: canonical module root
- TARGET_FIELD: canonical bracket-path
- OPERATION_TYPE: SET | TRANSITION | ADD_MODIFIER | REMOVE_MODIFIER | CAP | VETO
- PROPOSED_VALUE
- STRENGTH: QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
- MECHANISM_TAG
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION | INFERENCE | SPECULATION | COMMITTED_FACT | TOOL_REQUEST
- JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION | INFERENTIAL_LEAP | UNJUSTIFIED_LEAP | GOAL_DIRECTED | TRAIT_GATED
- JUSTIFICATION

CONDITIONALLY_REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- EVIDENCE_REF: required when EPISTEMIC_TAG = COMMITTED_FACT
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: required when proposal triggers an override gate or targets STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME as a governance corrective
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME is present
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME is present

OPTIONAL_FIELDS:
- NOTES

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: JUSTIFICATION, NOTES, NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE, and SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE are subject to translation-before-commit normalization. All other fields are write-surface tokens and remain untouched.

=== SECTION: NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every COT in PRIORITY_01 through PRIORITY_15 appends exactly one TRACE_ENTRY per cycle, even when no triggers fire, unless the COT is explicitly exempt from FIRES_WHEN and defines an always-run trace form.

EMPTY_CYCLE_TRACE_ENTRY:
COT_NAME: <canonical COT name>
KEY_OUTPUTS:
- NO_TRIGGERS_FIRED
GENERATED_IMPULSES:
- [NONE]
PROPOSED_UPDATES_COUNT: 0
NOTES:
- [Brief one-line context observation or NONE]

VERIFICATION:
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION confirms exactly one TRACE_ENTRY per priority slot from PRIORITY_01 through PRIORITY_17 before post-verification pass.
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS emits its own trace at PRIORITY_18 after verification.
- Missing trace entry before verification: WARN-class COT_TRACE_MISSING.
- Missing post-verification trace is recorded in the post-verification audit as POST_VERIFICATION_TRACE_MISSING.

=== SECTION: UPDATE_VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: After STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_PRIMARY_PASS, COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION verifies:
- All primary-pass proposals are COMMITTED, SUPPRESSED, DOWNGRADED, or VETOED with audit entries.
- Every primary-pass PROPOSAL_ID appears in FINAL_ACTION_LOG with exactly one final action.
- No invalid OPERATION_TYPE values exist.
- No legacy roots appear in proposals, commits, audits, or traces.
- BODY endocrine transitions obey step limits and invariants.
- SKILLS permanent changes only occur with MECHANISM_TAG SKILL_STATE_CHANGE and complete override proof.
- MEMORY creations obey per-cycle limit and include canonical SYSTEM_LINKAGE.
- TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS payloads are schema-valid and caps are enforced.
- DPF active prediction count does not exceed MAX_ACTIVE_PREDICTIONS.
- No orphaned commits exist.
- EPISTEMIC_TAG valid and consistent with linkage and JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY mapping.
- COMMITTED_FACT proposals carry resolvable EVIDENCE_REF.
- Override-gate proposals carry complete structural override proof.
- No CASCADE_TRACE entry shows a character-cognition COT reading a GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL module.
- Exactly one TRACE_ENTRY per priority slot from PRIORITY_01 through PRIORITY_17 exists before post-verification pass.
- FIRES_WHEN discipline is respected for non-exempt COTs.

POST_VERIFICATION_VALIDATION:
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS validates verifier-originated governance-runtime proposals.
- It emits its own audit closure after COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION.
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION does not verify PRIORITY_18 because PRIORITY_18 has not yet executed.

=== SECTION: SCHEMATIC_PROPOSAL_DIRECTIVE_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COT and DYNAMICS PROPOSE ... blocks are schematic templates unless stated otherwise.

SCHEMATIC_DIRECTIVE_RULE:
- PROPOSE ... blocks appearing inside COT templates and DYNAMICS modules are schematic directives.
- Schematic directives illustrate the operation type, target path, and value form required for a class of proposals.
- Schematic directives omit globally required fields such as PROPOSAL_ID, SOURCE_COT, and TARGET_MODULE because those fields are implied by the execution context and must be supplied at runtime.

RUNTIME_EXPANSION_REQUIREMENT:
- Before a proposal enters [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY], it must be expanded to the complete PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA defined in this registry.
- No proposal may enter the scratchpad missing any required global field.
- Required global fields that schematic directives omit must be injected at proposal-construction time:
  - PROPOSAL_ID: unique PUP_<unique> token for this cycle
  - SOURCE_COT: canonical COT name of the constructing COT
  - TARGET_MODULE: canonical module root matching TARGET_FIELD prefix
- Conditionally required fields (EVIDENCE_REF, INVOKED_RULE_NAME, NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE, SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE) must also be injected when their conditions are met.

COMPLETE_EXAMPLE_EXCEPTION:
- A proposal block that explicitly enumerates all PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA fields including PROPOSAL_ID, SOURCE_COT, and TARGET_MODULE is a complete packet form and does not require further expansion.
