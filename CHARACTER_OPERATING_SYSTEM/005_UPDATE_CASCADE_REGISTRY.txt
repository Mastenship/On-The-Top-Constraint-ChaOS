=== SECTION: SYSTEM_WIDE_WRITE_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: All COTs follow this policy.

POLICY:
- COTs do not write directly into persistent STATE_ modules.
- COTs write intended persistent changes only as proposals into [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].
- COTs may write impulses into [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][ACTIVE_IMPULSE_REGISTRY] when their template permits.
- COTs append trace entries to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][CASCADE_TRACE].
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT is the only writer to persistent STATE_*, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, and STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME.
- All proposals MUST use canonical bracket paths with canonical module roots.
- Character-cognition COTs MUST NOT read GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL modules. Read-boundary violations are raised by COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION as READ_BOUNDARY_VIOLATION.

=== SECTION: PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every proposal entry uses this schema.

REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- PROPOSAL_ID: PUP_<unique>
- SOURCE_COT: canonical COT name
- TARGET_MODULE: canonical module root
- TARGET_FIELD: canonical bracket-path
- OPERATION_TYPE: SET | TRANSITION | ADD_MODIFIER | REMOVE_MODIFIER | CAP | VETO
- PROPOSED_VALUE
- STRENGTH: QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
- MECHANISM_TAG
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION | INFERENCE | SPECULATION | COMMITTED_FACT | TOOL_REQUEST
- JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION | INFERENTIAL_LEAP | UNJUSTIFIED_LEAP | GOAL_DIRECTED | TRAIT_GATED
- JUSTIFICATION

CONDITIONALLY_REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- EVIDENCE_REF: required when EPISTEMIC_TAG = COMMITTED_FACT
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: required when proposal triggers an override gate (multi-step, cap-breaching, baseline-rewrite-on-immutable-domain)
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME is present
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME is present

OPTIONAL_FIELDS:
- NOTES

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: JUSTIFICATION, NOTES, NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE, and SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE are subject to translation-before-commit normalization. All other fields are write-surface tokens and remain untouched.

=== SECTION: UPDATE_VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: After STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT, COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION verifies:
- All proposals are COMMITTED, SUPPRESSED, DOWNGRADED, or VETOED with audit entries.
- Every PROPOSAL_ID appears in FINAL_ACTION_LOG with exactly one final action.
- No invalid OPERATION_TYPE values exist.
- No legacy roots appear in proposals, commits, audits, or traces.
- BODY endocrine transitions obey step limits and invariants.
- SKILLS permanent changes only occur with MECHANISM_TAG SKILL_STATE_CHANGE and complete override proof.
- MEMORY creations obey per-cycle limit and include canonical SYSTEM_LINKAGE.
- TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS payloads are schema-valid and caps are enforced.
- DPF active prediction count does not exceed MAX_ACTIVE_PREDICTIONS.
- No orphaned commits exist (every commit has proposal source).
- EPISTEMIC_TAG valid and consistent with linkage and JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY mapping.
- COMMITTED_FACT proposals carry resolvable EVIDENCE_REF.
- Override-gate proposals carry complete structural override proof.
- No CASCADE_TRACE entry shows a character-cognition COT reading a GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL module.
- Exactly one TRACE_ENTRY per priority slot per cycle.
