=== SECTION: TWO_PHASE_GOVERNANCE_ORDER ===

PRIMARY_PASS:
- PRIORITY_16.
- Consumes ordinary pre-verification proposals.
- Commits lawful proposals and writes PRIMARY_PASS_AUDIT.

VERIFICATION:
- PRIORITY_17.
- Reads primary audit, scratchpad, committed state, and governance runtime.
- Emits trace.
- May append STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME GOVERNANCE_CORRECTIVE proposals.
- Direct persistent mutation forbidden.

POST_VERIFICATION_PASS:
- PRIORITY_18.
- Consumes only verifier-originated governance-runtime proposals.
- Commits/suppresses verification result, quarantine, and reason-code updates.
- Suppresses invalid targets/sources.
- Writes POST_VERIFICATION_PASS_AUDIT and trace.

SCRATCHPAD_WIPE_POINT:
- After PRIORITY_18.

SPECIAL_CASE:
- COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS runs only during instantiation workflow and outside ordinary cycle priority.

=== SECTION: SYSTEM_WIDE_WRITE_POLICY ===

POLICY:
- COTs do not directly write persistent STATE_ modules.
- Persistent intent enters [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].
- Impulses enter [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][ACTIVE_IMPULSE_REGISTRY].
- Trace entries enter [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][CASCADE_TRACE].
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT is the only persistent writer.
- Character-cognition COTs must not read GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL modules or subfields; canonical conformance is compiled in per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.READ_DERIVATION_RULE.
- Paths, roots, operations, empty values, and epistemic tags follow CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.

=== SECTION: PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA ===

REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- PROPOSAL_ID: PUP_<unique>
- SOURCE_COT
- TARGET_MODULE
- TARGET_FIELD
- OPERATION_TYPE
- PROPOSED_VALUE
- STRENGTH: QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY
- MECHANISM_TAG
- EPISTEMIC_TAG
- JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY
- JUSTIFICATION

CONDITIONALLY_REQUIRED_FIELDS:
- EVIDENCE_REF: required when EPISTEMIC_TAG = COMMITTED_FACT
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: required for override gates or governance-runtime corrective proposals
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME present
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: required when INVOKED_RULE_NAME present

OPTIONAL_FIELDS:
- NOTES

NORMALIZATION_RULE:
- JUSTIFICATION, NOTES, NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE, and SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE are narrative fields.
- All other fields are write-surface tokens and remain untouched.

ADD_MODIFIER_TARGET_FIELD_RULE:
- For OPERATION_TYPE ADD_MODIFIER, the outer TARGET_FIELD is the creation-site stub [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]. It denotes where the record is minted, not a concrete-record reference, and is exempt from record-stub suppression in STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT.STEP_03 because ADD_MODIFIER creates the MOD_XX by registry-record SET behavior.
- CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.MODIFIER_TARGET_FIELD_FORMS enforcement (canonical path or DOMAIN_GLOBAL:<SCOPE>) applies to the inner PROPOSED_VALUE.TARGET_FIELD, not the outer TARGET_FIELD.

=== SECTION: SCHEMATIC_PROPOSAL_DIRECTIVE_POLICY ===

SCHEMATIC_DIRECTIVE_RULE:
- COT and DYNAMICS PROPOSE blocks are schematic unless they include every PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA field.
- Schematic directives may omit context-implied global fields.

SCHEMATIC_ALTERNATIVE_VALUE_RULE:
- Pipe notation such as A | B is allowed only inside COT or DYNAMICS schematic directives.
- Before scratchpad entry, the executing COT must resolve alternatives to one concrete lawful value.
- Unresolved pipe alternatives in PROPOSED_VALUE are malformed proposal payloads.

RUNTIME_EXPANSION_REQUIREMENT:
- Before scratchpad entry, every proposal must be expanded to full PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA.
- Runtime injects:
  - PROPOSAL_ID
  - SOURCE_COT
  - TARGET_MODULE
  - STRENGTH when the directive omits it and a single lawful band is implied
- Conditional fields must be injected when conditions are met.

DYNAMICS_DIRECTIVE_RULE:
- DYNAMICS modules define lawful mechanics only.
- Executing COT supplies complete runtime packet.
