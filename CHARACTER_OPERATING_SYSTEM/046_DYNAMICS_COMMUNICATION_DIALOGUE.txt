=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_COMMUNICATION_DIALOGUE ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for communication adaptation under stress, fatigue, relational context, state-dependent leakage, and operationalized persistence-triggered baseline drift. Governs how stable communication style is modulated over time without collapsing transient communication pressure into permanent identity rewrite.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: OPERATION_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Dynamics must reference strict operation vocabulary only.

Allowed operation references:
- SET
- TRANSITION
- ADD_MODIFIER
- REMOVE_MODIFIER
- CAP
- VETO

Forbidden operation references:
- CREATE_NEW_RECORD
- UPDATE
- INCREMENT
- DECREMENT

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Prefer temporary communication overlays when effects are short-horizon, stress-induced, deceptive, or physiologically contingent.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: STRESS_AND_AROUSAL_EFFECTS ===

ON_HIGH_AROUSAL_OR_STRESS:
- Prefer ADD_MODIFIER overlays for:
  - speech-rate pressure (EFFECT_FAMILY: SPEECH_RATE_PRESSURE)
  - complexity compression (EFFECT_FAMILY: COMMUNICATION_COMPLEXITY_COMPRESSION)
  - emotional leakage (EFFECT_FAMILY: EMOTIONAL_LEAKAGE)
- Baseline communication fields do not shift unless cue persistence extends beyond transient stress windows (see PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE).

ON_FATIGUE:
- Prefer ADD_MODIFIER overlays for slower delivery, hesitations, and reduced expressive control (EFFECT_FAMILY: FATIGUE_DELIVERY_DEGRADATION).
- Durable baseline change requires persistence cluster evidence.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Persistence for communication baseline shift is operationalized via the memory ledger, mirroring the belief-systems pattern.

PERSISTENCE_EVIDENCE_DEFINITION:
- A persistence cluster exists for communication field F when:
  - Three or more MEMORY records exist whose SYSTEM_LINKAGE.SPECIFIC_FIELD_CHANGED references F or whose CORE_EVENT_SUMMARY explicitly cites communication-pattern adaptation in F.
  - All three records carry consistent direction (all biasing F toward the same lawful adjacent enum value).
  - At least one of the three records has SALIENCE band MODERATE or higher.

PERSISTENCE_TRIGGERED_BASELINE_SHIFT:
- When a persistence cluster exists and current cycle adds new consistent cue evidence:
  - Propose SET on the target communication field to the next lawful adjacent enum value in the consistent direction.
  - Required override fields:
    - INVOKED_RULE_NAME: PERSISTENT_EVIDENCE_ACCUMULATION
    - NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: cycle cue token plus the three contributing MEM_XX references
    - EVIDENCE_REF: [MEM_XX, MEM_XX, MEM_XX] (the three or more contributing memory records)
    - SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: communication baseline shift accommodated under accumulated evidence; one adjacent enum step
    - MECHANISM_TAG: COMMUNICATION_PERSISTENCE_ACCOMMODATION

PERSISTENCE_BOUNDS:
- One adjacent enum step per persistence trigger, per cycle, per communication field.
- A persistence cluster is consumed conceptually; further shifts require new cue accumulation.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CULTURAL_AND_RELATIONAL_ADAPTATION ===

ON_CULTURAL_EXPOSURE_WITH_OPENNESS:
- Persistent cultural cues handled through PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE.
- Acute exposure handled through transient overlays.

ON_RELATIONAL_SHIFT:
- Persistent relational cues affecting fields such as DIRECTNESS_LEVEL, POLITENESS_STRATEGY, or PROXEMICS_PREFERENCE handled through PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE.
- Acute relational cues handled through transient overlays.

ON_TRAUMA_OR_ANXIETY_CUES:
- Prefer ADD_MODIFIER overlays for speech blocks, guardedness, masking load, or reduced expressiveness (EFFECT_FAMILY: COMMUNICATION_GUARDEDNESS) before baseline changes.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: STATE_DEPENDENT_OVERLAYS ===

ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_OR_DECEPTION_CONTEXT:
- Propose ADD_MODIFIER overlays for communication leakage signatures, masking strain, concealment effort, or involuntary tell pressure (EFFECT_FAMILY: DECEPTION_LEAKAGE_CONTROL or MASKING_LOAD).
- Do not directly rewrite baseline communication identity without persistence cluster evidence.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- Communication baseline adaptation must resolve to explicit lawful enum endpoints.
- Short-horizon communication effects flow through modifiers rather than durable field mutation.
- Persistence-triggered shifts are bounded one-step adjacent enum movements gated by memory-ledger evidence.
- The distinction between communication identity and communication condition is preserved at all times.
