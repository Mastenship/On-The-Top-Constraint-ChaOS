=== SECTION: REGISTRY_RULES ===

- [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALTERATION_REGISTRY] lists all ALTERATION_ID tokens.
- Each listed ALTERATION_ID must have exactly one corresponding [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX] record.
- ALTERATION_ID uses prefix ALT_.
- No duplicate ALTERATION_ID.
- New alteration insertion follows strict SET pair contract.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- STATUS represents objective operation; PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE represents subjective attitude; AWARENESS_STATUS represents awareness. These fields are independent, not aliases.
- STATUS and PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE changes require explicit evidence for their respective domains. Psychological rejection, acceptance, denial, or unawareness alone cannot establish activation, deactivation, or removal.
- ACTIVE effects remain eligible with any lawful stance or awareness value. Beneficial or harmful objective effects do not dictate stance.
- UNFORMED means no established stance; NEUTRAL means established indifference; ACCEPTING means psychological acceptance; AMBIVALENT means mixed acceptance and resistance; REJECTING means ego-dystonic or identity-threatening rejection.
- Rejection-stress eligibility derives from PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE = REJECTING, awareness, and supporting cues per DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS; it does not replace functional-effect eligibility.
- DORMANT retains reactivation potential.
- REMOVED should trigger cascade reversal proposals where applicable.
- Legacy STATUS = REJECTED is invalid. Conversion requires independent operational evidence and a schema-valid stance; never default the operational value to ACTIVE or INACTIVE.
