=== SECTION: COT_TEMPORAL_DECAY_RESOLUTION ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Governs slow decay, recovery, and weakening effects caused by time passage without strong reinforcement. Handles memory salience decay, prediction certainty decay, modifier expiry, and unresolved low-activation pressures.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: weakening proposals for persistent and temporary structures; low-activation impulses.
Is Informed By: STATE_MEMORY, STATE_DPF, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS, elapsed-time cues, DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: WRITE_POLICY_OVERRIDE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT proposes; it never commits. STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT is the only persistent writer.
- Proposals go to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY] per COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD.PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY.PROPOSAL_ENTRY_SCHEMA.
- Impulses go to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][ACTIVE_IMPULSE_REGISTRY].
- One trace entry is appended to [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][CASCADE_TRACE]; existing trace content is never read back.
- OPERATION_TYPE is SET, TRANSITION, ADD_MODIFIER, or REMOVE_MODIFIER.
- Every proposal carries one EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION, INFERENCE, SPECULATION, or COMMITTED_FACT, which requires EVIDENCE_REF.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input describes elapsed time, major time skip, scene closure, or sustained lack of reinforcement.
- Active modifier expiry condition is met or deterministically inferable.
- Unresolved prediction has gone unconfirmed across meaningful elapsed interval.
- Memory salience decay or reconsolidation cue is present.
- An eligible [STATE_MEMORY][DEFERRED_NARRATIVE_CUES] entry names this COT as SOURCE_COT, per SCHEME_MEMORY.DEFERRED_NARRATIVE_CUE_QUEUE.COT_RECONSIDERATION.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM only, per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.

=== SECTION: MEMORY_SALIENCE_DECAY ===

ON_EXTENDED_TIME_WITHOUT_REINFORCEMENT:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_MEMORY][MEM_XX][MEMORY_METADATA][SALIENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
  STRENGTH: LOW
  MECHANISM_TAG: TEMPORAL_SALIENCE_DECAY
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: time passage without reinforcement reduces retrieval salience

CONDITIONS:
- One-band bounded.
- Highly linked or recently reactivated memories decay more slowly.
- Governance may suppress when linkage protection or recent retrieval justifies stability.
