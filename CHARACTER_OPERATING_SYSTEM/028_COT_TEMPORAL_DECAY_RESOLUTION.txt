=== SECTION: COT_TEMPORAL_DECAY_RESOLUTION ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT governs slow decay, recovery, and weakening effects caused by time passage without strong reinforcing input. It handles gradual fade processes across memory salience, prediction confidence, temporary modifiers, and unresolved low-activation pressures.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- weakening proposals for persistent and temporary structures
- low-activation impulses where relevant
Is Informed By:
- [STATE_MEMORY]
- [STATE_DPF]
- [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS]
- [STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS]
- narrative elapsed-time cues
- DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS

=== SECTION: WRITE_POLICY_OVERRIDE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT does not directly edit persistent modules.
All durable weakening intent must be proposal-only in [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].

Allowed strict operations only:
- SET
- TRANSITION
- ADD_MODIFIER
- REMOVE_MODIFIER
- CAP
- VETO

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when at least one of the following conditions is present in the current cycle.

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input describes elapsed time, a major time skip, scene closure, or sustained lack of reinforcement.
- An active modifier expiry condition is met or is deterministically inferable from the current temporal state.
- An unresolved prediction has gone unconfirmed across a meaningful elapsed interval without confirming evidence.
- A memory salience decay or reconsolidation cue is present in the current cycle input.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not propose memory salience decay, prediction confidence decay, or modifier expiry.
- Do not generate LET_GO, REVISIT, REINFORCE, or DISENGAGE impulses.

=== SECTION: MEMORY_SALIENCE_DECAY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: SALIENCE is a QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band per SCHEME_MEMORY. Use SET to an adjacent lower band; respect per-cycle one-band limit unless override proof applies.

ON_EXTENDED_TIME_WITHOUT_REINFORCEMENT:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_MEMORY][MEM_XX][MEMORY_METADATA][SALIENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
  STRENGTH: LOW
  MECHANISM_TAG: TEMPORAL_SALIENCE_DECAY
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: time passage without reinforcement reduces retrieval salience

CONDITIONS:
- decay must remain bounded
- highly linked or recently reactivated memories may decay more slowly
- governance may suppress decay when linkage protection or recent retrieval justifies retention stability

=== SECTION: PREDICTION_CONFIDENCE_DECAY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: CERTAINTY on prediction records and CERTAINTY_LEVEL.VALUE on STATE_DPF are QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY bands. Use SET to an adjacent lower band.

ON_PROLONGED_INACTIVITY_OR_NONCONFIRMATION:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_DPF][PRD_XX][CERTAINTY]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
  STRENGTH: LOW
  MECHANISM_TAG: PREDICTION_CONFIDENCE_DECAY
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE

=== SECTION: MODIFIER_EXPIRY_PRESSURE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT may propose modifier expiry, but lifecycle resolution remains governed by DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.

ON_DURATION_LAPSE_OR_EXPIRY_CUE:
- PROPOSE REMOVE_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: target MOD_XX reference
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: MODIFIER_EXPIRY_RESOLUTION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: expiry condition met

CONDITIONS:
- expiry cues must be explicit or deterministically inferable
- expired modifiers must not remain active overlays
- final lifecycle handling must remain consistent with DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS
