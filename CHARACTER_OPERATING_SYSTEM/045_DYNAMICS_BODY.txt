=== SECTION: CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Multiple trait schemes (HEXACO, DARK_TRIAD, MMPI, ASQ) may all bias the same physiological or behavioral response. When trait biases conflict or compound, precedence is deterministic.

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This precedence rule applies system-wide whenever cross-scheme trait modulation occurs. It is anchored here because physiological response is the most-modulated surface, but the rule is referenced by other DYNAMICS modules as needed.

PRECEDENCE_ORDER (highest to lowest):
1) MMPI clinical scale at SIGNIFICANT or CLINICAL band (clinical psychopathology dominates ordinary trait modulation).
2) DARK_TRIAD trait at VERY_HIGH percentile (extreme antagonistic traits override softer dispositions).
3) ASQ ATTACHMENT_SECURITY at VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH percentile (attachment extremes shape relational and stress response strongly).
4) HEXACO trait at VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH percentile (extreme baseline personality).
5) MMPI clinical scale at MODERATE band.
6) DARK_TRIAD trait at HIGH percentile.
7) HEXACO trait at HIGH or LOW percentile.
8) All other lawful trait bands.

CONFLICT_RESOLUTION:
- When two trait biases at the same precedence tier point in opposite directions: the one with stronger band wins (CLINICAL beats SIGNIFICANT; VERY_HIGH beats HIGH).
- When still tied: neither bias is applied; the response is governed only by lower-tier influences and situational cues.

COMPOUNDING_RULE:
- Same-direction biases at different tiers do not stack additively.
- The highest-precedence applicable bias determines magnitude; lower-tier same-direction biases reinforce the cue interpretation but do not increase magnitude beyond one effective band shift per cycle.

VERIFIER_NOTE:
- Proposals citing trait modulation should reference the dominant trait per this precedence order in JUSTIFICATION.
- Proposals stacking trait biases additively are flagged as covert magnitude inflation and may be downgraded.
