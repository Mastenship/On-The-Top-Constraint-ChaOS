=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_BODY ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for physiological transitions, acute stress response, traumatic injury, stress recovery, bounded biomedical drift, cardiovascular perceptual-snapshot derivation, and cross-scheme trait precedence. Defines lawful transition patterns only; never performs direct writes.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION: CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Applies system-wide when HEXACO, DARK_TRIAD, MMPI, or ASQ biases conflict or compound.

PRECEDENCE_ORDER:
1) MMPI SIGNIFICANT or CLINICAL
2) DARK_TRIAD VERY_HIGH
3) ASQ ATTACHMENT_SECURITY VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH
4) HEXACO VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH
5) MMPI MODERATE
6) DARK_TRIAD HIGH
7) HEXACO HIGH or LOW
8) all other lawful trait bands

CONFLICT_RESOLUTION:
- Same tier, opposite direction: stronger band wins.
- CLINICAL beats SIGNIFICANT.
- VERY_HIGH beats HIGH.
- If still tied: neither bias applies; use lower-tier influences and cues.

COMPOUNDING_RULE:
- Same-direction biases do not stack additively.
- Highest-precedence applicable bias determines magnitude.
- Lower-tier same-direction biases may support interpretation, not extra magnitude.
- Additive trait stacking that creates covert magnitude inflation is unlawful.

VERIFIER_NOTE:
- Trait-modulated proposals cite the dominant trait in JUSTIFICATION.
- Additive trait stacking may be downgraded or suppressed.

=== SECTION: CARDIOVASCULAR_SNAPSHOT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: HEART_RATE, BLOOD_PRESSURE, and RESPIRATORY_RATE are PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT categorical fields per SCHEME_BODY. They are perceptual readouts of current arousal and biochemical state, written by SET to any lawful endpoint, and exempt from one-step limits. They carry no durable identity meaning and require no persistence evidence.

ON_ACUTE_AROUSAL_OR_ADRENALINE_RISE:
- When ADRENALINE moves to HEIGHTENED or higher, or an acute-threat cue is present:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][HEART_RATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ELEVATED | TACHYCARDIC
    MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: acute arousal cardiovascular readout
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][RESPIRATORY_RATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ELEVATED | TACHYPNEA
    MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: acute arousal respiratory readout
  - Resolve the pipe alternative to one concrete endpoint by cue intensity before scratchpad entry.

ON_SUSTAINED_CORTISOL_ELEVATION:
- When CORTISOL is SPIKE or CHRONIC:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][BLOOD_PRESSURE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: HYPERTENSION
    MECHANISM_TAG: CORTISOL_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: sustained cortisol cardiovascular readout

ON_RECOVERY_OR_BASELINE_RETURN:
- When ADRENALINE and CORTISOL are at or returning toward baseline and no acute cue remains:
  - PROPOSE SET cardiovascular fields toward NORMAL endpoints:
    - HEART_RATE: NORMAL
    - BLOOD_PRESSURE: NORMAL
    - RESPIRATORY_RATE: NORMAL
  - MECHANISM_TAG: CARDIOVASCULAR_BASELINE_RETURN
  - EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  - JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  - JUSTIFICATION: cardiovascular readout returns to baseline

SNAPSHOT_NOTE:
- Cardiovascular snapshots track biochemical state; they do not independently drift and are not subject to cumulative-ordinal counting because they are not ordinal.
- LOW and the low clinical poles (BRADYCARDIC, HYPOTENSION, BRADYPNEA) are reached by SET only under explicit matching cue.

=== SECTION: ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE is the named override trigger for the fight-or-flight response to sudden physical danger, such as an ambush. The sympathetic-adrenal-medullary surge reaches full strength within seconds, faster than one step per cycle can show. The HPA axis follows over minutes, so CORTISOL rises by the ordinary rule.

GRANT_CONDITION:
- The Dungeon Master's narration establishes sudden physical danger to the character: an attack without warning, a sudden fall or collapse, fire, or any immediate threat to life or limb.
- The danger arrives at once. A threat that builds across cycles moves by the ordinary one step.
- Physical danger only. Emotional and social threats follow their own rules, including DYNAMICS_RELATIONAL.ATTACHMENT_INJURY when someone the character cares about is behind them.
- One invocation per onset. While the danger continues, PEAK holds per BIOCHEMICAL_ARC_PHASES.

COVERED_FIELDS:
- [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][ADRENALINE], from BASELINE only
- [STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS][DRIVE_STATES][SAFETY_DRIVES][PHYSICAL_SAFETY]
- Deterioration direction only.

ENDPOINTS:
- ADRENALINE to PEAK.
- PHYSICAL_SAFETY to THREATENED, or to ENDANGERED when life or limb is at immediate risk.

EXCLUDED:
- CORTISOL, which follows COT_STRESS_CASCADE_RESOLUTION.ON_SUSTAINED_OR_REPEATED_STRESS.
- ADRENALINE arc phases. A DEPLETED body cannot surge again until it recovers.
- EMOTIONAL_SAFETY, coping, beliefs, relationships, and memories.

PROOF_FIELDS:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: the Dungeon Master's statement of sudden physical danger
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: names the target field's ladder in SCHEME_BODY.BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS or SCHEME_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS.DRIVE_STATES and asserts the endpoint lies on it, in the deterioration direction, within ENDPOINTS
- MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_SURGE
- Use SET to the explicit endpoint. Relative transition directives move one step only.

ENFORCEMENT:
- ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE used without its grant condition, on a field outside COVERED_FIELDS, in the recovery direction, or past ENDPOINTS: OVERRIDE_PROOF_INCOMPLETE.
- Movement backed by ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE proof is excluded from the cumulative movement count per STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.STEP_LIMITS_PER_CYCLE.CUMULATIVE_ORDINAL_RULE.

=== SECTION: ENDOCRINE_RECOVERY ===

ON_REST_CYCLE_WITHOUT_ACTIVE_STRESS:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][CORTISOL]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE
  JUSTIFICATION: recovery cue

- If ADRENALINE is not baseline:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][ADRENALINE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE
    JUSTIFICATION: sympathetic cooldown cue

- Both transitions above apply only to on-ladder values. When CORTISOL is CHRONIC or CRASH, or ADRENALINE is POST_PEAK or DEPLETED, recovery uses BIOCHEMICAL_ARC_PHASES.ARC_PHASE_RECOVERY instead.
- When DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.EXTENDED_REST applies, on-ladder ADRENALINE and acute CORTISOL may SET directly to BASELINE under its proof fields.

RECOVERY_GATE:
- Requires explicit rest, safety, cooldown, treatment, or absence of active stressor.
- If active stressor remains, prefer suppression or temporary stabilization overlay.

=== SECTION: BIOCHEMICAL_ARC_PHASES ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: POST_PEAK, DEPLETED, CHRONIC, and CRASH are OFF_LADDER arc phases per SCHEME_BODY.ARC_PHASE_NOTE. Each entry or exit is one SET counted as one step per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.OFF_LADDER_MOVEMENT_RULE.

SYMPATHETIC_ARC:
- POST_PEAK: SET from PEAK when the acute threat ends; the comedown.
- DEPLETED: SET from PEAK or POST_PEAK when activation has been sustained or repeated without recovery.
- While ADRENALINE is PEAK, continued acute threat holds PEAK; do not propose ONE_STEP_UP.

HPA_ARC:
- CHRONIC: SET from ELEVATED or SPIKE when stress has persisted across many cycles without recovery.
- CRASH: SET from CHRONIC only, when chronic load continues to exhaustion.
- While CORTISOL is SPIKE, further sustained stress moves toward CHRONIC by this rule; do not propose ONE_STEP_UP.

ARC_PHASE_RECOVERY:
- Recovery from an arc phase is one SET to the phase's declared EXIT member in SCHEME_BODY, subject to RECOVERY_GATE.
- POST_PEAK and DEPLETED exit to BASELINE. CHRONIC exits to ELEVATED. CRASH exits to BASELINE.
- After exit, ordinary ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE recovery resumes on the ladder.

ARC_PHASE_EFFECTS:
- CHRONIC sustains the elevated-cortisol cardiovascular readout per ON_SUSTAINED_CORTISOL_ELEVATION.
- CRASH, POST_PEAK, and DEPLETED do not trigger elevated cardiovascular readouts; they read as fatigue, shakiness, and low reserve.

=== SECTION: STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS ===

ON_SIGNIFICANT_INJURY_OR_ILLNESS:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][IMMUNE_MARKERS]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: COMPROMISED
  JUSTIFICATION: injury or illness cue
- IMMUNE_MARKERS is ordinal per SCHEME_BODY; a move from ROBUST to COMPROMISED is two steps and requires TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY. From NORMAL, COMPROMISED is one lawful step.

ON_SUBSTANCE_USE_EVENT:
- If NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE = BALANCED:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: STRAINED
    JUSTIFICATION: substance perturbation cue
- If NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE = STRAINED:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: DYSREGULATED
    JUSTIFICATION: substance perturbation cue
- Deterioration is one step per cycle; stronger proposals are downgraded or suppressed by governance.

ON_MALNUTRITION_OR_DEHYDRATION:
- METABOLIC_HEALTH may move one lawful step toward FAILING.
- ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE may move one lawful step toward CRITICAL.
- Deterioration is one step per cycle; stronger proposals are downgraded or suppressed by governance.

IMMUNE_TERMINAL_RECONCILIATION_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: When two or more terminal biochemical states project concurrently with IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST, governance applies IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION: terminal-state transitions commit and IMMUNE_MARKERS is force-corrected one band off ROBUST toward NORMAL. DYNAMICS does not need to emit a separate immune-demotion proposal for this; the bounded correction is governance-side. DYNAMICS may still emit an explicit injury/illness IMMUNE_MARKERS proposal where a direct cue exists.

=== SECTION: TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY is the named override trigger for grievous physical injury. Major trauma suppresses the immune system at once, and maiming, nerve damage, or brain injury can take away in a single moment the capacity a skill depends on. Neither waits for one step per cycle.

GRANT_CONDITION:
- The Dungeon Master's narration establishes grievous physical injury: a severed or crushed limb, a deep penetrating wound, severe burns, nerve or spinal damage, or brain injury.
- An ordinary wound, however painful, moves by STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS one step at a time.

COVERED_FIELDS:
- [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][IMMUNE_MARKERS]
- [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL] and [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][INSTINCT_LEVEL], only for skills whose capacity the injury has lastingly taken away, per DYNAMICS_SKILLS.ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU.ON_CAPABILITY_LOSS
- Deterioration direction only.

ENDPOINTS:
- IMMUNE_MARKERS to COMPROMISED.
- MASTERY_LEVEL to the level the remaining capacity supports.
- INSTINCT_LEVEL to the highest level SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING allows under the new MASTERY_LEVEL, when it would otherwise exceed it. The MASTERY_LEVEL and INSTINCT_LEVEL proposals are one linked unit.

EXCLUDED:
- Impairment that will heal, which stays on the temporary overlay surface and never changes mastery.
- Skills the injury leaves intact.
- ADRENALINE, which follows ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE when the injury comes from sudden danger.
- Other health fields and everything mental.

PROOF_FIELDS:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: the Dungeon Master's statement of the grievous injury
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: names the target field's ladder in SCHEME_BODY.PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH or SCHEME_SKILLS.SKILL_RECORD_SCHEMA and asserts the endpoint lies on it, in the deterioration direction, within ENDPOINTS, with SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING preserved
- MECHANISM_TAG: TRAUMATIC_INJURY on IMMUNE_MARKERS; SKILL_STATE_CHANGE on skill fields per DYNAMICS_SKILLS.GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE
- Use SET to the explicit endpoint. Relative transition directives move one step only.

ENFORCEMENT:
- TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY used without its grant condition, on a field outside COVERED_FIELDS, in the recovery direction, or past ENDPOINTS: OVERRIDE_PROOF_INCOMPLETE.
- Movement backed by TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY proof is excluded from the cumulative movement count per STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.STEP_LIMITS_PER_CYCLE.CUMULATIVE_ORDINAL_RULE.

=== SECTION: HEALTH_RECOVERY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Health heals with time and care, never in a single night. Each field recovers one step per cycle toward its baseline, only while its care condition is established by narration and its cause has stopped. ENDOCRINE_RECOVERY.RECOVERY_GATE applies.

IMMUNE_MARKERS:
- Care: rest with treatment, or rest while the injury or illness is resolving.
- From SUPPRESSED or COMPROMISED: PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE.
- From NORMAL to ROBUST: PROPOSE SET ROBUST only after sustained recovery across many cycles, with good sustenance and no terminal biochemical state.

NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE:
- Care: abstinence from the disrupting substance, with rest or treatment.
- PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE.

METABOLIC_HEALTH:
- Care: adequate food established by narration.
- PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE.

ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE:
- Care: adequate water established by narration.
- PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE.

HEALTH_RECOVERY_BOUNDS:
- One step per field per cycle. DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.EXTENDED_REST does not apply to these fields.
- No recovery while the cause continues: ongoing deprivation, continued substance use, or untreated worsening illness.
- Alteration-caused immune change follows COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS; its recovery uses this section.

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- Physiological drift resolves to canonical TRANSITION directives or explicit lawful SET endpoints.
- Cardiovascular PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT fields are SET from biochemical/arousal state and are limit-exempt.
- SET endpoint proposals on bounded ordinal fields remain step-, band-, and invariant-limited.
- Multi-step deterioration requires ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE, TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY, or DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE proof.
- The immune/terminal cross-field invariant is resolved by bounded force-correction, not by suppressing physiological decline.
- Trait modulation respects CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE.
- Dynamics may describe degradation conceptually; proposals must resolve to concrete lawful values.
