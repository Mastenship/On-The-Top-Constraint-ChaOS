=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when at least one of the following conditions is present in the current cycle.

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input introduces a social interaction with an entity present in STATE_RELATIONAL.
- A relational record's AFFECTIVE_VALENCE or PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE is updated this cycle by another COT, requiring attachment-pattern reinterpretation.
- An attachment-relevant drive (INTIMACY, BELONGING, REJECTION_SENSITIVITY) is at MODERATE band or higher.
- Active modifier targets an attachment EFFECT_FAMILY (ATTACHMENT_SOCIAL_THREAT_AMPLIFICATION).

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not generate seek_intimacy/withdraw/test_loyalty/cling impulses.
- Do not propose relational valence transitions.

=== SECTION: ATTACHMENT_SCHEMA_APPLICATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Apply style-conditioned bias.

ON_SECURE_STYLE:
- Favor trust and balanced intimacy-autonomy.

ON_ANXIOUS_PREOCCUPIED:
- Heighten rejection sensitivity.
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:
    MODIFIER_TYPE: PSYCHOLOGICAL_MODIFIER
    EFFECT_FAMILY: ATTACHMENT_SOCIAL_THREAT_AMPLIFICATION
    EFFECT_EXPRESSION: amplified perception of social threat or rejection
    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
    DURATION_TYPE: SCENE
    EXPIRY_CONDITION: relational reassurance cue or scene transition
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: ATTACHMENT_THREAT_AMPLIFICATION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: TRAIT_GATED
  JUSTIFICATION: anxious-preoccupied attachment bias

ON_AVOIDANT_DISMISSIVE:
- Bias toward distancing and suppression.

ON_FEARFUL_AVOIDANT:
- Bias toward conflicted approach-avoid patterns.
