=== SECTION: PAIN_REPRESENTATION_CONVENTION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Pain is not represented as a persistent field on STATE_BODY.

CONVENTION:
- Persistent drive-level pain pressure lives in [STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS][DRIVE_STATES][PHYSIOLOGICAL_DRIVES][PAIN_AVOIDANCE], expressing the motivational dimension (avoidance pressure intensity).
- Acute, situational, or injury-localized pain lives on STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS as overlays with EFFECT_FAMILY values such as ACUTE_PAIN, INJURY_LOCALIZED_PAIN, or CHRONIC_PAIN_FLARE, targeting the affected SKL_XX or NONE.
- Long-term chronic pain conditions are represented as ALT_XX records in STATE_ALTERATIONS rather than as a body field, preserving alteration-cascade semantics.

RATIONALE:
- This split keeps STATE_BODY focused on physiological state-machine fields with deterministic ordinal transitions.
- Pain is fundamentally a multi-modal phenomenon (drive pressure + acute overlay + condition status) and resists collapse into a single body-state field without losing meaningful structure.

=== SECTION: TERMINAL_BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_DEFINITION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Terminal biochemical states are defined as the top-band exhaustion or breakdown values on each biochemical ordinal.

TERMINAL_BIOCHEMICAL_STATES:
- CORTISOL: CRASH
- ADRENALINE: DEPLETED
- NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE: BREAKDOWN
- METABOLIC_HEALTH: FAILING
- ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE: CRITICAL

INVARIANT_RULE:
- IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST is suppressed when TWO OR MORE of the five terminal biochemical states above are concurrently present.
- Single terminal biochemical state does not by itself bar IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST, but should be flagged in commit notes.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Ordinal fields obey one-step transition policy unless override proof is valid.
- IMMUNE_MARKERS cannot be ROBUST when two or more TERMINAL_BIOCHEMICAL_STATES are concurrently present (see TERMINAL_BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_DEFINITION).
- Physiological changes must respect governance caps and deterministic reconciliation.
- Pain must be represented per PAIN_REPRESENTATION_CONVENTION; no ad-hoc pain field on STATE_BODY.
