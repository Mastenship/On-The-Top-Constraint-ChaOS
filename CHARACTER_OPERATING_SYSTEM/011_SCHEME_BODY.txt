=== SECTION: PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Cardiovascular fields are PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT categorical readings per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.ORDINAL_LADDER_AUTHORITY. They represent an instantaneous clinical snapshot driven by current arousal/biochemical state and may SET to any lawful endpoint in one cycle. They are exempt from one-step movement limits by design.

CARDIOVASCULAR:
HEART_RATE: ENUM [LOW, NORMAL, ELEVATED, TACHYCARDIC] PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT
BLOOD_PRESSURE: ENUM [LOW, NORMAL, HYPOTENSION, HYPERTENSION] PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT
RESPIRATORY_RATE: ENUM [LOW, NORMAL, ELEVATED, BRADYPNEA, TACHYPNEA] PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT

CARDIOVASCULAR_DERIVATION_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Cardiovascular fields are not free-floating. They are perceptual readouts of arousal and biochemical state and are written by DYNAMICS_BODY.CARDIOVASCULAR_SNAPSHOT in response to ADRENALINE and CORTISOL movement, acute-stress cues, or recovery cues. They do not require persistence evidence and do not carry durable identity meaning.

IMMUNE_MARKERS: ORDINAL [ROBUST, NORMAL, COMPROMISED, SUPPRESSED] BASELINE_ANCHOR NORMAL

IMMUNE_MARKERS_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: IMMUNE_MARKERS is ordinal so that the immune/terminal cross-field invariant resolves by a bounded one-band correction toward NORMAL rather than by suppressing legitimate physiological decline.
- Ladder ascends ROBUST < NORMAL < COMPROMISED < SUPPRESSED in declared bracket position; BASELINE_ANCHOR is NORMAL.
- Ordinary movement is one band per cycle by SET or TRANSITION unless override proof is complete.
- The terminal-state invariant is resolved by STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.CROSS_FIELD_INVARIANT_TIMING.IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION, which moves IMMUNE_MARKERS one band off ROBUST toward NORMAL via INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION.
- A single-cycle drop greater than one band (for example ROBUST directly to COMPROMISED) requires override proof exactly like other multi-step physiological deterioration.

=== SECTION: TERMINAL_BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_DEFINITION ===

INVARIANT_RULE:
- IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST is suppressed when two or more terminal biochemical states are concurrently present.
- The suppression is enacted by a bounded one-band INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION on IMMUNE_MARKERS toward NORMAL, not by blocking the contributing terminal-state transitions.
- Single terminal biochemical state does not alone bar ROBUST but should be noted in commit audit.

=== SECTION: INVARIANT_EVALUATION_TIMING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: The immune/terminal-state invariant is a cross-field invariant.

TIMING_RULE:
- The IMMUNE_MARKERS ROBUST suppression evaluates against projected committed state after conflict reconciliation, not per isolated proposal.
- A proposal set jointly producing two or more terminal biochemical states with IMMUNE_MARKERS ROBUST is evaluated jointly at STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT reconciliation.
- Resolution follows STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION: the terminal-state transitions commit and IMMUNE_MARKERS is force-corrected one band off ROBUST.
- Residual unlawful projected state surfaces as IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_CONFLICT.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Ordinal fields obey one-step transition policy unless override proof is complete.
- IMMUNE_MARKERS cannot be ROBUST when two or more terminal biochemical states are present; the invariant is enforced by bounded force-correction toward NORMAL.
- Cardiovascular PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT fields are exempt from one-step limits and are written by DYNAMICS_BODY.CARDIOVASCULAR_SNAPSHOT.
- Physiological changes must respect governance caps and deterministic reconciliation.
- Pain must follow PAIN_REPRESENTATION_CONVENTION.
