=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for alteration activation, deactivation, removal, the ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE override, psychological stance, cascade-class inference, and cascade triggering. Defines lawful transition patterns only; never performs direct writes.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: OPERATION_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Use strict operation vocabulary per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY. Proposal patterns here are schematic directives per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: STATUS, CHARACTER_AWARENESS.PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE, and CHARACTER_AWARENESS.AWARENESS_STATUS are independent categorical fields under SCHEME_ALTERATIONS. Changes use SET to explicit endpoints, not relative movement.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: NEW_ALTERATION_ADMISSION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: When the Dungeon Master establishes a new durable alteration, DYNAMICS defines the gate; COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS is the sole runtime packet producer.

NEW_ALTERATION_GATE:
- Explicit cue establishes a persistent, temporary-but-record-worthy, conditional, or episodic change in physical, psychological, magical, technological, or environmental capability/constraint.
- No existing ALT_XX adequately captures this change.
- It materially affects body, skill capability, perceived identity, vulnerability, behavior, goals, or narrative continuity beyond a passing scene effect.
- Enough evidence exists to populate a complete SCHEME_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_RECORD_SCHEMA, including independently grounded STATUS, AWARENESS_STATUS, and PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE.
- An injury, disease, or other cause of pain that will still affect the character beyond the moment is a TEMPORARY alteration, or PERMANENT when it will not heal. Its ending is adjudicated by the Dungeon Master per OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_GATE.
- A passing hurt or fleeting sensation is carried by narration and the PAIN_AVOIDANCE drive, and a momentary performance shift by a temporary modifier, instead of inventing ALT_XX.

ON_NEW_ALTERATION:
- When gate passes, executing COT emits a linked SET pair:
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALTERATION_REGISTRY] append provisional ALT_XX
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX] full schema-valid alteration record
- STATUS reflects established operation; rejection or denial provides no operational endpoint. If operation cannot be established, do not invent ACTIVE or INACTIVE to complete admission.
- Stance reflects established attitude; use UNFORMED when no attitude has been established. Awareness is seeded independently.
- Cascade packets depend on surviving creation and their specific supporting fields: STATUS for functional effects; stance, awareness, and stress cues for rejection effects.

=== SECTION: ACTIVATION_DEACTIVATION ===

ON_CONSTRAINT_MET:
- If STATUS = DORMANT and activation conditions are satisfied:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ACTIVE
    JUSTIFICATION: activation condition satisfied

ON_CONSTRAINT_UNMET:
- If STATUS = ACTIVE and activation conditions are no longer satisfied:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: DORMANT
    JUSTIFICATION: activation condition no longer met

ON_REMOVAL_EVENT:
- If a cue establishes functional cessation or actual removal, resolve one endpoint:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: INACTIVE | REMOVED
    JUSTIFICATION: functional cessation for INACTIVE, actual removal for REMOVED

OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_GATE:
- Activation, dormancy, functional cessation, removal, and reactivation require objective mechanism or Dungeon Master cue evidence.
- Psychological rejection, denial, acceptance, or attitude change alone cannot satisfy this gate. If resistance actually disables an alteration through a lawful physical or magical mechanism, that event supplies separate operational evidence.

STATUS_ENDPOINTS:
- ACTIVE: produces runtime-relevant effects.
- DORMANT: present but inactive.
- INACTIVE: present in history or identity but non-functional.
- REMOVED: no longer present; may trigger cascade reversal.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE is the named override trigger for an alteration whose onset, activation, or failure strikes at once: a toxin or curse taking hold, an implant rejected, an augmentation failing, a maiming wound. Its effect may exceed one step in that cycle.

GRANT_CONDITION:
- A STATUS change this cycle per ACTIVATION_DEACTIVATION, or a new alteration admitted this cycle per NEW_ALTERATION_ADMISSION, meeting OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_GATE.
- The Dungeon Master's narration establishes that the effect is immediate and severe. A severe description of the alteration alone does not meet this condition.
- Psychological rejection, denial, or stance change never meets it.

COVERED_FIELDS:
- [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][IMMUNE_MARKERS], for an alteration of INJURY_OR_ILLNESS_CLASS
- [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL] and [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][INSTINCT_LEVEL], only where the alteration lastingly takes away the capacity a skill depends on, per DYNAMICS_SKILLS.ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU.ON_CAPABILITY_LOSS
- Deterioration direction only.

ENDPOINTS:
- IMMUNE_MARKERS to COMPROMISED.
- MASTERY_LEVEL to the level the remaining capacity supports.
- INSTINCT_LEVEL to the highest level SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING allows under the new MASTERY_LEVEL, when it would otherwise exceed it. The MASTERY_LEVEL and INSTINCT_LEVEL proposals are one linked unit.

EXCLUDED:
- Enhancement and degradation effects, which stay on the temporary overlay surface per COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS.
- Reversal. Cessation or removal is not restoration; recovery follows DYNAMICS_BODY.HEALTH_RECOVERY.
- The immune shock of a wound in the cycle it is narrated, which follows DYNAMICS_BODY.STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS and DYNAMICS_BODY.TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY.
- STATUS, PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE, and AWARENESS_STATUS, which are categorical and need no override.

PROOF_FIELDS:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: the objective mechanism or Dungeon Master statement of the alteration's immediate effect
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: names the target field's ladder in SCHEME_BODY.PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH or SCHEME_SKILLS.SKILL_RECORD_SCHEMA and asserts the endpoint lies on it, in the deterioration direction, within ENDPOINTS, with SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING preserved
- MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_PHYSIOLOGICAL_PROPAGATION on IMMUNE_MARKERS; SKILL_STATE_CHANGE on skill fields per DYNAMICS_SKILLS.GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE
- Use SET to the explicit endpoint. Relative transition directives move one step only.

ENFORCEMENT:
- ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE used without its grant condition, on a field outside COVERED_FIELDS, in the recovery direction, or past ENDPOINTS: OVERRIDE_PROOF_INCOMPLETE.

=== SECTION: PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE ===

ON_STANCE_CUE:
- Resolve one cue-supported SCHEME_ALTERATIONS.PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE endpoint.
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][CHARACTER_AWARENESS][PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: one lawful stance endpoint
  JUSTIFICATION: explicit attitude, adaptation, ambivalence, indifference, or loss-of-formed-stance cue
- Acceptance or adaptation may resolve rejection; operational status remains independently governed.

ON_AWARENESS_CUE:
- PROPOSE SET to [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][CHARACTER_AWARENESS][AWARENESS_STATUS] only when explicit knowledge, discovery, denial, or loss-of-awareness cues support one lawful endpoint. Do not infer awareness from operation or stance alone.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: PSYCHOLOGICAL_REJECTION ===

ON_TRAUMATIC_REJECTION:
- If linked memory salience and awareness support rejection:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][CHARACTER_AWARENESS][PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: REJECTING
    JUSTIFICATION: trauma-linked rejection cue
- Optionally trigger downstream stress overlay proposals; neither rejection nor its stress overlay changes STATUS.

REJECTION_GATE:
- REJECTING requires explicit cue evidence of ego-dystonic, traumatic, alien, shameful, violating, or identity-threatening experience and awareness of the alteration or its perceived effects.
- Mere inconvenience or dislike is insufficient.
- Rejection stress additionally requires supporting stress cues and AWARENESS_STATUS other than UNAWARE. DENIED may coexist with experienced identity threat; neither denotes operational inactivity.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CASCADE_TRIGGER ===

ON_STATUS_STANCE_OR_AWARENESS_CHANGE:
- Trigger COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS.
- Dynamics does not directly write propagated effects.

ON_STATUS_NO_LONGER_ACTIVE:
- Evaluate removal of functional overlays attributable to that source alteration. Physiological reversal needs independent recovery evidence and existing BODY bounds; cessation is not automatic restoration.
- Psychological stress remains independently eligible while its stance, awareness, and stress cues support it, including after removal.

ON_REJECTION_RESOLVED:
- Evaluate removal of attributable rejection-stress overlays when stance, awareness, or supporting stress cues no longer satisfy REJECTION_GATE. Preserve lawful ACTIVE functional effects.

REVERSAL_BOUNDARY:
- Reversal proposals remain bounded and must not erase memories, alteration records, or audit history.

=== SECTION: CASCADE_EFFECT_CLASS_INFERENCE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS classifies relevant alterations at runtime from their independent operational and psychological fields. No persistent classification field exists.

INJURY_OR_ILLNESS_CLASS:
- Functional eligibility requires STATUS = ACTIVE, irrespective of stance or awareness.
- Infer from PHYSIOLOGICAL_CHANGES or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing damage, disease, infection, organ impairment, wound, or compromised biological function.
- Drives STATE_BODY proposals.
- Focus: biological integrity or health state.

ENHANCEMENT_CLASS:
- Functional eligibility requires STATUS = ACTIVE, irrespective of stance or awareness.
- Infer from NEW_ABILITIES or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing added capability, performance augmentation, expanded sense, or new motor function.
- Drives STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS enhancement overlays.
- Persistent capability source remains the alteration record.

DEBILITATING_CLASS:
- Functional eligibility requires STATUS = ACTIVE, irrespective of stance or awareness.
- Infer from VULNERABILITIES_CONSTRAINTS or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing functional reduction, capacity loss, action restriction, or performance penalty.
- Drives STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS degradation overlays.
- Focus: functional capability loss.

REJECTION_PSYCHOLOGICAL_CLASS:
- Infer when CHARACTER_AWARENESS.PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE = REJECTING, AWARENESS_STATUS is not UNAWARE, and REJECTION_GATE stress cues apply, regardless of STATUS.
- Drives ALTERATION_REJECTION_STRESS overlays.

MULTI_CLASS_RESOLUTION:
- Classes are independent; an alteration may satisfy multiple classes.
- Each applicable class may generate bounded proposals subject to governance caps.
- No class takes precedence over another.
- An ACTIVE, REJECTING alteration may produce both functional effects and rejection stress; an ACTIVE, UNAWARE alteration may produce functional effects without rejection stress.

CLASSIFICATION_TRACE:
- Runtime classification is recorded in COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS TRACE_ENTRY NOTES.
- Verifier may inspect trace-classification consistency against the alteration record.
