=== SECTION: NEW_ALTERATION_ADMISSION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: When the Dungeon Master establishes a new durable alteration, DYNAMICS defines the gate; COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS is the sole runtime packet producer.

NEW_ALTERATION_GATE:
- Explicit cue establishes a persistent, temporary-but-record-worthy, conditional, or episodic change in physical, psychological, magical, technological, or environmental capability/constraint.
- No existing ALT_XX adequately captures this change.
- It materially affects body, skill capability, perceived identity, vulnerability, behavior, goals, or narrative continuity beyond a passing scene effect.
- Enough evidence exists to populate a complete SCHEME_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_RECORD_SCHEMA, including STATUS and CHARACTER_AWARENESS.
- A purely fleeting sensation, ordinary injury without alteration significance, or momentary performance shift is handled by body state or temporary modifier instead of inventing ALT_XX.

ON_NEW_ALTERATION:
- When gate passes, executing COT emits a linked SET pair:
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALTERATION_REGISTRY] append provisional ALT_XX
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX] full schema-valid alteration record
- Status reflects what is established by the cue; do not assume ACTIVE when dormant, rejected, or unknown evidence does not support it.
- Subsequent physiological/psychological/skill cascade packets must depend on the surviving new alteration record and lawful initial STATUS.

=== SECTION: ACTIVATION_DEACTIVATION ===

ON_CONSTRAINT_MET:
- If STATUS = DORMANT and activation conditions are satisfied:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ACTIVE
    JUSTIFICATION: activation condition satisfied

ON_CONSTRAINT_UNMET:
- If STATUS = ACTIVE and activation conditions are no longer satisfied:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: DORMANT
    JUSTIFICATION: activation condition no longer met

ON_REMOVAL_EVENT:
- If removal cue establishes that the alteration is no longer functionally present:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: INACTIVE | REMOVED
    JUSTIFICATION: removal cue

STATUS_ENDPOINTS:
- ACTIVE: produces runtime-relevant effects.
- DORMANT: present but inactive.
- REJECTED: ego-dystonic or identity-threatening; may drive stress overlays.
- INACTIVE: present in history or identity but non-functional.
- REMOVED: no longer present; may trigger cascade reversal.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: PSYCHOLOGICAL_REJECTION ===

ON_TRAUMATIC_REJECTION:
- If linked memory salience and awareness support rejection:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: REJECTED
    JUSTIFICATION: trauma-linked rejection cue
- Optionally trigger downstream stress overlay proposals.

REJECTION_GATE:
- REJECTED requires explicit cue evidence of ego-dystonic, traumatic, alien, shameful, violating, or identity-threatening experience.
- Mere inconvenience or dislike is insufficient.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CASCADE_TRIGGER ===

ON_STATUS_CHANGE:
- Trigger COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS.
- Dynamics does not directly write propagated effects.

ON_REMOVED_STATUS:
- Trigger downstream reversal evaluation for prior active overlays or state effects attributable to the removed alteration.
- Reversal proposals remain bounded and must not erase memories, records, or audit history.

=== SECTION: CASCADE_EFFECT_CLASS_INFERENCE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS classifies active or status-changed alterations at runtime from alteration record fields. No persistent classification field exists.

INJURY_OR_ILLNESS_CLASS:
- Infer from PHYSIOLOGICAL_CHANGES or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing damage, disease, infection, organ impairment, wound, or compromised biological function.
- Drives STATE_BODY proposals.
- Focus: biological integrity or health state.

ENHANCEMENT_CLASS:
- Infer from NEW_ABILITIES or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing added capability, performance augmentation, expanded sense, or new motor function.
- Drives STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS enhancement overlays.
- Persistent capability source remains the alteration record.

DEBILITATING_CLASS:
- Infer from VULNERABILITIES_CONSTRAINTS or OBJECTIVE_SYSTEM_EFFECTS describing functional reduction, capacity loss, action restriction, or performance penalty.
- Drives STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS degradation overlays.
- Focus: functional capability loss.

REJECTED_PSYCHOLOGICAL_CLASS:
- Infer when STATUS = REJECTED and CHARACTER_AWARENESS.AWARENESS_STATUS is not UNAWARE.
- Drives ALTERATION_REJECTION_STRESS overlays.

MULTI_CLASS_RESOLUTION:
- Classes are independent; an alteration may satisfy multiple classes.
- Each applicable class may generate bounded proposals subject to governance caps.
- No class takes precedence over another.

CLASSIFICATION_TRACE:
- Runtime classification is recorded in COT_ALTERATION_CASCADE_EFFECTS TRACE_ENTRY NOTES.
- Verifier may inspect trace-classification consistency against the alteration record.
