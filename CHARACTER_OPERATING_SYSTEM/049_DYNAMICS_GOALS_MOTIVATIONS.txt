=== SECTION: FATIGUE_REPRESENTATION_COHERENCE_NOTE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: The SLEEP drive and STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL are distinct surfaces and are intentionally not coupled.

COHERENCE_RULE:
- SLEEP here is a motivational drive: the pressure to rest, owned by DYNAMICS_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS.
- CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL in SCHEME_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS is physiological fatigue load, owned by DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.
- Both share the ascending rung vocabulary RESTED, MILD_FATIGUE, MODERATE_FATIGUE, SEVERE_FATIGUE, EXHAUSTED for readability.
- The two may lawfully diverge; no cross-field invariant binds them. Drive pressure and physiological load respond to different cues on different cadences.

=== SECTION: REGISTRY_RULES ===

- [STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS][GOAL_REGISTRY] lists all GOAL_ID tokens.
- Each listed GOAL_ID must have exactly one corresponding [STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS][GOAL_XX] record.
- GOAL_ID uses prefix GOAL_.
- No duplicate GOAL_ID.
- New goal creation follows strict SET pair contract.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Drive fields are ordinal and obey one-step transitions unless override proof is valid.
- URGENCY band must align with DRIVE_SERVED intensity.
- CONFIDENCE band must be plausible against resource, body, and skill context.
- PARENT_GOAL_ID must reference existing GOAL_ID when non-empty.
- STATUS transitions require explicit cue or event support.
- HIGH or EXTREME urgency goals should trigger DPF prediction linkage behavior per DYNAMICS_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS.
