=== SECTION: COT_STRESS_CASCADE_RESOLUTION ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT defines the cascade from stressor detection through physiological response to behavioral impulses. It translates stress cues into proposed physiological state changes (proposal-only), temporary overlays in STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS (proposal-only), and stress-driven impulses.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- STATE_BODY (biochemical proposals)
- STATE_COPE (selection pressure proposals)
- STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS (overlay proposals)
- DECISION synthesis (impulses)
Is Informed By:
- STATE_MEMORY
- SCHEME personality modules
- STATE_RELATIONAL
- narrative input
- SCHEME_BODY and DYNAMICS_BODY

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when at least one of the following conditions is present in the current cycle.

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input describes a stressor matching one of STRESSOR_CLASSIFICATION categories (ACUTE_PHYSICAL_THREAT, ACUTE_SOCIAL_THREAT, CHRONIC_OVERLOAD, LOSS_OR_GRIEF, UNCERTAINTY_OR_AMBIGUITY, EXISTENTIAL).
- Current STATE_BODY biochemical markers are above baseline (CORTISOL not BASELINE, ADRENALINE not BASELINE, NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE not BALANCED).
- Active modifier targets a stress-related EFFECT_FAMILY (HYPERVIGILANCE, CHRONIC_LOAD_NUMBING).
- A high-elevation MMPI scale (SIGNIFICANT or CLINICAL) interacts with a moderate cue per CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not generate fight/flight/freeze/fawn/appease/withdraw impulses.
- Do not propose biochemical transitions.

=== SECTION: PHYSIOLOGICAL_PROPAGATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Propose transitions respecting SCHEME_BODY ordinals and limits.

ON_ACUTE_STRESSOR:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][ADRENALINE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  STRENGTH: HIGH
  MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_ACTIVATION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: sympathetic activation cue

ON_SUSTAINED_OR_REPEATED_STRESS:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][CORTISOL]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  STRENGTH: HIGH
  MECHANISM_TAG: HPA_AXIS_ACTIVATION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: HPA-axis sustained stress cue

ON_CHRONIC_PATTERN:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION toward chronic states only if step limits or override rules permit.

=== SECTION: BEHAVIORAL_IMPULSE_GENERATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Emit one or more impulses.

IMPULSE_TYPE: FIGHT | FLIGHT | FREEZE | FAWN | APPEASE | WITHDRAW
IMPULSE_STRENGTH: QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
TARGET_ID: ENT_XX or NONE

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: FAWN is the canonical fourth-F submission/placation response (replaces prior FAP token). APPEASE remains a distinct related response with goal-directed conciliation rather than reflexive submission.
