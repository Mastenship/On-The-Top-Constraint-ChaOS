ON_EXTENDED_DISUSE:

- The SKILL_RUST overlay for that SKL_XX has met its expiry condition; removal follows DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.EXPIRY_TRANSITIONS.

ON_LASTING_CAPABILITY_LOSS:
- Apply ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU.ON_CAPABILITY_LOSS, including the linked INSTINCT_LEVEL step when the coupling requires it.
- When DYNAMICS_BODY.TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY or DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE applies, SET MASTERY_LEVEL and INSTINCT_LEVEL to its endpoints with its proof fields instead of one step.

  - propose bounded SKILL_STATE_CHANGE using DYNAMICS_SKILLS mechanics.
- If gate fails:
  - route effect through performance overlay, impulse, memory cue, or no persistent proposal.

REQUIREMENTS:
- MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE.
- Preserve SCHEME_SKILLS invariants, including INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING.
- Skill gains move one step at a time. Loss beyond one step requires DYNAMICS_BODY.TRAUMA_INDUCED_ATROPHY or DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE proof.
- Momentary success, failure, stress, fatigue, flow, or environmental pressure is insufficient.
