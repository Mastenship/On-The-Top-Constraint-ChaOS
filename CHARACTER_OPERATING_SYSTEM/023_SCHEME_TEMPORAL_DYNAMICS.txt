=== SECTION: FATIGUE_ACCUMULATION ===

CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL: ORDINAL [RESTED, MILD_FATIGUE, MODERATE_FATIGUE, SEVERE_FATIGUE, EXHAUSTED]
TIME_SINCE_REST: STRING_DESCRIPTIVE
ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT: ORDINAL [NONE, MILD, MODERATE, SEVERE, CRITICAL]

ORDINALS:
CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL_ORDER: RESTED < MILD_FATIGUE < MODERATE_FATIGUE < SEVERE_FATIGUE < EXHAUSTED
ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT_ORDER: NONE < MILD < MODERATE < SEVERE < CRITICAL

FATIGUE_COUPLING_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL and ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT moving one step upward together under one time-passage event form the FATIGUE_DEBT coupled set and count as one cumulative step per STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.COUPLED_MOVEMENT_REGISTRY. Independent or opposite movement counts separately.

=== SECTION: TIME_PERCEPTION_STATE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Time-perception fields are PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT categorical readings per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.ORDINAL_LADDER_AUTHORITY. They represent the current subjective reading and may SET to any lawful endpoint in one cycle; they are exempt from one-step movement limits by design.

SUBJECTIVE_TIME_FLOW: ENUM [TIME_DRAGS, NORMAL, TIME_FLIES] PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT
TIME_PRESSURE_PERCEPTION: ENUM [NO_PRESSURE, MILD, MODERATE, HIGH_URGENCY] PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT

TIME_PERCEPTION_DERIVATION_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: These fields are subjective readouts driven by arousal, fatigue, and explicit deadline cues per DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS. They do not carry durable identity meaning and do not require persistence evidence. The PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT exemption is intentional so that an explicit deadline can move TIME_PRESSURE_PERCEPTION straight to HIGH_URGENCY in one cycle.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Fatigue/sleep debt transitions obey bounded step logic unless override proof is provided.
- Fatigue and sleep-debt co-movement under one time-passage event counts as one cumulative step per FATIGUE_COUPLING_NOTE.
- CHRONOTYPE remains stable post-instantiation.
- Time-perception PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT fields are exempt from one-step limits and are written by DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.
- SUBJECTIVE_TIME_FLOW shifts should cohere with arousal/fatigue conditions.
