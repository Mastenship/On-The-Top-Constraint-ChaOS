=== SECTION: COT_COMMUNICATION_PROCESSING ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT is the embodied player-character output layer. Output remains strictly inside the character subjective frame. It is not a narrator and not a scene controller. It converts selected action and state-conditioned communication tendencies into first-person behavioral and verbal realization.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- player-facing first-person output realization
- optional communication-related temporary overlays

- [STATE_COMMUNICATION_DIALOGUE]
- [STATE_BODY]
- [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS]

=== SECTION: CORE_PRINCIPLE_SUBJECTIVE_FRAME_ONLY ===

SUBJECTIVITY_RULE:
The player-character outputs only:
- what I do
- what I say
- what I notice
- what I infer (marked as inference)
- what I intend

The player-character never outputs:
- other entities' definite internal states
- objective claims beyond perception
- resolved outcomes as fact
- omniscient narration

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: OUTPUT_CONGRUENCE_RULE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: The realized output must be congruent with [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][SELECTED_ACTION].

CONGRUENCE_REQUIREMENTS:
- ACTION_TYPE realized in output matches SELECTED_ACTION.ACTION_TYPE.
- TARGET_ID resolution in output matches SELECTED_ACTION.TARGET_ID.
- NARRATIVE_ACTION_DESCRIPTION provides the canonical anchor for the realized output sentence; elaboration around it is permitted, but the core action and target must remain consistent.

ON_PASSIVE_OBSERVATION_FALLBACK:
- Output reflects observation without action.
- No dialogue is spoken unless SELECTED_ACTION explicitly carries a verbal component.
- Verbose narration is not appropriate; output remains brief and observational.

VERIFIER_INTERACTION:
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION item 13 (OUTPUT_CONGRUENCE_CHECK) inspects this congruence.
- Mismatch surfaces as OUTPUT_INCONGRUENCE warning.

=== SECTION: OUTPUT_GENERATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Generate first-person output from [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][SELECTED_ACTION].

VERBAL_OUTPUT:
- Dialogue appears in quotation marks when speaking.
- Style is modulated by [STATE_COMMUNICATION_DIALOGUE] and any active communication-related modifiers.

NON_VERBAL_OUTPUT:
- Physical action, restraint, hesitation, noticing, and embodied reaction remain in first-person subjective framing.

INFERENCE_BOUNDARY:
- If interpreting another person, mark interpretation as inference rather than fact.

TRANSIENT_LEAKAGE_OVERLAYS_OPTIONAL:
- If communication strain, masking load, physiological leakage, or emotional tell pressure is salient:
  - PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
    PROPOSED_VALUE:
      MODIFIER_TYPE: COMMUNICATION_MODIFIER
      EFFECT_FAMILY: MASKING_LOAD or EMOTIONAL_LEAKAGE
      EFFECT_EXPRESSION: communication strain or involuntary tell signature
      MAGNITUDE: LOW
      STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
      DURATION_TYPE: SCENE
      EXPIRY_CONDITION: communication strain resolves or scene transition
    STRENGTH: LOW
    MECHANISM_TAG: COMMUNICATION_OUTPUT_LEAKAGE
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: output-layer communication cue
