=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_RELATIONAL ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for affective valence, perceived-other-valence, cognitive complexity, trust, attachment injury, encounter onboarding, and concern resolution. Valence fields are independent and evidence-specific.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION: RELATIONAL_UPDATE_THRESHOLD_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Persistent relational valence movement is conservative. Minor social texture usually produces no proposal or a temporary modifier.

MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE:
Persistent valence movement requires at least one:
- Trust, safety, attachment, autonomy, status, intimacy, loyalty, betrayal, protection, humiliation, abandonment, or dependence is affected.
- Existing CURRENT_CONCERN is confirmed or contradicted.
- Prior pattern repeats with accumulated relational significance.
- Cue links to memory candidate, goal pressure, DPF prediction, or belief relevance.
- Character's willingness to approach, avoid, rely on, protect, confront, or disclose changes.

NON_MOVEMENT_CASES:
- Ordinary politeness.
- Minor pleasantness.
- Background friendliness without consequence.
- Incidental proximity without meaningful interaction.
- Generic hostility adding no new relational information.
- Single ambiguous cue better represented as PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE inference or modifier.

=== SECTION: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE moves on the character's own emotional response.

ON_MEANINGFUL_POSITIVE_INTERACTION:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION: meaningful positive emotional response

ON_MEANINGFUL_NEGATIVE_INTERACTION:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION: meaningful negative emotional response

ON_BETRAYAL_OR_HARM_EVENT:
- Multi-step downward movement requires ATTACHMENT_INJURY proof.

ALLY_PROXIMITY_NOTE:
- Ally proximity alone ordinarily produces temporary comfort/co-regulation overlay.
- Durable valence movement requires meaningful relational action.

=== SECTION: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE moves on observed or inferred entity affect, not the character's own feelings.

EVIDENCE_TYPES:
- Observed warmth, hostility, withdrawal, approach, aid, threat, or avoidance.
- Disclosed entity statements.
- Inferred entity affect from expression, proxemics, or pattern.

ON_POSITIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: positive entity behavior implies warmer perceived-other valence

ON_NEGATIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: negative entity behavior implies colder perceived-other valence

ON_DISCLOSED_ENTITY_STATEMENT:
- Disclosure may establish the statement occurred.
- Entity internal state remains INFERENCE unless substrate grants direct access.
- Use COMMITTED_FACT only for the disclosure event, with EVIDENCE_REF.

ASYMMETRY_NOTE:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE and PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE may lawfully diverge.

=== SECTION: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY moves on accumulated interaction depth, independent of valence.

EVIDENCE_FOR_UPWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Multiple distinct contexts.
- Contradiction of prior simple categorization.
- Direct disclosure of history, motive, or internal state.
- Sustained varied interaction.
- Discovery of more complex motives, constraints, loyalties, fears, or values.

EVIDENCE_FOR_DOWNWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Explicit forced recategorization.
- Evidence prior complexity was mistaken projection.
- Prolonged absence is usually handled through memory decay.

ON_DEPTH_EVIDENCE:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  JUSTIFICATION: accumulated interaction depth

ON_FORCED_RECATEGORIZATION:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: lawful lower band
  JUSTIFICATION: forced recategorization cue
- Lowering requires an explicit cue. More than one band in a cycle requires ATTACHMENT_INJURY proof.

DECOUPLING_RULE:
- Movement on AFFECTIVE_VALENCE, PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE, COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY, and TRUST requires independent justification.

=== SECTION: TRUST_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: TRUST moves on evidence of the entity's reliability, not on feeling or time. It never drifts toward baseline per SCHEME_RELATIONAL.TRUST_NOTE.

EVIDENCE_TYPES:
- Commitments kept or broken.
- Confidences kept or exposed.
- Reliability or abandonment when it cost the entity something.
- Deception discovered or honesty demonstrated.
- Use EPISTEMIC_TAG PERCEPTION for directly witnessed evidence and INFERENCE for reported or inferred evidence.

ON_RELIABILITY_DEMONSTRATED:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][TRUST]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    MECHANISM_TAG: RELIABILITY_EVIDENCE
    JUSTIFICATION: entity proved reliable when it mattered

ON_RELIABILITY_FAILURE:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][TRUST]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    MECHANISM_TAG: RELIABILITY_FAILURE
    JUSTIFICATION: entity failed a reliance the character placed on them

ON_BETRAYAL_EVENT:
- A betrayal is a deliberate violation of trust the character had extended.
- If TRUST is GUARDED, TRUSTING, or IMPLICIT:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][TRUST]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: BETRAYED
    MECHANISM_TAG: TRUST_RUPTURE
    JUSTIFICATION: deliberate violation of extended trust
- If TRUST is DISTRUSTFUL, the act confirms an existing expectation; it is not a rupture. Record it through memory or AFFECTIVE_VALENCE as warranted.
- Entering BETRAYED is one SET per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.OFF_LADDER_MOVEMENT_RULE; no override proof is needed for the rupture itself.
- Betrayal usually also warrants a separate AFFECTIVE_VALENCE proposal and a memory candidate; each needs its own justification under DECOUPLING_RULE.

ON_REPAIR_EVENT:
- Leaving BETRAYED requires a meaningful repair act by the entity, such as restitution, sacrifice, or sustained proof of change. Time alone never repairs it.
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][TRUST]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: DISTRUSTFUL
  MECHANISM_TAG: TRUST_REPAIR
  JUSTIFICATION: meaningful repair act after betrayal
- Further recovery proceeds one step at a time through ON_RELIABILITY_DEMONSTRATED.

ATTACHMENT_BIAS:
- Attachment style may bias how readily trust is extended or withdrawn per COT_ATTACHMENT_BEHAVIORAL_MAPPING. It never moves TRUST without reliability evidence.

=== SECTION: ATTACHMENT_INJURY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: ATTACHMENT_INJURY is the named override trigger for betrayal, abandonment, or harm by someone the character cares about, especially at a moment of need. An attachment injury violates the assumptions the bond rests on, and the character must redefine the relationship. Feeling can fall further than one step in that moment. Love does not always turn to hatred; it may land on coldness or a numb neutrality instead.

GRANT_CONDITION:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE toward the ENT_XX is WARM or DEVOTED.
- The Dungeon Master's narration establishes a deliberate betrayal, an abandonment at a moment of need, or deliberate harm by that entity.
- The character witnesses the act or has strong evidence of it. Rumor or suspicion alone moves by the ordinary one step.

COVERED_FIELDS:
- [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE]
- [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY], when the act forces the character to recategorize who the entity is
- [STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS][DRIVE_STATES][SAFETY_DRIVES][EMOTIONAL_SAFETY]
- Deterioration direction only. Each covered field needs its own justification per DECOUPLING_RULE.

ENDPOINTS:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE to NEUTRAL, COLD, or HOSTILE, as the character's own response supports.
- COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY to the band the recategorization supports, as low as ONE_DIMENSIONAL.
- EMOTIONAL_SAFETY to THREATENED, or to VIOLATED when the bond was central to the character's sense of safety.

EXCLUDED:
- TRUST, which enters BETRAYED by one SET per TRUST_DYNAMICS.ON_BETRAYAL_EVENT.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE, which remains inference about the entity per PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE_DYNAMICS.
- Beliefs, coping, and memories, which follow their own rules.

ATTACHMENT_STYLE_NOTE:
- Attachment style shapes how far feeling falls and how the character responds, per COT_ATTACHMENT_BEHAVIORAL_MAPPING. It never supplies the injury.

PROOF_FIELDS:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: ATTACHMENT_INJURY
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: the Dungeon Master's statement of the betrayal, abandonment, or harm
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: names the target field's ladder in SCHEME_RELATIONAL.RELATIONAL_RECORD_SCHEMA or SCHEME_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS.DRIVE_STATES and asserts the endpoint lies on it, in the deterioration direction, within ENDPOINTS
- MECHANISM_TAG: ATTACHMENT_INJURY_RESPONSE
- Use SET to the explicit endpoint. Relative transition directives move one step only.

ENFORCEMENT:
- ATTACHMENT_INJURY used without its grant condition, on a field outside COVERED_FIELDS, in the recovery direction, or past ENDPOINTS: OVERRIDE_PROOF_INCOMPLETE.
- Movement backed by ATTACHMENT_INJURY proof is excluded from the cumulative movement count per STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.STEP_LIMITS_PER_CYCLE.CUMULATIVE_ORDINAL_RULE.

=== SECTION: NEW_ENTITY_ENCOUNTER ===

SALIENT_SOCIAL_ENCOUNTER_GATE:
A new ENT_XX is warranted when at least one is true:
- Entity likely recurs.
- Entity affects goal, memory, prediction, safety, attachment, belief, or alteration.
- Entity is named, role-significant, relationally charged, threatening, protective, or consequential.
- Entity becomes target of action, concern, trust, hostility, dependence, or curiosity.

ON_SALIENT_SOCIAL_ENCOUNTER:
- Instantiate ENT_XX via SET pair only if gate is met.

DEFAULT_NEW_ENTITY_VALUES:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE: NEUTRAL
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE: NEUTRAL
- COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY: ONE_DIMENSIONAL
- TRUST: GUARDED, unless a same-cycle cue or SCHEME_BACKGROUND.OUTLOOK_ON_OUTSIDERS_AND_STRANGERS supports DISTRUSTFUL or TRUSTING
- INTERACTION_FREQUENCY_ESTIMATE: derived from cue
- CURRENT_CONCERN: derived from cue or NONE

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- The four relational ladders have independent evidence types.
- TRUST moves only on reliability evidence; BETRAYED is an off-ladder rupture entered and left by SET.
- One-step law applies; multi-step downward shifts require ATTACHMENT_INJURY proof.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE is inference-based by default.
- Persistent AFFECTIVE_VALENCE movement requires MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE.
- Ally proximity alone produces overlay effects, not durable valence movement.
