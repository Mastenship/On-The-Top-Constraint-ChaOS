=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_RELATIONAL ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for affective valence transitions, perceived-other-valence inference, cognitive complexity development, encounter onboarding, and concern resolution. The two valence fields are governed by independent evidence types.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: OPERATION_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Dynamics must reference strict operation vocabulary only.

Allowed operation references:
- SET
- TRANSITION
- ADD_MODIFIER
- REMOVE_MODIFIER
- CAP
- VETO

Forbidden operation references:
- CREATE_NEW_RECORD
- UPDATE
- INCREMENT
- DECREMENT

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: New entity creation uses SET pair pattern:
- SET [STATE_RELATIONAL][RELATIONAL_REGISTRY] append ENT_XX
- SET [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX] record payload

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE moves on the character's own emotional response to interaction.

ON_POSITIVE_INTERACTION:
- Propose TRANSITION ONE_STEP_UP for [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE].
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION (character's own emotional response is directly perceived).

ON_NEGATIVE_INTERACTION:
- Propose TRANSITION ONE_STEP_DOWN for [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE].
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION.

ON_BETRAYAL_OR_HARM_EVENT:
- Multi-step downward TRANSITION requires full override proof.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE moves on observed or inferred entity affect, not on the character's own feelings.

EVIDENCE_TYPES:
- Observed entity behavior (warmth gestures, hostility cues, withdrawal, approach).
- Disclosed entity statements about their feelings toward the character.
- Inferred entity affect from facial expressiveness, proxemics, or relational pattern.

ON_POSITIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- Propose TRANSITION ONE_STEP_UP for [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE].
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE (perceived other's affect is always inferred).
- JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP.

ON_NEGATIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- Propose TRANSITION ONE_STEP_DOWN for [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE].
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE.
- JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP.

ON_DISCLOSED_ENTITY_STATEMENT:
- Propose SET to lawful PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE band consistent with disclosed content.
- EPISTEMIC_TAG: COMMITTED_FACT only when disclosure is direct and verifiable; otherwise INFERENCE.
- EVIDENCE_REF required when COMMITTED_FACT (typically MEM_XX of the disclosure event).

ASYMMETRY_NOTE:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE and PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE may diverge significantly. Asymmetric pairings (devoted toward hostile-perceived, hostile toward devoted-perceived) are realistic and lawful.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY moves on accumulated interaction depth, independent of either valence direction.

EVIDENCE_FOR_UPWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Observation of behavior in multiple distinct contexts.
- Observation contradicting prior simple categorization.
- Direct disclosure of internal state, history, or motive by the entity.
- Sustained interaction frequency with varied content.

EVIDENCE_FOR_DOWNWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Prolonged absence with no interaction (typically handled via DYNAMICS_MEMORY decay).
- Explicit cue of forced re-categorization.

ON_DEPTH_EVIDENCE:
- Propose TRANSITION ONE_STEP_UP for [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY].
- Single events rarely justify movement.

ON_FORCED_RECATEGORIZATION:
- Propose SET to a lawful lower band only with explicit cue support and override proof.

DECOUPLING_RULE:
- Movement on AFFECTIVE_VALENCE, PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE, and COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY is independent.
- Proposals bundling movement on multiple fields must justify each independently.

=== SECTION: NEW_ENTITY_ENCOUNTER ===

ON_SALIENT_SOCIAL_ENCOUNTER:
- Instantiate ENT_XX via SET pair pattern with baseline relational defaults:
  - AFFECTIVE_VALENCE: NEUTRAL
  - PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE: NEUTRAL
  - COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY: ONE_DIMENSIONAL
  - INTERACTION_FREQUENCY_ESTIMATE: derived from cue
  - CURRENT_CONCERN: derived from cue or NONE

=== SECTION: CONCERN_RESOLUTION ===

ON_CONCERN_RESOLUTION_EVENT:
- Propose SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][CURRENT_CONCERN]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: NONE
  JUSTIFICATION: concern resolution cue

ON_CONCERN_REFRESH:
- Propose SET to a new CURRENT_CONCERN value when a different active concern displaces the prior one.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- Three independent ordinal ladders (AFFECTIVE_VALENCE, PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE, COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY) with independent evidence types.
- One-step law applies to all three; multi-step shifts require override proof.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE proposals are inference-based by default and rarely qualify as COMMITTED_FACT.
