=== SECTION: COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Volatile runtime workspace for ChaOS. Stores all non-persistent cycle artifacts required for proposal staging, impulse arbitration, DPF snapshotting, selected action output, temporary coordination flags, governance audit logging, and cascade trace accumulation. Instantiated fresh each cycle, populated during cascade execution, read by synthesis and governance, verified post-commit, wiped after verification.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION: WRITE_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This file is the only authorized volatile write surface for normal cycle processing.

RULES:
- COTs may write only to explicitly permitted scratchpad sections.
- Scratchpad contents are temporary and non-persistent unless committed through STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT.
- Cross-cycle governance signal lives in STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME, not here.
- Canonical write-surface tokens (bracket-paths, IDs, OPERATION_TYPE, EPISTEMIC_TAG, JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY, MECHANISM_TAG, INVOKED_RULE_NAME, EVIDENCE_REF) remain untranslated.
- Internal deliberation may occur in any language; commit-bound narrative artifacts must be normalized at STEP_05.
- Scratchpad is fully wiped after COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION.
