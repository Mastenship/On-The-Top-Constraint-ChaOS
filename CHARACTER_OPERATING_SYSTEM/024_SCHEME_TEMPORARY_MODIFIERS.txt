=== SECTION: SCHEME_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable structural definition of temporary modifiers: transient overlays that modulate runtime behavior without directly rewriting persistent baseline state.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: MODIFIER_RECORD_SCHEMA ===

MODIFIER_ID: ID_TOKEN (MOD_XX)
STATUS: ENUM [ACTIVE]

MODIFIER_TYPE: ENUM [
  PERFORMANCE_MODIFIER,

  BIOCHEMICAL_MODIFIER,
  PSYCHOLOGICAL_MODIFIER,

=== SECTION: MODIFIER_TYPE_RESERVATION_NOTE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: BIOCHEMICAL_MODIFIER and OTHER are declared forward-compatibility modifier types but are currently reserved.

RULES:
- BIOCHEMICAL_MODIFIER and OTHER are valid MODIFIER_TYPE enum literals.
- They are currently uncreatable because DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.EFFECT_FAMILY_REGISTRY assigns them [NONE].
- A creation attempt using either type surfaces as MODIFIER_SCHEMA_VIOLATION with diagnostic MODIFIER_TYPE_RESERVED_NO_FAMILY.
- Populating either family list requires constitutional update.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: MINIMUM_CREATION_REQUIREMENTS ===

REQUIRED_ON_CREATION:
- MODIFIER_ID
- STATUS
- MODIFIER_TYPE
- SOURCE_COT
- MECHANISM_TAG
- EFFECT_FAMILY
- TARGET_FIELD
- EFFECT_EXPRESSION
- MAGNITUDE
- STACKING_RULE
- DURATION_TYPE
- EXPIRY_CONDITION
- CREATED_TIMESTAMP
- JUSTIFICATION

ADDITIONAL_REQUIREMENTS:
- EFFECT_FAMILY must be a registry token paired with MODIFIER_TYPE per DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.EFFECT_FAMILY_REGISTRY.
- TARGET_FIELD must be a lawful MODIFIER_TARGET_FIELD_FORM: a canonical path, or DOMAIN_GLOBAL:<SCOPE> for diffuse overlays.
- TARGET_ID is required for record-specific or entity-specific scope and empty for DOMAIN_GLOBAL or domain-global scope.
- EFFECT_EXPRESSION must distinguish the transient effect.
- LAST_REFRESHED may be empty on first creation.
- BIOCHEMICAL_MODIFIER and OTHER are currently reserved and have no lawful EFFECT_FAMILY pairing until constitutional update.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: TARGETING_LOGIC ===

TARGET_FIELD_RULES:
- TARGET_FIELD identifies the state domain or field being modulated.
- TARGET_FIELD does not authorize direct mutation of the target.
- Scoped modulation uses a canonical path.
- Diffuse modulation with no single canonical field uses DOMAIN_GLOBAL:<SCOPE> per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.DOMAIN_GLOBAL_SCOPES.

TARGET_ID_RULES:
- Use TARGET_ID when scoped to concrete ENT_XX, SKL_XX, GOAL_XX, MEM_XX, or other canonical record.
- TARGET_ID is empty for DOMAIN_GLOBAL and domain-global modifiers.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: ACTIVE_SURFACE_INTERPRETATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS is an active-overlay surface, not history.

ACTIVE_SURFACE_RULES:
- Present modifier records are runtime-relevant active overlays.
- STATUS is ACTIVE for every record on this surface.
- Expired or removed modifiers leave the surface.
- Lifecycle history belongs to audit and trace, not persistent modifier retention.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: LIFECYCLE_AUTHORITY_NOTE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This SCHEME defines structure only. DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS defines lifecycle mechanics and the EFFECT_FAMILY and MECHANISM_TAG vocabularies.

DYNAMICS_OWNS:
- EFFECT_FAMILY registry
- MECHANISM_TAG policy
- refresh legality
- collision and identity matching
- replacement behavior
- stacking/cap interaction
- expiry
- REMOVE_MODIFIER behavior

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Temporary modifiers must not bypass baseline governance.
- TARGET_FIELD must be a lawful MODIFIER_TARGET_FIELD_FORM.
- EFFECT_FAMILY must be a registry token paired with MODIFIER_TYPE.
- BIOCHEMICAL_MODIFIER and OTHER are reserved until a constitutional update assigns lawful EFFECT_FAMILY pairings.
- MAGNITUDE must be QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE.
- EXPIRY_CONDITION and JUSTIFICATION are required for creation and refresh.
- Active modifiers must not be orphaned from registry or target path.
- Modifier effects remain bounded by realism, policy caps, and qualitative state-machine logic.
- Transient situational effects should use modifiers before baseline rewrite is considered.
