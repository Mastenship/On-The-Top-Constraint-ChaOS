=== SECTION: FORMATION_CRITERIA ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Memory admission is conservative. Vivid cycles do not automatically become persistent memories.

MEMORY_ADMISSION_GATE:
A new MEM_XX is warranted only when at least one PRIMARY_ADMISSION_CONDITION and one LINKAGE_CONDITION are met.

PRIMARY_ADMISSION_CONDITIONS:
- HIGH or EXTREME emotional arousal with narrative consequence.
- Novelty changing understanding, options, risk, identity, relationship, goal, skill, alteration, belief, or DPF.
- Consequence event producing or resolving meaningful state change.
- Prediction resolution, falsification, or major DPF contrast.
- Breakthrough, failure, betrayal, loss, rescue, revelation, injury, alteration activation, or other state-worthy event.
- Repeated cue completing a persistence cluster.

LINKAGE_CONDITIONS:
- At least one canonical SYSTEM_LINKAGE path can be identified.
- Memory associates with state change, state pressure, prediction, entity, skill, alteration, goal, or belief field.
- Memory is needed as evidence for COMMITTED_FACT, persistence cluster, breakthrough, or future governed transition.

NON_ADMISSION_CASES:
- Routine scene texture.
- Ordinary travel or waiting without consequence.
- Minor dialogue without relational, belief, goal, or DPF relevance.
- Raw chain-of-thought residue.
- Duplicate restatement without new state relevance.
- Sensory detail without salience, novelty, consequence, or linkage.

ON_SALIENCE_THRESHOLD_MET:
- If MEMORY_ADMISSION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE SET pair for MEM_XX.
  - Initial SALIENCE and SYSTEM_LINKAGE must be explicit.

FLASHBULB_GATE:
- Requires EXTREME salience plus direct identity, safety, attachment, trauma, revelation, or irreversible consequence pressure.
- Ordinary intensity alone is insufficient.
- Exceptions beyond ordinary creation limits require full proof.

=== SECTION: RECONSOLIDATION_RULES ===

ON_RETRIEVAL_WITH_NEW_CONTEXT:
- Propose SET only for lawful mutable subfields:
  - SALIENCE
  - CORE_EVENT_SUMMARY
  - SENSORY_IMPRINT
  - INTERNAL_MONOLOGUE_EXCERPT
  - lawful SYSTEM_LINKAGE augmentation
- Must include MECHANISM_TAG and cue-based justification.

RECONSOLIDATION_BOUNDS:
- May refine framing, salience, sensory emphasis, or linkage.
- Must not rewrite core event into contradictory past unless a later memory-correction policy exists.
- INTERNAL_MONOLOGUE_EXCERPT is stylized remembered artifact, not raw reasoning.

=== SECTION: DECAY_AND_FORGETTING ===

ON_TEMPORAL_DECAY_MAJOR:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_MEMORY][MEM_XX][MEMORY_METADATA][SALIENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
  JUSTIFICATION: temporal decay cue

DECAY_BOUNDS:
- Highly linked memories decay more slowly.
- Memories used as evidence require caution before salience reduction.
- Decay changes salience; it does not delete records.

RETENTION_THRESHOLD_NOTE:
- If salience falls below retention threshold and no protected linkage remains, do not delete silently.
- Any archival, retirement, or deactivation behavior requires explicit governance policy.
