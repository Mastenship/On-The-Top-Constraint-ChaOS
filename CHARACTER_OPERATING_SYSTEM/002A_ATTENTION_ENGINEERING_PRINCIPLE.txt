=== SECTION: CONSULTATION_CLASSES ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every persistent file falls into exactly one consultation class. Classes determine how aggressively the runtime must re-read the file each cycle.

CONSULTATION_CLASS: ENUM [ALWAYS_CONSULTED, ON_TRIGGER, ON_DEMAND]

ALWAYS_CONSULTED:
- Constitutional layer: TABLE_OF_CONTENTS, LLM_FORMATTING_GUIDELINES, ATTENTION_ENGINEERING_PRINCIPLE, CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.
- Kernel layer: STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY, UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY, STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT.
- Volatile layer: COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD.
- Re-evaluated every cycle without exception.

ON_TRIGGER:
- All COT_ templates (priorities 01 through 17).
- All DYNAMICS_ modules.
- Active only when the FIRES_WHEN conditions declared in the COT (or referenced from the DYNAMICS pattern) are present in the current cycle.
- Absent triggering conditions, COTs emit only the EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.

ON_DEMAND:
- All SCHEME_ modules.
- All STATE_ modules.
- Read when a COT or governance step needs the schema or current values; not blanket-loaded for cycles that do not touch that domain.

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN_DISCIPLINE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every COT in priority 01 through 15 declares a FIRES_WHEN section near the top of its template. The runtime evaluates FIRES_WHEN conditions before generating any impulses or proposals.

FIRES_WHEN_SEMANTICS:
- FIRES_WHEN lists cycle-resolvable conditions under which the COT is permitted to do more than emit an EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry.
- Conditions are deterministic and inspectable from the current cycle's narrative input, persistent state, and scratchpad contents.
- A COT whose FIRES_WHEN conditions are not met MUST emit only the EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace and MUST NOT generate impulses or non-trace proposals.

FIRING_DISCIPLINE_VERIFICATION:
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION inspects whether COTs that generated impulses or non-trace proposals had at least one FIRES_WHEN condition satisfied.
- A COT that fired without an anchor cue surfaces as COT_FIRED_WITHOUT_ANCHOR_CUE (WARN-class).

EXEMPTIONS:
- COT_DECISION_SYNTHESIS (priority 14) and COT_COMMUNICATION_PROCESSING (priority 15) always run because they consume scratchpad output and produce SELECTED_ACTION and the realized output respectively. They are exempt from FIRES_WHEN discipline.
- COT_DPF_SYNTHESIS_PROCESSING (priority 13) always runs because it produces the CURRENT_DPF snapshot used by decision synthesis. Exempt from FIRES_WHEN discipline.
- Governance band (priority 16, priority 17) is not subject to FIRES_WHEN.
- COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS runs only during instantiation workflow per its own template; it is outside ordinary cycle execution and therefore not subject to FIRES_WHEN.
