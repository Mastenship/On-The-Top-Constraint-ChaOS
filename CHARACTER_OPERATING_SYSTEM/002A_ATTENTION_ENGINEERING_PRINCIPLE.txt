=== SECTION: CONSULTATION_CLASSES ===

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN_DISCIPLINE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Priority 01–15 COTs declare FIRES_WHEN unless exempted. Runtime evaluates FIRES_WHEN before impulses or non-trace proposals.

RULES:
- If no FIRES_WHEN condition is met, COT emits only EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace.
- No impulses or non-trace proposals without at least one satisfied anchor cue.
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION raises COT_FIRED_WITHOUT_ANCHOR_CUE when a non-exempt COT fires without anchor cue.

EXEMPTIONS:
- COT_DPF_SYNTHESIS_PROCESSING: always runs; produces CURRENT_DPF.
- COT_DECISION_SYNTHESIS: always runs; produces SELECTED_ACTION.
- COT_COMMUNICATION_PROCESSING: always runs; realizes SELECTED_ACTION.
- Governance band PRIORITY_16–18.
- COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS: invoked only during instantiation workflow.
