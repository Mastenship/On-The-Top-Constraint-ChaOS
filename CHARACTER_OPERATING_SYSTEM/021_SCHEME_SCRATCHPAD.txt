=== SECTION: SCHEME_SCRATCHPAD ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable structural definition of COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD: volatile per-cycle workspace for proposals, impulses, CURRENT_DPF, SELECTED_ACTION, flags, governance audit, normalization audit, trace, and runtime summaries.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: all COTs, COT_DECISION_SYNTHESIS, STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT, COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION.
Is Informed By: narrative input, SCHEME_ constraints, UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY, STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: LIFECYCLE ===

LIFECYCLE_ORDER:
- INSTANTIATE at cycle start.
- POPULATE by COTs.
- READ by synthesis and governance.
- COMMIT primary proposals via STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_PRIMARY_PASS.
- VERIFY via COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION.
- COMMIT verifier-originated governance-runtime proposals via STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS.
- WIPE completely after post-verification governance pass.

INVARIANT:
- No scratchpad artifact survives across cycles unless lawfully committed to persistent STATE_* or STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Scratchpad is volatile and fully wiped after post-verification governance pass.
- Proposal and impulse packets must match UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY schemas.
- SELECTED_ACTION must match strict schema before communication processing.
- Governance audit must close every received proposal in each commit pass.
- Normalization audit is populated when commit-bound or display-bound normalization is relevant.
- Cross-cycle governance signal lives in STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME, not scratchpad.
- INSTANTIATION_MODE is TRUE only during COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS workflow.
- Empty-value forms follow CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.EMPTY_VALUE_FORMS.
