=== SECTION: STRICT_OPERATION_VOCABULARY ===

ALLOWED_OPERATION_TYPE:
- SET
- TRANSITION
- ADD_MODIFIER
- REMOVE_MODIFIER
- CAP
- VETO

FORBIDDEN_OPERATION_ALIASES:
- CREATE_NEW_RECORD
- UPDATE
- INCREMENT
- DECREMENT

OPERATION_SEMANTICS_MINIMUM:
- SET names explicit lawful endpoint or schema-valid payload; it does not bypass movement, enum, invariant, cap, or override-proof limits.
- TRANSITION applies canonical relative directive to lawful ordinal or SCHEME-declared ladder.
- Categorical fields use SET unless SCHEME declares transition ladder.
- ADD_MODIFIER and REMOVE_MODIFIER operate on STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.
- CAP applies bounded ceiling only where permitted.
- VETO suppresses lower-precedence or disallowed mutation.

=== SECTION: QUALITATIVE_ENUMS ===

QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY: ENUM [MINIMAL, LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, EXTREME]
QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY_GLOSS:
- MINIMAL: trace presence
- LOW: weak but relevant
- MODERATE: clearly present
- HIGH: domain-dominant
- EXTREME: acute saturating presence

QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY_USAGE:
- proposal STRENGTH
- impulse IMPULSE_STRENGTH
- memory SALIENCE
- prediction CERTAINTY
- DPF CERTAINTY_LEVEL.VALUE
- goal URGENCY, CONFIDENCE, PROGRESS, COMMITMENT_LEVEL, VALUE_ALIGNMENT, IDENTITY_RELEVANCE
- SELECTED_ACTION.CONFIDENCE
- COPE qualitative risk/probability fields

QUALITATIVE_PERCENTILE: ENUM [VERY_LOW, LOW, AVERAGE, HIGH, VERY_HIGH]
QUALITATIVE_PERCENTILE_GLOSS:
- VERY_LOW: below 20th percentile
- LOW: 20th to 40th percentile
- AVERAGE: 40th to 60th percentile
- HIGH: 60th to 80th percentile
- VERY_HIGH: above 80th percentile

QUALITATIVE_T_SCORE: ENUM [NORMAL, MILD_ELEVATION, MODERATE, SIGNIFICANT, CLINICAL]
QUALITATIVE_T_SCORE_GLOSS:
- NORMAL: below 55
- MILD_ELEVATION: 55 to 65
- MODERATE: 65 to 75
- SIGNIFICANT: 75 to 85
- CLINICAL: above 85

QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE: ENUM [TRACE, LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, EXTREME]
QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE_GLOSS:
- TRACE: barely perceptible transient influence
- LOW: weak transient influence
- MODERATE: clear situational influence
- HIGH: strong situational influence
- EXTREME: acute overriding pressure bounded by governance

QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE_USAGE:
- SCHEME_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.MAGNITUDE only

=== SECTION: EVIDENCE_REF_POLICY ===

EVIDENCE_REF_FIELD:
- Required when EPISTEMIC_TAG = COMMITTED_FACT.
- Optional otherwise.

EVIDENCE_REF_VALUE_FORMS:
- MEM_XX
- PRD_XX only when RESOLVED
- PRIOR_COMMIT:<COMMIT_CYCLE_ID>:<PROPOSAL_ID>
- USER_CONFIRMATION:<CONFIRMATION_ID>
- CYCLE_CUE:<CYCLE_ID>:<CUE_TOKEN>
- [MEM_XX, MEM_XX, MEM_XX] for persistence clusters of three or more memories

DISCLOSURE_BOUNDARY:
- Direct disclosure may establish that a statement occurred.
- Another entity's internal state remains INFERENCE or SPECULATION unless substrate grants direct access.

ENFORCEMENT:
- Missing COMMITTED_FACT evidence: COMMITTED_FACT_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE
- Unresolvable evidence: EVIDENCE_REF_UNRESOLVED
- CYCLE_CUE is lawful only when cue alone reaches COMMITTED_FACT threshold.
