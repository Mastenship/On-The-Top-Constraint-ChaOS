=== SECTION: CANONICAL_STYLE_RULES ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Canonical bracket-path tokens MUST be machine-stable.

TOKEN_STYLE:
- UPPER_SNAKE only
- No spaces, hyphens, or mixed case
- IDs uppercase with underscores (ENT_XX, SKL_XX, ALT_XX, MEM_XX, PRD_XX, GOAL_XX, MOD_XX, PUP_, ACT_)

CANONICAL_PATH_FORMAT:
- [MODULE_ROOT][GROUP_OR_ID][FIELD]
- Nested groups extend with additional brackets.

PROHIBITED:
- spaces in tokens
- colon pseudo-paths
- Title Case bracket tokens
- non-canonical roots
- legacy root translation

=== SECTION: QUALITATIVE_ENUMS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: These enums replace prior numeric scales. Each is distinct in semantic role and not interchangeable.

QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY: ENUM [MINIMAL, LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, EXTREME]
QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY_GLOSS:
- MINIMAL: trace presence
- LOW: weak but behaviorally relevant
- MODERATE: clearly present
- HIGH: behaviorally dominant within domain
- EXTREME: acute saturating presence
QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY_USAGE:
- STRENGTH on proposals
- IMPULSE_STRENGTH on impulses
- SALIENCE on memory records
- CERTAINTY on prediction records and CERTAINTY_LEVEL.VALUE on STATE_DPF
- CONFIDENCE on goal records and SELECTED_ACTION
- URGENCY, PROGRESS, COMMITMENT_LEVEL, VALUE_ALIGNMENT, IDENTITY_RELEVANCE on goal records
- SUBSTANCE_USE_RISK and the five COPE_PROBABILITY_BIASES fields

QUALITATIVE_PERCENTILE: ENUM [VERY_LOW, LOW, AVERAGE, HIGH, VERY_HIGH]
QUALITATIVE_PERCENTILE_GLOSS:
- VERY_LOW: below 20th percentile
- LOW: 20th to 40th percentile
- AVERAGE: 40th to 60th percentile
- HIGH: 60th to 80th percentile
- VERY_HIGH: above 80th percentile
QUALITATIVE_PERCENTILE_USAGE:
- SCHEME_HEXACO trait markers
- SCHEME_DARK_TRIAD trait markers
- SCHEME_ASQ ATTACHMENT_SECURITY

QUALITATIVE_T_SCORE: ENUM [NORMAL, MILD_ELEVATION, MODERATE, SIGNIFICANT, CLINICAL]
QUALITATIVE_T_SCORE_GLOSS:
- NORMAL: T-score below 55
- MILD_ELEVATION: 55 to 65
- MODERATE: 65 to 75
- SIGNIFICANT: 75 to 85
- CLINICAL: above 85
QUALITATIVE_T_SCORE_USAGE:
- SCHEME_MMPI clinical scale baselines

QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE: ENUM [TRACE, LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, EXTREME]
QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE_GLOSS:
- TRACE: barely perceptible transient influence
- LOW: weak transient influence
- MODERATE: clear situational influence
- HIGH: strong situational influence
- EXTREME: acute overriding pressure still bounded by governance
QUALITATIVE_MAGNITUDE_USAGE:
- SCHEME_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.MAGNITUDE only (carries lifecycle semantics)

=== SECTION: EPISTEMIC_TAG_ENUM ===

EPISTEMIC_TAG: ENUM [PERCEPTION, INFERENCE, SPECULATION, COMMITTED_FACT, TOOL_REQUEST]

RULES:
- Required on every proposal.

=== SECTION: EVIDENCE_REF_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COMMITTED_FACT proposals MUST carry structural evidence linkage.

EVIDENCE_REF_FIELD:
- Required when EPISTEMIC_TAG = COMMITTED_FACT
- Optional otherwise

EVIDENCE_REF_VALUE_FORMS:
- MEM_XX reference
- PRD_XX reference (only when prediction is RESOLVED)
- PRIOR_COMMIT:<COMMIT_CYCLE_ID>:<PROPOSAL_ID>

ENFORCEMENT:
- Missing EVIDENCE_REF on COMMITTED_FACT: COMMITTED_FACT_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE
- Unresolvable EVIDENCE_REF: EVIDENCE_REF_UNRESOLVED

=== SECTION: JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY_ENUM ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Required on every impulse and on every proposal originating from an impulse. Categorizes cognitive route, separately from epistemic stance.

JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: ENUM [DIRECT_APPLICATION, INFERENTIAL_LEAP, UNJUSTIFIED_LEAP, GOAL_DIRECTED, TRAIT_GATED]

GLOSS:
- DIRECT_APPLICATION: direct cue-to-rule application
- INFERENTIAL_LEAP: structured inference across multiple cues
- UNJUSTIFIED_LEAP: associative or subconscious surfacing
- GOAL_DIRECTED: active goal pursuit pressure
- TRAIT_GATED: trait-threshold gating

EPISTEMIC_TAG_MAPPING:
- DIRECT_APPLICATION: PERCEPTION or INFERENCE
- INFERENTIAL_LEAP: INFERENCE
- UNJUSTIFIED_LEAP: SPECULATION
- GOAL_DIRECTED: INFERENCE
- TRAIT_GATED: INFERENCE

ENFORCEMENT:
- Mismatch with mapping: JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY_TAG_MISMATCH

=== SECTION: STRUCTURAL_OVERRIDE_PROOF_FIELDS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Override proof is structural. Narrative justification text alone does not satisfy it.

REQUIRED_FIELDS_WHEN_OVERRIDE_GATE_TRIGGERED:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: short canonical token or quoted cue fragment from cycle narrative input; must be cycle-resolvable
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: short statement naming the SCHEME_ invariant the override interacts with and asserting compatibility under the named rule
- MECHANISM_TAG: domain-canonical mechanism name aligned with the relevant DYNAMICS_ pattern

ENFORCEMENT:
- Any required field missing or unlawful: OVERRIDE_PROOF_INCOMPLETE

=== SECTION: READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS_ENUM ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every persistent module declares a READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS in its [KEEP: Purpose & Role] block. The header declaration is the source of truth.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: ENUM [CHARACTER_VISIBLE, GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL]

GLOSS:

ENFORCEMENT:
- A character-cognition COT reading a GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL module: READ_BOUNDARY_VIOLATION (raised by COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION from CASCADE_TRACE inspection)

=== SECTION: RUNNABLE_BUT_LIFTABLE_RESERVATIONS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Surfaces declared but not actuatable on the current substrate use this pattern. Surface is canonically declared, annotated RESERVED, and governance enforces a deterministic suppression code on actuation attempts. Lifting to external governance consists of removing the suppression rule and supplying the actuation surface; no schema migration is required.

CURRENTLY_RESERVED:
- TOOL_REQUEST epistemic tag: TOOL_ACTUATION_NOT_ACTIVE
- ROLLBACK remediation mode: ROLLBACK_NOT_ACTIVE
