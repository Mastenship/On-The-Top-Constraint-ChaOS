=== SECTION: SKILL_CHANGE_THRESHOLD_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Permanent skill mutation is rare.

PERMANENT_SKILL_CHANGE_GATE:
A permanent skill transition is warranted only when at least one is true:
- Deliberate practice is explicit, sustained, and skill-relevant.
- Breakthrough event is memory-linked and salience-supported.
- Repeated successful execution shows durable automation beyond one scene.
- Lasting injury, impairment, or alteration takes away durable capability. Disuse alone never does.
- Training, instruction, adaptation, injury, alteration, or technology changes durable baseline.

NON_CHANGE_CASES:
- One ordinary success.
- One ordinary failure.
- Stress/fatigue underperformance.
- Environmental difficulty.
- Temporary boost from alteration, stimulant, flow, or assistance.
- Luck or outcome success without skill-change evidence.
- Disuse alone; it produces SKILL_RUST per ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU.ON_EXTENDED_NON_USE.

    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE

    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][GROWTH_POTENTIAL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: progression consumes growth potential

ON_BREAKTHROUGH_EVENT:
- If memory linkage and salience support:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: memory-linked breakthrough
- Mastery rises one step at a time; no rule permits a multi-step increase.

=== SECTION: INSTINCT_AUTOMATION ===

ON_REPEATED_SUCCESSFUL_EXECUTION:
- If mastery threshold and durable automation support exist:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][INSTINCT_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: repeated successful execution supports automation
- Must preserve INSTINCT_LEVEL <= MASTERY_LEVEL.

AUTOMATION_GATE:
- Requires more than one isolated success.
- Support may come from memory, training history, repeated scene pattern, or explicit practice.

=== SECTION: ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU ===

ON_EXTENDED_NON_USE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Hard-won skill is not lost to disuse. Disuse dulls sharpness: timing, speed, and confidence. That is SKILL_RUST, a temporary overlay that practice clears. MASTERY_LEVEL and INSTINCT_LEVEL do not change.
- If RUST_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
    PROPOSED_VALUE:
      MODIFIER_TYPE: PERFORMANCE_MODIFIER
      EFFECT_FAMILY: SKILL_RUST
      TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL]
      TARGET_ID: SKL_XX
      EFFECT_EXPRESSION: dulled timing, speed, and confidence from disuse
      MAGNITUDE: LOW | MODERATE | HIGH
      STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
      DURATION_TYPE: UNTIL_CONDITION
      EXPIRY_CONDITION: the character practices or uses the skill again
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_DISUSE_RUST
    JUSTIFICATION: extended disuse dulls sharpness
- MAGNITUDE reflects how long the skill has gone unused. An INSTINCTIVE skill rusts no further than LOW.
- Longer disuse replaces the rust in place with a higher MAGNITUDE. Practice removes it per DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.EXPIRY_TRANSITIONS.
- Optionally PROPOSE TRANSITION on GROWTH_POTENTIAL ONE_STEP_DOWN for stagnation.

RUST_GATE:
- Requires major elapsed time or practice deprivation relevant to the skill.
- Ordinary time passage without skill relevance is insufficient.

ON_CAPABILITY_LOSS:
- When an alteration, including a lasting injury, takes away the capacity a skill depends on:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: lasting loss of the capacity the skill depends on
  - If the lowered MASTERY_LEVEL would leave INSTINCT_LEVEL above what SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING allows, also PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][INSTINCT_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: instinct fades with the mastery it rests on
  - The MASTERY_LEVEL and INSTINCT_LEVEL proposals are one linked unit; neither commits without the other.
- Loss beyond one step requires DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE proof. A lasting injury is an alteration per DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.NEW_ALTERATION_ADMISSION.
- Impairment that will heal is a TEMPORARY alteration. Its effect rides on ALTERATION_SKILL_DEGRADATION and never changes mastery.

ON_PLATEAU_REACHED:
- If mastery is top bound or practice no longer progresses:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][GROWTH_POTENTIAL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: PLATEAUED
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: plateau reached

=== SECTION: NEW_SKILL_ACQUISITION ===

NEW_SKILL_ADMISSION_GATE:
A new SKL_XX is warranted only when:
- Skill is distinct from existing SKL_XX records.
- Character has explicit exposure, training, practice, instruction, alteration, or acquisition context.
- Skill is likely to recur or matter to action, identity, goals, or narrative.
- Proposed record contains lawful SKILL_DOMAIN, MASTERY_LEVEL, INSTINCT_LEVEL, GROWTH_POTENTIAL, UNIQUENESS_LEVEL, and ACQUISITION_CONTEXT.

ON_EXPLICIT_LEARNING_EVENT:
- Use SET pair for SKL_XX creation only when gate is met.

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- Permanent skill mutation carries MECHANISM_TAG SKILL_STATE_CHANGE and requires governance validation.
- Momentary execution pressure flows through modifiers.
- INSTINCT_LEVEL must not exceed MASTERY_LEVEL.
- GROWTH_POTENTIAL cannot remain HIGH when MASTERY_LEVEL is MASTER.
- New skill creation requires admission gate and registry-record coherence.
- Skill gains move one step at a time. Multi-step loss requires ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE proof.
