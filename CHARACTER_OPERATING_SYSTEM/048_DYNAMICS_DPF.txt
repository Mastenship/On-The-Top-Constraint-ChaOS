=== SECTION: PREDICTION_WORTHINESS_GATE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Not every expectation becomes PRD_XX. Prediction records are state-worthy anticipated outcomes with future resolution value.

PREDICTION_RECORD_ADMISSION_CRITERIA:
A new PRD_XX is warranted only when at least one is true:
- Outcome materially affects an ACTIVE or HIGH/EXTREME urgency GOAL_XX.
- Outcome would change safety, attachment, belief, alteration, or major relational state.
- Prediction has a clear future resolution condition: RESOLVED, RESOLVED_NEGATIVE, or FALSIFIED.
- Prediction links to salient memory, explicit threat, opportunity, promise, warning, or obstacle.
- Prediction is needed to explain durable DPF shift, not passing tactical thought.

NON_ADMISSION_CASES:
- Passing guesses.
- Flavor speculation without resolution condition.
- Momentary anxiety not linked to action, goal, safety, relationship, or belief.
- Redundant active PRD_XX coverage.
- Raw chain-of-thought fragments.

ADMISSION_RESULT:
- If not admitted, keep interpretation in [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][CURRENT_DPF] or temporary modifier.
- If admitted, use SET pair and respect MAX_ACTIVE_PREDICTIONS.

=== SECTION: PREDICTION_ACCUMULATION ===

ON_MINOR_CONTRADICTION:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_DPF][DPF_METRICS][CERTAINTY_LEVEL][VALUE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
  JUSTIFICATION: contradiction cue
- If prediction-specific:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_DPF][PRD_XX][CERTAINTY]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lower QUALITATIVE_INTENSITY band
    JUSTIFICATION: prediction contradiction cue
- If certainty falls below falsification threshold with cue support:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_DPF][PRD_XX][STATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: FALSIFIED | RESOLVED_NEGATIVE
    JUSTIFICATION: contradiction resolution cue
- If contradiction load exceeds cycle tolerance:
  - queue [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][TEMPORARY_STATE_FLAGS][DPF_RECALC_QUEUED] = TRUE

ON_RESOLUTION_EVENT:
- If confirmed:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_DPF][PRD_XX][STATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: RESOLVED
    JUSTIFICATION: resolution cue
- Certainty reinforcement may SET adjacent higher band on PRD_XX.CERTAINTY or DPF CERTAINTY_LEVEL.VALUE.

ON_DRIVE_ACTIVATION_WITH_OBSTACLE_OR_OPPORTUNITY:
- Create PRD_XX only when PREDICTION_RECORD_ADMISSION_CRITERIA are met.

ON_TIME_DRIFT_WITHOUT_SIGNIFICANT_EVENTS:
- Propose bounded SET drift to:
  - [STATE_DPF][DPF_METRICS][CERTAINTY_LEVEL][VALUE]
  - [STATE_DPF][DPF_CORE_STATE][EMOTIONAL_VALENCE][VALUE]
  when cue support indicates drift toward current baseline interpretive state.

=== SECTION: RECALCULATION_GUARD ===

CYCLE_RECALC_GUARD:
- Only one full DPF recalculation per cycle.
- Additional contradictions queue DPF_RECALC_QUEUED TRUE.

ON_DPF_CONTRAST_EVENT:
- Multi-field DPF shifts require full override proof.
- Reserve DPF_CONTRAST_EVENT for durable interpretive contrast, not ordinary mood fluctuation.
