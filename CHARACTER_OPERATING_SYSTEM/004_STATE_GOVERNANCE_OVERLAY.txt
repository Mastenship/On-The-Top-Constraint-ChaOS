=== SECTION: STEP_LIMITS_PER_CYCLE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Per-cycle movement is bounded unless complete override proof is present. Limits are hard; breach surfaces as STEP_LIMIT_EXCEEDED.

GENERAL_LIMITS:
- One ordinal step per field against its declared ladder.
- One qualitative band per field.
- SET endpoint placement is subject to the same movement limits as TRANSITION.
- Multi-step movement requires STRUCTURAL_OVERRIDE_PROOF_FIELDS.
- PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT categorical fields are exempt from one-step limits per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.ORDINAL_LADDER_AUTHORITY.

CUMULATIVE_ORDINAL_RULE:
- Cumulative ordinary ordinal movement is the sum of absolute step distances across all committed ordinal and qualitative fields in the cycle.
- Override-proof-backed movements are excluded from the cumulative count.
- A COUPLED_MOVEMENT_SET of same-direction movements driven by one mechanistic event counts as a single step toward the cumulative total per COUPLED_MOVEMENT_REGISTRY.
- Cumulative ordinary movement exceeding two steps is breach; the lowest-precedence ordinary movements are downgraded toward no-change until the count is two or fewer, otherwise surplus movements are suppressed with STEP_LIMIT_EXCEEDED.

INTENTIONAL_THROTTLE_NOTE:
- The two-step cumulative cap is deliberate throttling and is expected behavior.
- COT and DYNAMICS layers over-propose by design; not every lawful single-field proposal commits in an eventful cycle.
- CONFLICT_RESOLUTION_POLICY.PRECEDENCE_ORDER determines which movements commit; lower-precedence housekeeping movements such as decay and drift may defer to a later cycle rather than crowd out a primary event.
- Authors should not assume their proposals commit independently in a busy cycle.

COUPLED_MOVEMENT_REGISTRY:
- Named same-direction co-activations that count as one cumulative step because they are one mechanistic event:
  - SYMPATHETIC_HPA: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][ADRENALINE] with [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][CORTISOL]
  - FATIGUE_DEBT: [STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS][FATIGUE_ACCUMULATION][CURRENT_FATIGUE_LEVEL] with [STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS][FATIGUE_ACCUMULATION][ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT]
  - CERTAINTY_DECAY: [STATE_DPF][DPF_METRICS][CERTAINTY_LEVEL][VALUE] with [STATE_DPF][PRD_XX][CERTAINTY]
- A coupled set counts once only when every member moves one step in the same ladder direction in the same cycle under aligned mechanism.
- Members moving independently, in opposite directions, or beyond one step each count separately and are not collapsed.
- Coupling never raises a member above its own one-step-per-field limit.
- Additions require constitutional update.

MEMORY_LIMITS:
- Ordinary memory creation cap: 1 MEM_XX per cycle.
- FLASHBULB_BREAKTHROUGH may permit up to 2 MEM_XX with full proof.
- Memory creation requires registry-record SET pair and lawful SYSTEM_LINKAGE.

PREDICTION_LIMITS:
- MAX_ACTIVE_PREDICTIONS: 10.
- PRD_XX creation requires registry-record SET pair.
- Over-cap creation is suppressed unless concurrent resolution/falsification keeps active count within cap.

TEMPORARY_MODIFIER_LIMITS:
- Active overlay surface, not archive.
- Material sameness follows DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.MATCH_KEY_TUPLE.
- Same active modifier refreshes or replaces in place.
- Expired/removed modifiers leave active surface.
- Stacking must not create covert baseline rewrite.

GOAL_LIMITS:
- GOAL_XX creation requires registry-record SET pair and DYNAMICS_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS goal-worthiness.
- Passing wants and single-turn intents remain impulses, selected actions, modifiers, or DPF interpretations.

SKILL_LIMITS:
- Permanent skill mutation requires SKILL_STATE_CHANGE support.
- INSTINCT_LEVEL must satisfy SCHEME_SKILLS.INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING.
- Momentary performance effects use modifiers.
- Multi-step skill movement requires complete override proof.

BELIEF_AND_COMMUNICATION_LIMITS:
- Durable belief and communication baseline shifts require relevant DYNAMICS support.
- Persistence-triggered shifts require memory-ledger evidence and bounded movement.

=== SECTION: CROSS_FIELD_INVARIANT_TIMING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Cross-field invariants depend on resulting committed state, not single proposals.

RULES:
- Cross-field invariants evaluate against projected committed state after conflict reconciliation and limit evaluation, before persistent commit.
- Governed cross-field invariants:
  - SCHEME_BODY immune/terminal-state suppression: IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_CONFLICT.
  - SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS strong-strong contradiction marker requirement: SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.
  - SCHEME_SKILLS INSTINCT_MASTERY_COUPLING: SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.
  - SCHEME_SKILLS GROWTH_POTENTIAL ceiling (GROWTH_POTENTIAL must not remain HIGH when MASTERY_LEVEL is MASTER): SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.
- A proposal set whose members each pass individually but jointly violate a cross-field invariant is resolved jointly: the lowest-precedence contributing proposals are downgraded or suppressed until the projected state is lawful, except where an INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION is the designated resolution below.

IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This invariant is resolved by correcting the immune flag, not by blocking physiological decline.
- Trigger: projected committed state holds IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST with two or more terminal biochemical states present.
- Designated resolution: an INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION that moves IMMUNE_MARKERS one band off ROBUST toward NORMAL on its declared ladder.
- The contributing terminal-state transitions are preserved and committed; they are not suppressed to protect a stale ROBUST value.
- INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION is a bounded governance reconciliation write to a single existing committed field per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.GOVERNANCE_RECONCILIATION_ACTIONS; it is audit-logged and is not character-cognition proposal generation.
- If projected IMMUNE_MARKERS is already below ROBUST, the invariant is satisfied and no correction is applied.
- A severe single-cycle immune drop greater than one band (for example ROBUST directly to COMPROMISED) requires override proof on an IMMUNE_MARKERS proposal exactly like other multi-step physiological deterioration.
- Residual unresolved breach surfaces as IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_CONFLICT.

=== SECTION: CONFLICT_RESOLUTION_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Resolve conflicts deterministically. Do not merge incompatible mutations.

CONFLICT_GROUPING:
- Group proposals by effective TARGET_FIELD.
- Group modifier proposals by DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.MATCH_KEY_TUPLE.
- Evaluate registry-record SET pairs as linked creation units.

PRECEDENCE_ORDER:
1) SCHEME invariant preservation.
2) Canonical path, write-eligibility, and operation validity.
3) Complete override proof.
4) COMMITTED_FACT with resolvable EVIDENCE_REF.
5) Higher STRENGTH.
6) More specific TARGET_FIELD.
7) Earlier SOURCE_COT execution priority.
8) Conservative no-change or downgrade.

TIE_BREAKERS:
- Prefer deterministic bounded downgrade over suppression.
- Prefer temporary modifier over durable baseline mutation for short-horizon effects.
- Prefer existing record update over new record creation when lawful.
- Prefer in-place modifier refresh/replacement over new MOD_XX when materially same.
- If still tied, suppress less-evidenced proposal.

VETO_POLICY:
- VETO suppresses lower-precedence conflicting proposals.
- VETO must cite lawful reason and target a specific proposal or mutation class.
- VETO mutates only by suppression effect.

DOWNGRADE_POLICY:
- Lawful only when nearest bounded outcome is deterministic.
- May convert excessive movement to one-step/one-band.
- May redirect short-horizon baseline mutation to lawful modifier pattern.
- May apply an INVARIANT_FORCED_CORRECTION where a cross-field invariant designates it as the resolution; this corrects one existing field one band rather than suppressing the contributing proposals.
- If no deterministic downgrade exists, suppress.

=== SECTION: REMEDIATION_POLICY ===

OUTCOME_CLASSES:
- PASS: zero violations and zero warnings.
- WARN: zero violations and one or more warnings.
- FAIL: one or more violations.

DEFAULT_REMEDIATION:
- PASS: record PASS; clear quarantine if clean-pass conditions are met.
- WARN: record WARN; proceed.
- FAIL: record FAIL; engage quarantine when dominant violation maps to QUARANTINE_NEXT_CYCLE.

=== SECTION: QUARANTINE_POLICY ===

ENGAGEMENT:
- COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION proposes quarantine on FAIL when dominant violation is QUARANTINE_NEXT_CYCLE.
- STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS commits or suppresses the proposal.
- Quarantine persists in STATE_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME.

SCOPE_DECLARATION:
- Quarantine is a whole-cycle penalty band, not a per-domain hold.
- A single QUARANTINE_NEXT_CYCLE violation suspends ordinary character-cognition commitment for the following cycle.
- This blast radius is intentional: it forces a clean corrective cycle before ordinary state evolution resumes.

EFFECT_WHEN_ACTIVE:
- PRIMARY_PASS admits only GOVERNANCE_CORRECTIVE proposals.
- Others are suppressed with QUARANTINE_ACTIVE.

CLEARING:
- Clears on clean PASS or structurally sufficient GOVERNANCE_CORRECTIVE resolution.
- Clearing mechanics follow DYNAMICS_GOVERNANCE_RUNTIME.QUARANTINE_CLEAR, including the same-cycle guard that applies to every clear path.

REASON_CODE_CAP:
- QUARANTINE_REASON_CODES cap: 5.
- Overflow FIFO-evicts oldest reason code.

=== SECTION: READ_BOUNDARY_POLICY ===

CHARACTER_COGNITION_BAND:
- PRIORITY_01 through PRIORITY_15

GOVERNANCE_BAND:
- PRIORITY_16 through PRIORITY_18

RULES:
- CHARACTER_VISIBLE modules and subfields may be read by character-cognition COTs.
- GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL modules and subfields may be read only by governance-band authorities.
- Character-cognition COTs must not read governance internals.
- A lawful character-cognition append to an append-only GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL subfield (CASCADE_TRACE) is not a read per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.WRITE_VERSUS_READ_NOTE; only a read-back of GOVERNANCE_INTERNAL content is a violation.
- Canonical-contract conformance is compiled into character-cognition templates at authoring time per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.READ_DERIVATION_RULE and is not a runtime read.
- Violations are detected by COT_UPDATE_VERIFICATION through trace inspection.
