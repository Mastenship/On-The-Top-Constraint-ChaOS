=== SECTION: COT_TEMPORAL_FLOW_PROCESSING ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Processes passage of time, circadian alignment, fatigue accumulation, routine pressure, and subjective time perception. Generates temporal proposals, temporary overlays, and time-driven impulses. COT_TEMPORAL_DECAY_RESOLUTION responds to major time skips from the same narrative cue.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS proposals, HUNGER and THIRST drive proposals, sustenance-related STATE_BODY proposals, temporary overlays, DECISION impulses.
Is Informed By: narrative time cues, SCHEME_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS, DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS, STATE_BODY, STATE_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS, DYNAMICS_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS, DYNAMICS_BODY, STATE_MEMORY, context signals.

  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS][FATIGUE_ACCUMULATION][ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT]

  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: sleep-debt recovery cue
- FATIGUE_DEBT counts as one coupled ordinary movement only if both fields move one step in the same direction on the same event per STATE_GOVERNANCE_OVERLAY.COUPLED_MOVEMENT_REGISTRY.
- Exertion-only fatigue, ordinary stress, a short rest without meaningful sleep-debt reduction, or a second wind does not automatically move ACCUMULATED_SLEEP_DEBT. Do not form a spurious coupled set.
- If a field is already at its bound, omit that member instead of emitting a no-op or claiming coupling.

=== SECTION: SUSTENANCE_PROCESSING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT executes DYNAMICS_GOALS_MOTIVATIONS.DRIVE_ESCALATION.ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_DEPRIVATION and DRIVE_RELIEF.ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_NEED_MET for HUNGER and THIRST, DYNAMICS_BODY.STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS.ON_MALNUTRITION_OR_DEHYDRATION, and DYNAMICS_BODY.HEALTH_RECOVERY for METABOLIC_HEALTH and ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE. Food and water count only when established by the Dungeon Master's narration.

ON_TIME_WITHOUT_FOOD_OR_WATER:
- Propose HUNGER or THIRST one step more severe per ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_DEPRIVATION.
- When deprivation is prolonged or severe, propose METABOLIC_HEALTH or ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE decline per ON_MALNUTRITION_OR_DEHYDRATION.

ON_MEAL_OR_DRINK:
- Propose HUNGER or THIRST one step less severe per ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_NEED_MET.
- Where METABOLIC_HEALTH or ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE is below baseline, propose recovery per HEALTH_RECOVERY.

ON_EXTENDED_REST:
- Check DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.EXTENDED_REST.SUSTENANCE_GATE before claiming EXTENDED_REST for any field.
- With food and water during the rest, SET HUNGER and THIRST to its endpoints with its proof fields.
- Without them, apply ON_TIME_WITHOUT_FOOD_OR_WATER instead.
