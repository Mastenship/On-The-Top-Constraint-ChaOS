=== SECTION: COT_TEMPORAL_FLOW_PROCESSING ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT processes passage of time cues, circadian alignment, fatigue accumulation, routine pressure, and subjective time perception. It generates temporal proposals, temporary overlays, time-driven impulses, and decay trigger signal via proposals/flags when major skips occur.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- [STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS] proposals
- temporary overlay proposals
- DECISION synthesis impulses
Is Informed By:
- narrative time cues
- SCHEME_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS and DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS
- [STATE_BODY] and context signals

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when at least one of the following conditions is present in the current cycle.

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input describes elapsed time, time-of-day shift, deadline, schedule disruption, or rest event.
- Current STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS values place the cycle inside or near a peak window, energy dip window, or routine boundary.
- Active modifier targets a temporal EFFECT_FAMILY (PEAK_ALERTNESS, FATIGUE_PENALTY, DISORIENTATION).

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not generate impulses or non-trace proposals.
