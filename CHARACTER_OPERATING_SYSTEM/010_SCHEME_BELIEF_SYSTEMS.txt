=== SECTION: CONTRADICTION_MATRIX ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Two belief fields are contradictory when their values jointly imply mutually exclusive worldview commitments. Contradictions trigger BELIEF_DISSONANCE overlays per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS unless one position is at WEAK or REJECTED band.

CANONICAL_CONTRADICTION_PAIRS:
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs SPIRITUAL_DUALISM at STRONG
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs MAGICAL_THINKING at PRESENT
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs RELIGIOUS_TRANSCENDENCE at STRONG
- DETERMINISM at HARD vs LIBERTARIAN_FREE_WILL implied by AGENCY_BELIEF at HIGH
- DETERMINISM at HARD vs LOCUS_OF_CONTROL at INTERNAL
- JUST_WORLD_BELIEF at STRONG vs HUMAN_NATURE at INHERENTLY_EVIL
- UNIVERSAL_ETHICS at STRONG vs RELATIVISM at STRONG
- EMPIRICISM at STRONG vs FAITH_BASED at STRONG
- SKEPTICISM at HYPER vs FAITH_BASED at STRONG
- NIHILISM_EXISTENTIALISM at STRONG vs RELIGIOUS_TRANSCENDENCE at STRONG
- BINARY_THINKING at BLACK_WHITE vs DIALECTICAL_THINKING at FULL_SPECTRUM

CONTRADICTION_RESOLUTION:
- Coexisting strong contradictions are lawful only when supported by explicit narrative compartmentalization or active BELIEF_DISSONANCE overlay.
- Persistent unresolved contradiction across cycles eventually triggers persistence-cluster accommodation per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.
- The verifier inspects strong-strong contradictions in committed STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS values; presence without overlay or persistence justification surfaces as SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.
