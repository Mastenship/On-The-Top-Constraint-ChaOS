BINARY_DIALECTICAL_REPRESENTATION_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: BINARY_THINKING and DIALECTICAL_THINKING are intentionally retained as two independent ordinal fields rather than one field, so that mixed-mode cognition (for example concrete in moral reasoning yet dialectical in social reasoning) is representable.
- The representation is deliberately denormalized.
- The only incoherent corner (BINARY_THINKING at BLACK_WHITE jointly with DIALECTICAL_THINKING at FULL_SPECTRUM) is policed by the CONTRADICTION_MATRIX as a standing invariant obligation.
- Authors must not collapse these into a single field; downstream contradiction handling depends on both being present.

=== SECTION: CONTRADICTION_MATRIX ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Two belief fields are contradictory when their values jointly reach the matrix-specified contradiction band for that pair, implying mutually exclusive worldview commitments. A contradiction triggers BELIEF_DISSONANCE overlays per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS unless at least one field is below its specified contradiction band.

CANONICAL_CONTRADICTION_PAIRS:
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs SPIRITUAL_DUALISM at STRONG
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs MAGICAL_THINKING at PRESENT
- PHYSICAL_MATERIALISM at STRONG vs RELIGIOUS_TRANSCENDENCE at STRONG
- DETERMINISM at HARD vs AGENCY_BELIEF at HIGH
- DETERMINISM at HARD vs LOCUS_OF_CONTROL at INTERNAL
- JUST_WORLD_BELIEF at STRONG vs HUMAN_NATURE at INHERENTLY_EVIL
- UNIVERSAL_ETHICS at STRONG vs RELATIVISM at STRONG
- EMPIRICISM at STRONG vs FAITH_BASED at STRONG
- SKEPTICISM at HYPER vs FAITH_BASED at STRONG
- NIHILISM_EXISTENTIALISM at STRONG vs RELIGIOUS_TRANSCENDENCE at STRONG
- BINARY_THINKING at BLACK_WHITE vs DIALECTICAL_THINKING at FULL_SPECTRUM

CONTRADICTION_BAND_RULE:
- Each pair above names the exact band on each side at which the contradiction holds; these are the pair's contradiction bands.
- A pair is in active contradiction only when both fields are at their named contradiction band.
- If either field is below its named contradiction band (for ordinal fields, any value short of the named band; for categorical fields, any value other than the named value), the pair is not in active contradiction and requires no marker.

=== SECTION: CONTRADICTION_MARKER_REQUIREMENT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: A coexisting matrix-band contradiction (both fields at their named contradiction band) is lawful only when a structured contradiction marker is present in committed state. Prose assertion of compartmentalization is not a lawful marker.

LAWFUL_CONTRADICTION_MARKERS:
- An active STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS record with EFFECT_FAMILY BELIEF_DISSONANCE targeting the contradicted belief domain.
- An active STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS record with EFFECT_FAMILY WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE targeting the contradicted belief domain.
- A persistence-cluster accommodation in progress for one of the contradicted fields per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.

MARKER_RESOLUTION:
- A matrix-band contradiction without one lawful marker surfaces as SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.
- A pair with at least one field below its named contradiction band does not require a marker.
- The verifier inspects committed STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS values against active markers in STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.

=== SECTION: INVARIANT_EVALUATION_TIMING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Contradiction-matrix invariants are cross-field invariants.

TIMING_RULE:
- Matrix-band contradiction invariants evaluate against projected committed state after conflict reconciliation, not per isolated proposal.
- A proposal set producing a matrix-band contradiction jointly is evaluated jointly at STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT reconciliation.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Beliefs are stability-biased; baseline shifts require persistence-cluster evidence or watershed cues.
- Ordinal belief fields obey one-step movement against declared ladders unless override proof is complete.
- Matrix-band contradictions per CONTRADICTION_MATRIX require a lawful contradiction marker.
- BINARY_THINKING and DIALECTICAL_THINKING remain independent fields per BINARY_DIALECTICAL_REPRESENTATION_NOTE.
- Update-trigger profile must remain coherent with agency and control stance.
