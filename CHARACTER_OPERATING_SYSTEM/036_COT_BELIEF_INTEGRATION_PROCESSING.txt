[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Processes how beliefs filter perception, constrain options, and generate meaning. Prefers temporary dissonance and worldview-defense overlays over baseline rewrites unless persistence-cluster or watershed conditions are met per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

Is Informed By: STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS, STATE_MEMORY, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS, DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS, narrative input.

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input aligns with or contradicts a core belief field.
- Active BELIEF_DISSONANCE or WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE modifier exists.
- Persistence cluster exists and current cycle adds consistent cue evidence.
- Watershed or existential cue is present.
- Memory or DPF cue raises belief-relevant contradiction, meaning, justice, or worldview pressure.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM only, per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.

=== SECTION: BELIEF_FILTERING_AND_ALIGNMENT ===

ON_CUE_ALIGNING_WITH_CORE_BELIEF:
- Reinforce worldview-consistent interpretation.
- No baseline mutation.

ON_CUE_CONTRADICTING_CORE_BELIEF:
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:

    TARGET_FIELD: DOMAIN_GLOBAL:COGNITION
    EFFECT_EXPRESSION: dissonance pressure from cue contradicting core belief
    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
    DURATION_TYPE: UNTIL_CONDITION
    EXPIRY_CONDITION: contradiction resolves or accommodation occurs
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: BELIEF_CONTRADICTION_DISSONANCE
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: contradiction to core belief

ON_ACTIVE_DEFENSE_OF_THREATENED_CORE_BELIEF:
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:
    MODIFIER_TYPE: COGNITIVE_MODIFIER
    EFFECT_FAMILY: WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE
    TARGET_FIELD: DOMAIN_GLOBAL:COGNITION

    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE

  STRENGTH: MODERATE
