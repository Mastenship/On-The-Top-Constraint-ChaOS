=== SECTION: COT_BELIEF_INTEGRATION_PROCESSING ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
This COT processes how beliefs filter perception, constrain options, and generate meaning. It prefers temporary dissonance overlays over baseline rewrites unless persistence cluster conditions or watershed-shift conditions are met per DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs:
- DECISION constraints
- memory meaning-attribution cues
- stress framing
Is Informed By:
- [STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS]
- [STATE_MEMORY]
- [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS]
- SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS and DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS
- narrative input

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: WRITE_POLICY_OVERRIDE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: No direct persistent writes, including belief baselines.
All persistent intent must be proposal-only in [COGNITIVE_SCRATCHPAD][PROPOSED_UPDATES_REGISTRY].

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT generates impulses and non-trace proposals only when at least one of the following conditions is present in the current cycle.

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input aligns with or contradicts a core belief field in STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.
- An active BELIEF_DISSONANCE or worldview-defense modifier is present in STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.
- A persistence cluster exists for a belief field and the current cycle adds new consistent cue evidence.
- A watershed or existential cue is present in the narrative input.
- A memory or DPF cue raises belief-relevant contradiction, meaning, justice, or worldview pressure.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- Emit EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM trace entry per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
- Do not propose dissonance overlays, belief transitions, or meaning-oriented impulses.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: BELIEF_FILTERING_AND_ALIGNMENT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Filter cues through belief structure.

ON_CUE_ALIGNING_WITH_CORE_BELIEF:
- Reinforce worldview-consistent interpretation.
- No baseline mutation; interpretive reinforcement only.

ON_CUE_CONTRADICTING_CORE_BELIEF:
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:
    MODIFIER_TYPE: COGNITIVE_MODIFIER
    EFFECT_FAMILY: BELIEF_DISSONANCE
    EFFECT_EXPRESSION: dissonance pressure from cue contradicting core belief
    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
    DURATION_TYPE: UNTIL_CONDITION
    EXPIRY_CONDITION: contradiction resolves or accommodation occurs
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: BELIEF_CONTRADICTION_DISSONANCE
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: contradiction to core belief

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: PERSISTENCE_TRIGGERED_ACCOMMODATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Persistence-triggered baseline shift follows DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE.

ON_PERSISTENCE_CLUSTER_PLUS_NEW_CUE:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BELIEF_SYSTEMS][<canonical belief field path>]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: adjacent lawful enum value in consistent cue direction
  STRENGTH: HIGH
  MECHANISM_TAG: BELIEF_PERSISTENCE_ACCOMMODATION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: COMMITTED_FACT
  EVIDENCE_REF: [MEM_XX, MEM_XX, MEM_XX] (three or more contributing memory records)
  INVOKED_RULE_NAME: PERSISTENT_EVIDENCE_ACCUMULATION
  NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: cycle cue token plus contributing MEM_XX references
  SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: belief baseline shift accommodated under accumulated evidence; one adjacent enum step
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: persistence cluster plus new consistent cue justifies bounded accommodation

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: WATERSHED_AND_CRISIS_PATHWAYS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Single-event multi-axis or watershed shifts require explicit override pathways per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.INVOKED_RULE_NAME_ENUM.

ON_WATERSHED_TRIGGER:
- Propose TRANSITION or SET on specific belief fields with INVOKED_RULE_NAME WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT and full structural override proof.

ON_EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_TRIGGER:
- Propose multi-field SET only with INVOKED_RULE_NAME EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE and full structural override proof.
