Propagates operational effects and independently supported psychological responses through physiological, psychological, and skill systems.

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

ANCHOR_CUES:
- One or more ALT_XX records have STATUS = ACTIVE.
- Alteration operational status, psychological stance, or awareness has a change cue this cycle.
- Narrative input establishes a newly acquired alteration or references alteration effect, reactivation, removal, rejection, or constraint change.
- Narrative input describes an injury, disease, or other cause of pain that will still affect the character beyond the moment.
- An ACTIVE alteration, an aware REJECTING stance with stress cues, or an attributable alteration modifier requires cascade/expiry evaluation.
- An eligible [STATE_MEMORY][DEFERRED_NARRATIVE_CUES] entry names this COT as SOURCE_COT, per SCHEME_MEMORY.DEFERRED_NARRATIVE_CUE_QUEUE.COT_RECONSIDERATION.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM only, per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.

=== SECTION: NEW_ALTERATION_ONBOARDING ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT executes DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.NEW_ALTERATION_ADMISSION and produces the complete linked record pair.

ON_NEW_DURABLE_ALTERATION_CUE:
- Run NEW_ALTERATION_GATE before proposing ALT_XX creation.
- If it passes, PROPOSE SET pair:
  - TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALTERATION_REGISTRY]; PROPOSED_VALUE: append one provisional ALT_XX
  - TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX]; PROPOSED_VALUE: full SCHEME_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_RECORD_SCHEMA including alteration type, duration, independent operational status, physical/ability/constraint details, and CHARACTER_AWARENESS with separate awareness and psychological stance.
- MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE; EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE; JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION.
- If admission fails, prefer the appropriate BODY, SKILLS, or TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS mechanics; do not invent a persistent alteration record.
- Any same-cycle cascade depends on surviving ALT_XX creation and its specific projected supporting fields; failed creation suppresses all dependents, while a failed separate stance change does not invalidate a lawful creation pair.
- Only the governance writer resolves the final ALT_XX identifier.

=== SECTION: ALTERATION_LIFECYCLE_EXECUTION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This COT executes DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ACTIVATION_DEACTIVATION, PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE, and PSYCHOLOGICAL_REJECTION. It is the runtime proposer for alteration status, stance, and awareness; instantiation seeding remains with COT_CHARACTER_INSTANTIATION_PROCESS. Never write persistent state directly.

ON_STATUS_CUE:
- Resolve one explicit operational STATUS endpoint from SCHEME_ALTERATIONS.
- Apply DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ACTIVATION_DEACTIVATION.OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_GATE.
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][STATUS]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: one cue-supported lawful categorical endpoint
  MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: explicit activation, deactivation, reactivation, or removal cue
- Do not infer removal from temporary inhibition or operational inactivity from psychological rejection/denial.
- When cascade effects depend on a proposed status transition in this cycle, bind them to the same ALT_XX and evaluate against its projected lawful status. If the status SET is suppressed, its dependent activation/deactivation cascade proposals must not commit as if it succeeded.
- When STATUS ceases to be ACTIVE, evaluate removal of attributable functional overlays only. Physiological reversal needs independent recovery evidence; psychological stress follows its own supporting fields and cues.
- Existing ACTIVE alterations may still produce lawful ongoing effects without proposing a duplicate status change.

ON_STANCE_OR_AWARENESS_CUE:
- Apply DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE and PSYCHOLOGICAL_REJECTION gates.
- Emit separate SET packets for supported changes to:
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][CHARACTER_AWARENESS][PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE]
  - [STATE_ALTERATIONS][ALT_XX][CHARACTER_AWARENESS][AWARENESS_STATUS]
- Each packet resolves one owning SCHEME enum endpoint, with MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_PSYCHOLOGICAL_RESPONSE; EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE; JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION; JUSTIFICATION: explicit attitude or awareness cue.
- Neither packet implies a STATUS change. If both operational and psychological changes have evidence, propose them separately with separate justifications.

CASCADE_SOURCE_LINKAGE:
- Record source ALT_XX, supporting canonical field paths, and any provisional source PROPOSAL_IDs in NOTES for every cascade packet, including refresh/removal. Name the concrete source and field conditions in modifier JUSTIFICATION and EXPIRY_CONDITION; keep TARGET_ID empty for DOMAIN_GLOBAL modifiers under SCHEME_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.
- Dependents of suppressed source changes cannot commit as if those changes succeeded; independently supported effects remain eligible under the surviving committed fields.
- Apply REMOVE_MODIFIER proposals to concrete attributable MOD_XX records per DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS; do not blanket-remove alteration effects or erase persistent consequences.

=== SECTION: PHYSIOLOGICAL_PROPAGATION ===

ON_INJURY_OR_ILLNESS_TYPE:
- Require operational eligibility from DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.CASCADE_EFFECT_CLASS_INFERENCE, irrespective of stance or awareness.
- Apply DYNAMICS_BODY.STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS and SCHEME_BODY.PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH ordinal law before writing.
- When the alteration records a wound narrated this cycle, that cycle's immune effect belongs to COT_STRESS_CASCADE_RESOLUTION; do not propose it again here.
- Only when an active alteration's injury/illness class actually supports deterioration:
  - If IMMUNE_MARKERS is ROBUST: PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_UP to NORMAL.
  - If IMMUNE_MARKERS is NORMAL: PROPOSE TRANSITION ONE_STEP_UP to COMPROMISED.
  - If IMMUNE_MARKERS is COMPROMISED or SUPPRESSED: do not propose this injury-related immune worsening solely to reach COMPROMISED.
- Concrete transition packet:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][IMMUNE_MARKERS]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  OPERATION_TYPE: TRANSITION
  STRENGTH: HIGH
  MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_PHYSIOLOGICAL_PROPAGATION
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: one-step immune deterioration supported by active alteration
- A ROBUST-to-COMPROMISED jump is two steps. It requires DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.ALTERATION_ACTIVATION_OR_FAILURE: SET IMMUNE_MARKERS to COMPROMISED with its proof fields. A severe description alone does not meet its grant condition.
- Lasting skill loss from an alteration belongs to COT_PERFORMANCE_STATE_INTEGRATION.
- No change when alteration is no longer active; reversal to a healthier immune band needs independent recovery evidence under DYNAMICS_BODY and may not be inferred from the mere removal of an alteration.
- This is distinct from governance's IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION one-band forced correction.

ON_ENHANCEMENT_TYPE:
- Require STATUS = ACTIVE; psychological rejection or denial does not inhibit the enhancement.
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:

    EFFECT_FAMILY: ALTERATION_SKILL_ENHANCEMENT
    TARGET_FIELD: DOMAIN_GLOBAL:SKILL_EXECUTION
    EFFECT_EXPRESSION: bounded skill enhancement from active alteration
    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
    DURATION_TYPE: UNTIL_CONDITION
    EXPIRY_CONDITION: named source ALT_XX STATUS is no longer ACTIVE
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: ALTERATION_ENHANCEMENT_OVERLAY
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: active enhancement effect

ON_DEBILITATING_TYPE:
- Require STATUS = ACTIVE; psychological rejection or denial does not inhibit the constraint.
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:
    MODIFIER_TYPE: PERFORMANCE_MODIFIER
    EFFECT_FAMILY: ALTERATION_SKILL_DEGRADATION

=== SECTION: PSYCHOLOGICAL_PROPAGATION ===

ON_REJECTING_STANCE:
- Require DYNAMICS_ALTERATIONS.REJECTION_GATE: projected PSYCHOLOGICAL_STANCE = REJECTING, AWARENESS_STATUS other than UNAWARE, and supporting stress cues. Operational STATUS is independent.
- PROPOSE ADD_MODIFIER:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS][MOD_XX]
  PROPOSED_VALUE:
    MODIFIER_TYPE: PSYCHOLOGICAL_MODIFIER
    EFFECT_FAMILY: ALTERATION_REJECTION_STRESS
    TARGET_FIELD: DOMAIN_GLOBAL:AFFECT
    EFFECT_EXPRESSION: bounded stress overlay from psychological rejection of alteration
    MAGNITUDE: MODERATE
    STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
    DURATION_TYPE: PERSISTENT_UNTIL_REFRESH
    EXPIRY_CONDITION: named source ALT_XX stance is no longer REJECTING, awareness is UNAWARE, or supporting rejection-stress cues resolve
  STRENGTH: MODERATE
  MECHANISM_TAG: REJECTION_STRESS_OVERLAY
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  JUSTIFICATION: source alteration's rejecting stance, awareness, and explicit stress cue
- When this rejection condition resolves, propose removal of attributable rejection-stress modifiers; preserve independently lawful functional overlays. Operational dormancy or removal alone does not prove psychological recovery.
