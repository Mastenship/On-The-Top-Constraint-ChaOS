=== SECTION: CROSS_SCHEME_TRAIT_PRECEDENCE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Applies system-wide when HEXACO, DARK_TRIAD, MMPI, or ASQ biases conflict or compound.

PRECEDENCE_ORDER:
1) MMPI SIGNIFICANT or CLINICAL
2) DARK_TRIAD VERY_HIGH
3) ASQ ATTACHMENT_SECURITY VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH
4) HEXACO VERY_LOW or VERY_HIGH
5) MMPI MODERATE
6) DARK_TRIAD HIGH
7) HEXACO HIGH or LOW
8) all other lawful trait bands

CONFLICT_RESOLUTION:
- Same tier, opposite direction: stronger band wins.
- CLINICAL beats SIGNIFICANT.
- VERY_HIGH beats HIGH.
- If still tied: neither bias applies; use lower-tier influences and cues.

COMPOUNDING_RULE:
- Same-direction biases do not stack additively.
- Highest-precedence applicable bias determines magnitude.
- Lower-tier same-direction biases may support interpretation, not extra magnitude.
- Additive trait stacking that creates covert magnitude inflation is unlawful.

VERIFIER_NOTE:
- Trait-modulated proposals cite the dominant trait in JUSTIFICATION.
- Additive trait stacking may be downgraded or suppressed.

=== SECTION: CARDIOVASCULAR_SNAPSHOT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: HEART_RATE, BLOOD_PRESSURE, and RESPIRATORY_RATE are PERCEPTUAL_SNAPSHOT categorical fields per SCHEME_BODY. They are perceptual readouts of current arousal and biochemical state, written by SET to any lawful endpoint, and exempt from one-step limits. They carry no durable identity meaning and require no persistence evidence.

ON_ACUTE_AROUSAL_OR_ADRENALINE_RISE:
- When ADRENALINE moves to HEIGHTENED or higher, or an acute-threat cue is present:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][HEART_RATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ELEVATED | TACHYCARDIC
    MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: acute arousal cardiovascular readout
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][RESPIRATORY_RATE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ELEVATED | TACHYPNEA
    MECHANISM_TAG: SYMPATHETIC_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: acute arousal respiratory readout
  - Resolve the pipe alternative to one concrete endpoint by cue intensity before scratchpad entry.

ON_SUSTAINED_CORTISOL_ELEVATION:
- When CORTISOL is SPIKE or higher:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][CARDIOVASCULAR][BLOOD_PRESSURE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: HYPERTENSION
    MECHANISM_TAG: CORTISOL_CARDIOVASCULAR_READOUT
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
    JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
    JUSTIFICATION: sustained cortisol cardiovascular readout

ON_RECOVERY_OR_BASELINE_RETURN:
- When ADRENALINE and CORTISOL are at or returning toward baseline and no acute cue remains:
  - PROPOSE SET cardiovascular fields toward NORMAL endpoints:
    - HEART_RATE: NORMAL
    - BLOOD_PRESSURE: NORMAL
    - RESPIRATORY_RATE: NORMAL
  - MECHANISM_TAG: CARDIOVASCULAR_BASELINE_RETURN
  - EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  - JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: DIRECT_APPLICATION
  - JUSTIFICATION: cardiovascular readout returns to baseline

SNAPSHOT_NOTE:
- Cardiovascular snapshots track biochemical state; they do not independently drift and are not subject to cumulative-ordinal counting because they are not ordinal.
- LOW and the clinical poles (HYPOTENSION, BRADYPNEA) are reached by SET only under explicit matching cue.

=== SECTION: STRESS_INJURY_ILLNESS ===

ON_SIGNIFICANT_INJURY_OR_ILLNESS:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][PHYSIOLOGICAL_HEALTH][IMMUNE_MARKERS]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: COMPROMISED
  JUSTIFICATION: injury or illness cue
- IMMUNE_MARKERS is ordinal per SCHEME_BODY; a move from ROBUST to COMPROMISED is multi-step and requires override proof. From NORMAL, COMPROMISED is one lawful step.

ON_SUBSTANCE_USE_EVENT:
- If NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE = BALANCED:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: STRAINED
    JUSTIFICATION: substance perturbation cue
- If NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE = STRAINED:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_BODY][BIOCHEMICAL_STATE_MARKERS][NEUROCHEMICAL_BALANCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: DYSREGULATED
    JUSTIFICATION: substance perturbation cue
- Stronger deterioration requires override proof or governance downgrade/suppression.

ON_MALNUTRITION_OR_DEHYDRATION:
- METABOLIC_HEALTH may move one lawful step toward FAILING.
- ELECTROLYTE_BALANCE may move one lawful step toward CRITICAL.
- Stronger deterioration requires override proof or governance downgrade/suppression.

IMMUNE_TERMINAL_RECONCILIATION_NOTE:
[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: When two or more terminal biochemical states project concurrently with IMMUNE_MARKERS = ROBUST, governance applies IMMUNE_TERMINAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION: terminal-state transitions commit and IMMUNE_MARKERS is force-corrected one band off ROBUST toward NORMAL. DYNAMICS does not need to emit a separate immune-demotion proposal for this; the bounded correction is governance-side. DYNAMICS may still emit an explicit injury/illness IMMUNE_MARKERS proposal where a direct cue exists.

- Dynamics may describe degradation conceptually; proposals must resolve to concrete lawful values.
