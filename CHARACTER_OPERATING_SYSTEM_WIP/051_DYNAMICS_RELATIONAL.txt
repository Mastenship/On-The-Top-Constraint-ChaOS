=== SECTION: RELATIONAL_UPDATE_THRESHOLD_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Persistent relational valence movement is conservative. Minor social texture usually produces no proposal or a temporary modifier.

MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE:
Persistent valence movement requires at least one:
- Trust, safety, attachment, autonomy, status, intimacy, loyalty, betrayal, protection, humiliation, abandonment, or dependence is affected.
- Existing CURRENT_CONCERN is confirmed or contradicted.
- Prior pattern repeats with accumulated relational significance.
- Cue links to memory candidate, goal pressure, DPF prediction, or belief relevance.
- Character's willingness to approach, avoid, rely on, protect, confront, or disclose changes.

NON_MOVEMENT_CASES:
- Ordinary politeness.
- Minor pleasantness.
- Background friendliness without consequence.
- Incidental proximity without meaningful interaction.
- Generic hostility adding no new relational information.
- Single ambiguous cue better represented as PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE inference or modifier.

=== SECTION: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE moves on the character's own emotional response.

ON_MEANINGFUL_POSITIVE_INTERACTION:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION: meaningful positive emotional response

ON_MEANINGFUL_NEGATIVE_INTERACTION:
- If MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE is met:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][AFFECTIVE_VALENCE]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    EPISTEMIC_TAG: PERCEPTION
    JUSTIFICATION: meaningful negative emotional response

ON_BETRAYAL_OR_HARM_EVENT:
- Multi-step downward movement requires full override proof.

ALLY_PROXIMITY_NOTE:
- Ally proximity alone ordinarily produces temporary comfort/co-regulation overlay.
- Durable valence movement requires meaningful relational action.

=== SECTION: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE moves on observed or inferred entity affect, not the character's own feelings.

EVIDENCE_TYPES:
- Observed warmth, hostility, withdrawal, approach, aid, threat, or avoidance.
- Disclosed entity statements.
- Inferred entity affect from expression, proxemics, or pattern.

ON_POSITIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: positive entity behavior implies warmer perceived-other valence

ON_NEGATIVE_ENTITY_BEHAVIOR_TOWARD_CHARACTER:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
  EPISTEMIC_TAG: INFERENCE
  JUSTIFICATION_CATEGORY: INFERENTIAL_LEAP
  JUSTIFICATION: negative entity behavior implies colder perceived-other valence

ON_DISCLOSED_ENTITY_STATEMENT:
- Disclosure may establish the statement occurred.
- Entity internal state remains INFERENCE unless substrate grants direct access.
- Use COMMITTED_FACT only for the disclosure event, with EVIDENCE_REF.

ASYMMETRY_NOTE:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE and PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE may lawfully diverge.

=== SECTION: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY_DYNAMICS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY moves on accumulated interaction depth, independent of valence.

EVIDENCE_FOR_UPWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Multiple distinct contexts.
- Contradiction of prior simple categorization.
- Direct disclosure of history, motive, or internal state.
- Sustained varied interaction.
- Discovery of more complex motives, constraints, loyalties, fears, or values.

EVIDENCE_FOR_DOWNWARD_MOVEMENT:
- Explicit forced recategorization.
- Evidence prior complexity was mistaken projection.
- Prolonged absence is usually handled through memory decay.

ON_DEPTH_EVIDENCE:
- PROPOSE TRANSITION:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
  JUSTIFICATION: accumulated interaction depth

ON_FORCED_RECATEGORIZATION:
- PROPOSE SET:
  TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_RELATIONAL][ENT_XX][COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY]
  PROPOSED_VALUE: lawful lower band
  JUSTIFICATION: forced recategorization cue
- Lowering usually requires explicit cue and override proof.

DECOUPLING_RULE:
- Movement on AFFECTIVE_VALENCE, PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE, and COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY requires independent justification.

=== SECTION: NEW_ENTITY_ENCOUNTER ===

SALIENT_SOCIAL_ENCOUNTER_GATE:
A new ENT_XX is warranted when at least one is true:
- Entity likely recurs.
- Entity affects goal, memory, prediction, safety, attachment, belief, or alteration.
- Entity is named, role-significant, relationally charged, threatening, protective, or consequential.
- Entity becomes target of action, concern, trust, hostility, dependence, or curiosity.

ON_SALIENT_SOCIAL_ENCOUNTER:
- Instantiate ENT_XX via SET pair only if gate is met.

DEFAULT_NEW_ENTITY_VALUES:

- COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY: ONE_DIMENSIONAL

- CURRENT_CONCERN: derived from cue or NONE

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- The three relational ladders have independent evidence types.
- One-step law applies; multi-step shifts require override proof.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE is inference-based by default.
- Persistent AFFECTIVE_VALENCE movement requires MEANINGFUL_INTERACTION_GATE.
- Ally proximity alone produces overlay effects, not durable valence movement.
