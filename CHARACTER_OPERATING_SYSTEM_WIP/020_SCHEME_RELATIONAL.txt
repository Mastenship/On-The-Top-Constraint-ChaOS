- No decay or toward-baseline directive may lower this field; the BASELINE_ANCHOR is declared only to satisfy ladder formality and must not be used as a drift target.

=== SECTION: DIRECTIONALITY_RULE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: AFFECTIVE_VALENCE and PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE are independent directional fields that must not be conflated.

DIRECTIONALITY_DEFINITIONS:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE: how the character feels toward the entity. First-person directional. Always character-resolvable from internal state.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE: how the character perceives the entity feels toward the character. Inference-based directional. Always carries EPISTEMIC_TAG INFERENCE or SPECULATION when proposed.

INDEPENDENCE_RULE:
- The two valences may differ at any band combination.
- Asymmetry is lawful and frequently realistic (unrequited devotion, unreciprocated hostility, mutual misperception).
- Proposals shifting one valence do not implicitly shift the other; each shift requires its own cue support.

EVIDENCE_TYPES:
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE moves on the character's own emotional response to interaction events.
- PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE moves on observed entity behavior, disclosed entity statements, or inferred entity affect.

BASELINE_ANCHOR_NOTE:
- Both valence ladders anchor at NEUTRAL.
- ONE_STEP_TOWARD_BASELINE and ONE_STEP_TOWARD_NEUTRAL on either valence resolve toward NEUTRAL from either direction.
- COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY does not participate in toward-baseline movement per COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY_DIRECTION_NOTE.

- ENTITY_ID uses prefix ENT_.
- No duplicate ENTITY_ID.
- New entity insertion follows strict SET pair contract.

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- Ordinal transitions on both valence fields obey one-step policy unless override proof is valid.
- AFFECTIVE_VALENCE and PERCEIVED_OTHER_VALENCE move independently on independent evidence.
- COGNITIVE_COMPLEXITY moves on accumulated interaction depth, independent of either valence direction, and never drifts toward baseline.
- CURRENT_CONCERN should clear on resolution unless refreshed by new cues.
