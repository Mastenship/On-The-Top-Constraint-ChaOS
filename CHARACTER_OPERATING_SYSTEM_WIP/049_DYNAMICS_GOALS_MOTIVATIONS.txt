=== SECTION: DRIVE_ESCALATION ===

ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_DEPRIVATION:
- Propose SET to next more severe lawful drive value.
- Elapsed time is deprivation: a long rest or time skip without food established by narration moves HUNGER one step more severe, and without water moves THIRST one step more severe.
- Deprivation worsens one step per cycle; stronger proposals are downgraded or suppressed by governance.

ON_SAFETY_THREAT:
- Propose SET to next more severe lawful value in PHYSICAL_SAFETY and/or EMOTIONAL_SAFETY as cue type warrants.
- Sudden physical danger may SET PHYSICAL_SAFETY beyond one step under DYNAMICS_BODY.ACUTE_STRESS_RESPONSE.
- Betrayal, abandonment, or harm by someone the character cares about may SET EMOTIONAL_SAFETY beyond one step under DYNAMICS_RELATIONAL.ATTACHMENT_INJURY.
- Higher-order drive suppression requires explicit governance-readable proposals.

ON_PAIN_OR_EXPOSURE:
- A new injury, an illness, or an ACTIVE alteration that hurts moves PAIN_AVOIDANCE one step more severe. Exposure to cold or heat moves TEMPERATURE one step more severe.
- While ADRENALINE is PEAK, pain is blunted and PAIN_AVOIDANCE holds; it catches up one step per cycle once the surge ends.

ON_ATTACHMENT_EVENT:
- Positive cues may SET attachment drive fields less severe.
- Negative cues may SET attachment drive fields more severe.
- Minor social texture should not change durable drive fields unless attachment-relevant, repeated, or state-salient.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: DRIVE_RELIEF ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Every drive that can escalate can also be relieved. Relief follows the specific need behind the drive being met in the story, one step less severe per cycle.

ON_PHYSIOLOGICAL_NEED_MET:
- A meal eases HUNGER; water eases THIRST; warmth or shelter eases TEMPERATURE; treatment or relief of pain eases PAIN_AVOIDANCE.
- Propose SET to the next less severe lawful drive value.
- A long rest with food and water may relieve HUNGER and THIRST further per DYNAMICS_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS.EXTENDED_REST.

ON_THREAT_PASSED:
- When the threat behind PHYSICAL_SAFETY has passed and the character is somewhere safe, propose SET to the next less severe value.
- EMOTIONAL_SAFETY eases only on reassurance, repair, or a lasting change in what threatened it, not on physical safety alone.

ON_PSYCHOLOGICAL_NEED_MET:
- Esteem, cognitive, and transcendence drives ease when their need is met in the story: success or mastery for COMPETENCE, regained freedom for AUTONOMY, respect for STATUS, discovery for CURIOSITY, understanding for MEANING_MAKING, progress for SELF_ACTUALIZATION, a secured legacy for LEGACY.
- Propose SET to the next less severe lawful value.

ON_GOAL_ACHIEVED:
- When a GOAL_XX reaches STATUS = ACHIEVED, the drive named in its DRIVE_SERVED may ease one step.

RELIEF_BOUNDS:
- One step per drive per cycle, except physiological relief under EXTENDED_REST.
- Rest and the passage of time never relieve psychological drives.
- Relief requires the need to be met in the narrative, not merely anticipated.

=== SECTION: GOAL_WORTHINESS_GATE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Not every desire, impulse, or immediate action intent becomes GOAL_XX.

GOAL_RECORD_ADMISSION_CRITERIA:
A new GOAL_XX is warranted only when all are true:
- Pressure is action-organizing beyond a single moment.
- No existing ACTIVE, BLOCKED, or DORMANT GOAL_XX captures it.
- Success criteria are describable.
- It serves drive, value, safety, attachment, competence, meaning, legacy, or self-actualization pressure.
- It can persist across cycles or structure multiple actions.

NON_ADMISSION_CASES:
- Passing wants.
- Single-turn tactical actions.
- Momentary impulses handled by COT_DECISION_SYNTHESIS.
- Duplicates of existing goals.
- Emotional pressure better represented as temporary modifier.
- Predictions better represented as PRD_XX.
- Memories better represented as MEM_XX.

ADMISSION_RESULT:
- If not admitted, express as impulse, selected action, modifier, or DPF interpretation.
- If admitted, use SET pair with schema-valid payload.

=== SECTION: GOAL_FORMATION ===

ON_HIGH_URGENCY_DRIVE:
- Form GOAL_XX only when GOAL_RECORD_ADMISSION_CRITERIA are met.
- Initial URGENCY and CONFIDENCE must cohere with drive intensity, resources, body state, and skills.

ON_OPPORTUNITY_OR_OBSTACLE:
- Prefer SET updates to existing GOAL_XX when lawful.
- Create new GOAL_XX only when existing records do not capture the pressure and admission criteria are met.

=== SECTION: CONFLICT_RESOLUTION ===

ON_GOAL_CONFLICT:
- Classify conflict.
- Propose bounded SET to URGENCY, STATUS, or CONFIDENCE when cue support exists.
- Use ADD_MODIFIER for short-horizon conflict pressure when more lawful than durable goal rewrite.

CONFLICT_TYPES:
- RESOURCE_CONFLICT
- VALUE_CONFLICT
- SAFETY_VS_DESIRE
- ATTACHMENT_VS_AUTONOMY
- SHORT_TERM_VS_LONG_TERM
- IDENTITY_VS_SURVIVAL
- TIME_PRESSURE_CONFLICT
