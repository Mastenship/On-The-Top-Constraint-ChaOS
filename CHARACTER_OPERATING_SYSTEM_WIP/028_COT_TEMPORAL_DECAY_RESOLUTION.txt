=== SECTION: COT_TEMPORAL_DECAY_RESOLUTION ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Governs slow decay, recovery, and weakening effects caused by time passage without strong reinforcement. Handles memory salience decay, prediction certainty decay, modifier expiry, and unresolved low-activation pressures.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: weakening proposals for persistent and temporary structures; low-activation impulses.
Is Informed By: STATE_MEMORY, STATE_DPF, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, STATE_TEMPORAL_DYNAMICS, elapsed-time cues, DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS.

=== SECTION: FIRES_WHEN ===

ANCHOR_CUES:
- Narrative input describes elapsed time, major time skip, scene closure, or sustained lack of reinforcement.
- Active modifier expiry condition is met or deterministically inferable.
- Unresolved prediction has gone unconfirmed across meaningful elapsed interval.
- Memory salience decay or reconsolidation cue is present.

DEFAULT_BEHAVIOR_WHEN_NO_ANCHOR:
- EMPTY_CYCLE_FORM only, per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY.NO_TRIGGER_BEHAVIOR_POLICY.
