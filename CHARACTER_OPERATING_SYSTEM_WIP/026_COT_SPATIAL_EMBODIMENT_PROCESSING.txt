=== SECTION: COT_SPATIAL_EMBODIMENT_PROCESSING ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Translates spatial position, proximity, and environmental features into embodied effects, stress modulation, relational pressure, and behavior impulses. Prefers temporary overlays for short-horizon spatial effects. Mere proximity does not create durable relational valence change.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

[KEEP: Relationship to Other Modules]
Informs: stress proposals, performance overlays, relational bias proposals, memory context cues.
Is Informed By: MAP_DATA, scene geometry, STATE_BODY, STATE_ALTERATIONS, STATE_RELATIONAL, STATE_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, SCHEME_RELATIONAL, DYNAMICS_RELATIONAL, DYNAMICS_TEMPORARY_MODIFIERS, narrative input.
