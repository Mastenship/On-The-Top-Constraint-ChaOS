=== SECTION: DYNAMICS_BELIEF_SYSTEMS ===

[KEEP: Purpose & Role]
Immutable mechanics for belief stability, dissonance handling, categorical belief transitions, ordinal belief drift, contradiction-marker emission, and rare baseline accommodation under persistence or override proof. Belief change is semantic, bounded, evidence-led, and resistant to impulsive baseline rewrite.

READ_VISIBILITY_CLASS: CHARACTER_VISIBLE

=== SECTION: LADDER_AUTHORITY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Ordinal belief fields and their step order are declared inline in SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS via ORDINAL [...] BASELINE_ANCHOR declarations within CORE_WORLDVIEW, ATTRIBUTION_STYLES, and COGNITIVE_BIASES, per CANONICAL_TOKEN_DICTIONARY.ORDINAL_LADDER_AUTHORITY. ORDINAL_FIELD_CLASS:
- Fields carrying an inline ORDINAL declaration in SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS are ordinal.
- Ordinal movement uses TRANSITION when direction is unambiguous, otherwise SET to explicit adjacent endpoint.
- One adjacent movement per field per cycle unless override proof is complete.

CATEGORICAL_FIELD_CLASS:
- SIMULATION_THEORY, MAGICAL_THINKING, LOCUS_OF_CONTROL, HUMAN_NATURE, SUCCESS_ATTRIBUTION, FAILURE_ATTRIBUTION are categorical.
- Categorical movement uses SET to explicit target and, when persistence-triggered, CATEGORICAL_FIELD_TRANSITION_MAPS.
- LOGIC_ARGUMENT VARIABLE is off-ladder per SCHEME; entry or exit uses SET only.

=== SECTION: BELIEF_STABILITY ===

GENERAL_RULES:
- Baseline belief shifts are rare.
- Isolated contradiction defaults to temporary modifier, not baseline rewrite.
- Repeated coherent evidence weighs more than isolated anomalous cue.
- Core worldview fields are identity-relevant and stability-biased.
- Matrix-band contradictions are lawful only with an active contradiction marker or persistence-cluster processing per SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.CONTRADICTION_MARKER_REQUIREMENT.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CONTRADICTION_MARKER_EMISSION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: A coexisting matrix-band contradiction (both fields at their named contradiction band per SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.CONTRADICTION_BAND_RULE) requires a structured marker in committed state. The executing COT supplies the complete modifier packet per UPDATE_CASCADE_REGISTRY; this module defines lawful marker mechanics only.

MARKER_FAMILIES:
- BELIEF_DISSONANCE: dissonance pressure from coexisting contradictory commitments at matrix band.
- WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE: protective rationalization sustaining a threatened core belief.

MARKER_MECHANICS:
- MODIFIER_TYPE: COGNITIVE_MODIFIER
- TARGET_FIELD: DOMAIN_GLOBAL:COGNITION
- STACKING_RULE: REPLACE
- DURATION_TYPE: UNTIL_CONDITION
- EXPIRY_CONDITION: contradiction resolves, one field drops below its named contradiction band, or accommodation completes

EMISSION_RULE:
- Matrix-band contradiction present or imminent emits BELIEF_DISSONANCE.
- Active defense of a threatened core belief emits WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE.
- A lawful marker is BELIEF_DISSONANCE, WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE, or an in-progress persistence-cluster accommodation.
- Absent a lawful marker, committed matrix-band contradiction surfaces as SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.

=== SECTION: PERSISTENCE_TRIGGER_RULE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Persistence is operationalized through the memory ledger.

PERSISTENCE_CLUSTER_EXISTS_FOR_FIELD_F_WHEN:
- Three or more MEM_XX records reference field F through SYSTEM_LINKAGE.SPECIFIC_FIELD_CHANGED or CORE_EVENT_SUMMARY.
- Records share consistent direction relative to F: all reinforcing or all challenging.
- At least one record has SALIENCE MODERATE or higher.
- Records are distinct cue occasions, not duplicate encodings.

PERSISTENCE_TRIGGERED_BASELINE_SHIFT:
- When cluster exists and current cycle adds consistent cue evidence:
  - ordinal fields: propose adjacent lawful value by SET or lawful TRANSITION when ladder direction is unambiguous
  - categorical fields: propose SET to lawful mapped target from CATEGORICAL_FIELD_TRANSITION_MAPS

REQUIRED_PROOF_FIELDS:
- INVOKED_RULE_NAME: PERSISTENT_EVIDENCE_ACCUMULATION
- NARRATIVE_CUE_REFERENCE: cycle cue token plus contributing MEM_XX references
- EVIDENCE_REF: [MEM_XX, MEM_XX, MEM_XX]
- SCHEME_INVARIANT_NOTE: accumulated evidence supports one adjacent enum step or lawful named categorical transition
- MECHANISM_TAG: BELIEF_PERSISTENCE_ACCOMMODATION

PERSISTENCE_BOUNDS:
- One adjacent movement per field per cycle.
- Multi-step shift requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT or EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE proof in addition to persistence evidence.
- A cluster's basis is consumed on commit; later shifts on the same field require fresh cue accumulation beyond the consumed basis per CONSUMED_BASIS_DEFINITION.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CONSUMED_BASIS_DEFINITION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: "Consumed basis" is scoped to the field, not to the memory and not to the transition. It is the set of MEM_XX cited in the EVIDENCE_REF of a committed PERSISTENT_EVIDENCE_ACCUMULATION shift on a given belief field.

ADMISSION_RULE:
- A later persistence shift on field F is admissible only when its qualifying cluster contains at least one MEM_XX outside every consumed basis recorded for F, and still independently satisfies PERSISTENCE_CLUSTER_EXISTS_FOR_FIELD_F_WHEN on its full membership.
- Re-citation of an already-consumed basis for F without fresh accumulation is not new evidence. A shift resting on it fails admission and surfaces as SCHEME_INVARIANT_VIOLATION.

FIELD_SCOPING:
- Consumption is field-scoped: a MEM_XX consumed for F remains lawful evidence for clusters on any other field. Reuse of one memory across distinct belief fields over a narrative arc is expected and preserved.

TRANSITION_SCOPING:
- Consumption is not transition-scoped: one basis may not fund a further band on F by re-citation. Multi-band movement on F requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT or EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE proof per PERSISTENCE_BOUNDS, never repeated use of one basis.

JUDGMENT_BOUNDARY:
- Whether a non-consumed contributor is substantive enough to carry a band is a cue judgment, not a form check.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: CONSUMED_BASIS_RETENTION_NOTE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: A consumed basis is established solely by the committed EVIDENCE_REF of the funding shift; no separate consumption field is written and no schema change is required.

IN_LOOP_BEHAVIOR:
- STATE_COMMIT_AUDIT is wiped after STATE_GOVERNANCE_AND_COMMIT_POST_VERIFICATION_PASS, so prior-cycle bases are not re-readable within the loop.
- Admission therefore relies on lawful self-reported declaration of fresh accumulation, and this rule is advisory at runtime.

OUT_OF_LOOP_BEHAVIOR:
- The same committed EVIDENCE_REF stream reconstructs every consumed basis per field deterministically.
- An external governor that retains the stream enforces this rule as a hard gate with no added state and no schema change.

LIFT_SPECIFICATION:
- This note is the lift specification. The consumed basis is the EVIDENCE_REF history; retention of that history is the entire delta between advisory in-loop enforcement and hard external enforcement.

=== SECTION: SHIFT_TRIGGERS ===

ON_TRAUMATIC_EVENT:
- Prefer acute dissonance/worldview-destabilization overlay.
- Durable field mutation requires direct challenge to identity-relevant worldview assumption.
- Single-event major shift requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT proof.

ON_MIRACLE_OR_HIGHLY_ANOMALOUS_EXPERIENCE:
- Prefer dissonance overlay first.
- Baseline accommodation requires persistence cluster or unusually strong narrative support.
- Single anomalous cue ordinarily does not rewrite physical, spiritual, or epistemic baselines.

ON_BETRAYAL:
- May justify bounded SET to trust-, agency-, human-nature-, or justice-related fields when direct evidence is salient.
- Belief shift and relational valence shift are separate proposals with independent justification.

ON_REPEATED_EVIDENCE:
- Initial resistance is expected.
- Persistence cluster is the normal accommodation path.
- Accommodation is field-specific.

ON_EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_WITH_HIGH_SALIENCE_MEMORY:
- EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE may allow multi-axis shift only with full proof.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: DEFENSE_MECHANISMS ===

ON_THREAT_TO_CORE_BELIEF:
- Prefer reinforcement, rationalization pressure, WORLDVIEW_DEFENSE overlay, or BELIEF_DISSONANCE overlay per CONTRADICTION_MARKER_EMISSION.
- Defensive rationalization may generate impulses or modifiers.
- Defensive rationalization is not COMMITTED_FACT evidence.
- If contradiction persists across cycles, persistence cluster handles bounded accommodation.

=== SECTION: CATEGORICAL_FIELD_TRANSITION_MAPS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Listed transitions are lawful when cue support and required persistence/override conditions are met. Unlisted transitions require override proof.

SIMULATION_THEORY [CONSIDERED, REJECTED, UNKNOWN]:
- UNKNOWN → CONSIDERED: concept introduced and engaged.
- UNKNOWN → REJECTED: dismissal after exposure.
- CONSIDERED → REJECTED: evaluated and dismissed.
- REJECTED → CONSIDERED: anomalous evidence reopens possibility.
- CONSIDERED → UNKNOWN: requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT plus compartmentalization/dissociation cue.
- REJECTED → UNKNOWN: requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT plus compartmentalization/dissociation cue.

MAGICAL_THINKING [PRESENT, ABSENT, CONTEXTUAL]:
- ABSENT → CONTEXTUAL: situational/cultural magical framing adopted.
- CONTEXTUAL → PRESENT: contextual framing generalizes under repeated reinforcement.
- PRESENT → CONTEXTUAL: retreat to bounded acceptance after disconfirmation.
- CONTEXTUAL → ABSENT: contextual cases rationalized away after strong empirical/skepticism cue.
- PRESENT → ABSENT: full rejection; requires persistence cluster or override proof.
- ABSENT → PRESENT: full conversion; requires override proof.

LOCUS_OF_CONTROL [INTERNAL, EXTERNAL, BALANCED]:
- INTERNAL → BALANCED: external factors gain explanatory weight.
- EXTERNAL → BALANCED: personal agency gains explanatory weight.
- BALANCED → INTERNAL: sustained agency-confirming cues.
- BALANCED → EXTERNAL: sustained helplessness/systemic-force cues.
- INTERNAL → EXTERNAL: polar shift; requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT.
- EXTERNAL → INTERNAL: polar shift; requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT.

HUMAN_NATURE [INHERENTLY_GOOD, INHERENTLY_EVIL, TABULA_RASA, CONTEXTUAL]:
- INHERENTLY_GOOD → CONTEXTUAL: trusted-bad behavior erodes universal optimism.
- INHERENTLY_EVIL → CONTEXTUAL: altruism erodes universal cynicism.
- TABULA_RASA → CONTEXTUAL: situational dependency added.
- CONTEXTUAL → INHERENTLY_GOOD: persistent universal prosocial evidence.
- CONTEXTUAL → INHERENTLY_EVIL: persistent universal self-interest evidence.
- CONTEXTUAL → TABULA_RASA: retreat to pure environmental framing.
- INHERENTLY_GOOD → TABULA_RASA: innate-goodness shifts to environmentalism.
- INHERENTLY_EVIL → TABULA_RASA: innate-evil shifts to environmentalism.
- INHERENTLY_GOOD → INHERENTLY_EVIL: polar shift; requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT; add EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE if concurrent multi-axis reorganization.
- INHERENTLY_EVIL → INHERENTLY_GOOD: polar shift; requires WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT; add EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE if concurrent multi-axis reorganization.

SUCCESS_ATTRIBUTION [INTERNAL_STABLE, INTERNAL_UNSTABLE, EXTERNAL]:
- INTERNAL_STABLE → INTERNAL_UNSTABLE: reliability of capability doubted.
- INTERNAL_UNSTABLE → INTERNAL_STABLE: consistent competence evidenced.
- INTERNAL_STABLE → EXTERNAL: credit externalized by strong luck/circumstance cue.
- INTERNAL_UNSTABLE → EXTERNAL: internal attribution abandoned after repeated failure-adjacent cues.
- EXTERNAL → INTERNAL_UNSTABLE: partial self-credit after efficacy cue.
- EXTERNAL → INTERNAL_STABLE: stable self-credit; requires persistence cluster or override proof.

FAILURE_ATTRIBUTION [INTERNAL_STABLE, INTERNAL_UNSTABLE, EXTERNAL]:
- INTERNAL_STABLE → INTERNAL_UNSTABLE: enduring-fault belief questioned.
- INTERNAL_UNSTABLE → INTERNAL_STABLE: stable self-blame consolidates.
- INTERNAL_STABLE → EXTERNAL: blame externalized after betrayal, injustice, or systemic obstacle.
- INTERNAL_UNSTABLE → EXTERNAL: self-attribution abandoned under sustained unfairness/systemic cue.
- EXTERNAL → INTERNAL_UNSTABLE: partial responsibility accepted.
- EXTERNAL → INTERNAL_STABLE: stable internalization of fault; requires persistence cluster or override proof.

=== SECTION: OVERRIDE_APPLICABILITY ===

WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT:
- Required for large identity-relevant mutation in one belief field.
- Authorizes field-level shift when full proof is present.

EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE:
- Required in addition to WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT when shift is part of concurrent multi-axis worldview reorganization.
- Authorizes multi-axis scope.

SPECIFIC_NOTES:
- LOCUS_OF_CONTROL polar shifts require WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT; add EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE only if multi-axis reorganization is independently justified.
- HUMAN_NATURE polar shifts always require WATERSHED_BELIEF_SHIFT; add EXISTENTIAL_CRISIS_OVERRIDE when concurrent multi-axis collapse/reorganization occurs.
- Persistence evidence alone does not authorize unlisted categorical transitions.

=== SECTION: GOVERNANCE_COMPATIBILITY_NOTE ===

- Belief shifts resolve to explicit lawful field values.
- Temporary modifiers absorb acute contradiction before durable fields shift.
- Matrix-band contradictions carry a structured marker per SCHEME_BELIEF_SYSTEMS.
- Persistence-triggered ordinal shifts are one adjacent movement.
- Persistence-triggered categorical shifts use named mapped targets.
- Multi-field worldview reorganization requires override proof and remains rare.
- Consumed-basis admission is advisory in-loop and hard under external EVIDENCE_REF retention per CONSUMED_BASIS_RETENTION_NOTE.
