=== SECTION: MECHANISM_DEFINITIONS ===

ACTIVE_COPING: Acting directly on the stressor.
PLANNING: Working out how to handle the stressor before acting.
SEEKING_SUPPORT: Turning to others for help, comfort, or advice.
ACCEPTANCE: Coming to terms with what cannot be changed.
HUMOR: Making light of the stressor to keep it bearable.
RELIGIOUS_COPING: Drawing on faith, prayer, ritual, or spiritual meaning.
DISTRACTION: Turning attention to other activities to avoid dwelling on the stressor.
DENIAL: Refusing to accept that the stressor is real or serious.
SELF_BLAME: Turning blame inward and criticizing oneself for the stressor.
BEHAVIORAL_DISENGAGEMENT: Giving up on dealing with the stressor.
SUBSTANCE_USE: Using alcohol or drugs to feel better or get through it.
VENTING: Expressing distress outwardly to release it.

MALADAPTIVE_MECHANISMS:
- DENIAL
- SELF_BLAME
- BEHAVIORAL_DISENGAGEMENT
- SUBSTANCE_USE
- VENTING

=== SECTION: INVARIANTS ===

- PRIMARY_MECHANISM and SECONDARY_MECHANISM should differ unless justified override.
- Probability bands are independent and do not require sum-to-total constraints.
- SUBSTANCE_USE_RISK escalates on explicit event evidence per DYNAMICS_COPE.
- Every mechanism in the PRIMARY_MECHANISM enum has exactly one matching probability bias field.
- Maladaptive escalation applies only to MALADAPTIVE_MECHANISMS.
- Selection ties resolved deterministically per DYNAMICS_COPE.COPE_SELECTION_RULE.
