=== SECTION: SKILL_CHANGE_THRESHOLD_POLICY ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Permanent skill mutation is rare.

PERMANENT_SKILL_CHANGE_GATE:
A permanent skill transition is warranted only when at least one is true:
- Deliberate practice is explicit, sustained, and skill-relevant.
- Breakthrough event is memory-linked and salience-supported.
- Repeated successful execution shows durable automation beyond one scene.
- Extended non-use plausibly degrades retained ability.
- Training, instruction, adaptation, injury, alteration, or technology changes durable baseline.

NON_CHANGE_CASES:
- One ordinary success.
- One ordinary failure.
- Stress/fatigue underperformance.
- Environmental difficulty.
- Temporary boost from alteration, stimulant, flow, or assistance.
- Luck or outcome success without skill-change evidence.

=== SECTION: INSTINCT_AUTOMATION ===

ON_REPEATED_SUCCESSFUL_EXECUTION:
- If mastery threshold and durable automation support exist:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][INSTINCT_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_UP
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: repeated successful execution supports automation
- Must preserve INSTINCT_LEVEL <= MASTERY_LEVEL.

AUTOMATION_GATE:
- Requires more than one isolated success.
- Support may come from memory, training history, repeated scene pattern, or explicit practice.

=== SECTION: ATROPHY_AND_PLATEAU ===

ON_EXTENDED_NON_USE:
- If meaningful enough to affect durable capability:
  - PROPOSE TRANSITION:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][MASTERY_LEVEL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: ONE_STEP_DOWN
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: extended non-use supports bounded atrophy
- Optionally PROPOSE TRANSITION on GROWTH_POTENTIAL ONE_STEP_DOWN for stagnation.

ATROPHY_GATE:
- Requires major elapsed time, practice deprivation, impairment, loss of access, injury, alteration, or explicit cue.
- Ordinary time passage without skill relevance is insufficient.

ON_PLATEAU_REACHED:
- If mastery is top bound or practice no longer progresses:
  - PROPOSE SET:
    TARGET_FIELD: [STATE_SKILLS][SKL_XX][GROWTH_POTENTIAL]
    PROPOSED_VALUE: PLATEAUED
    MECHANISM_TAG: SKILL_STATE_CHANGE
    JUSTIFICATION: plateau reached
