OTTC (On-The-Top Constraint) Systems — Academic ML / LLM Research Rendering

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: ABSTRACT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This section presents research hypotheses, not completed efficacy findings.

OTTC proposes a constitutional architecture for durable, consequence-bearing state in opaque generative systems. It separates stochastic proposals from authorized commits using canonical addresses, a closed mutation vocabulary, single-writer state authority, bounded transitions, epistemic-status typing, accountable audit closure, and post-commit verification. In Stage 1 these functions are rendered as language-native governance; Stages 2 and 3 progressively move durable authority and checks outside the generator. The **testable hypothesis**, not an established result, is that qualified OTTC implementations can reduce unauthorized state mutation, epistemic overreach, and unjustified long-horizon drift compared with relevant baselines while retaining lawful responsiveness. ChaOS, a psychologically informed narrative character architecture, is the worked single-square example, not a cross-domain performance qualification. The ten-invariant kernel is prescribed; its claimed irreducible minimality remains ARGUED, NOT YET CONFIRMED pending deletion experiments.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: MOTIVATION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This section defines the motivation and the epistemic scope of the research.

Long-running LLM agents may lose stable referents, mutate persistent state without explicit authority, treat inference as fact, or present plausible audit records unsupported by the operations actually performed. Ordinary transcript inspection can leave the boundary between tentative generation and durable consequence underspecified. The Mutation Boundary Problem asks what lawful conditions must hold before proposed semantic content changes shared state. Both **egress** (proposals becoming commitments) and **ingress** (external framing changing what the evaluator treats as evidence) are research targets. A taxonomy of failures is not evidence that one proposed solution already outperforms alternatives.

=== SECTION: OTTC_CORE_CONSTRUCT ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This section describes the proposed constitutional functions, without confusing functional necessity with measured efficacy.

OTTC prescribes TEN kernel invariants, distinguishing the nine-invariant governability floor from the tenth's epistemic function:

1) CANONICAL_ADDRESS_SPACE — one canonical address for each commit-worthy state target.
2) PROPOSE_COMMIT_SEPARATION — reasoning modules propose; commits are separate accountable events.
3) SINGLE_WRITER_PERSISTENT_STATE — only the designated writer may alter persistent state per cycle, including restricted post-verification invocation of that same writer.
4) EXPLICIT_UPDATE_OPERATIONS — a closed operation vocabulary, with unlawful aliases rejected.
5) HARD_STATE_VS_SOFT_MODIFIERS — durable baseline distinguished from time-bounded overlays.
6) STEP_LIMITS_OVERRIDE_TRIGGERS — bounded updates with lawful exception evidence.
7) DETERMINISTIC_CONFLICT_RESOLUTION — stated precedence for competing proposals.
8) AUDIT_CLOSURE — each proposal receives exactly one accountable disposition; no silent drops or manufactured reasons.
9) POST_COMMIT_VERIFICATION — independent, fallible checking of committed effects and invariants; the verifier cannot unilaterally write persistent state.
10) EPISTEMIC_STATUS_TYPING — each registered proposal uses the closed EPISTEMIC_TAG vocabulary: PERCEPTION, INFERENCE, SPECULATION, COMMITTED_FACT, TOOL_REQUEST. A COMMITTED_FACT claim requires verifiable evidence linkage.

The first nine principally govern how and by whom state changes. The tenth governs **the epistemic standing asserted for claims supporting such changes**. A source identifier (provenance), source authorization to disclose, supporting evidence, and epistemic status are distinct fields or decisions that must be checked together. Authorized transport of a statement does not entitle the receiver to upgrade inference into fact.

The framework treats all ten as normative for an OTTC-compliant serious build. Its strongest necessity argument for the tenth concerns mixed-epistemic durable state and predictive-falsificationist cycles. A consequential single-mode workflow is not intrinsically trivial; the argument does not establish universal indispensability in every such system. Irreducible minimality is a separate, falsifiable hypothesis.

A predictive ledger is a possible *square-specific module*, not an additional required kernel invariant. Epistemic tags alone do not implement predictive processing or Popperian falsification. Those claims require actual predictions, evidence able to contradict them, lawful revision, and reproducible attribution of error.

=== SECTION: RESEARCH_CONTRIBUTIONS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Keep structural proposals, testable hypotheses, and measured results epistemically distinct.

COMPLIANCE_AND_MINIMALITY:
- OTTC's current prescribed kernel contains ten invariants.
- Nine form the argued governability floor; the tenth is argued necessary where differently warranted claims affect durable state.
- Minimality status: ARGUED, NOT YET CONFIRMED (OTTC/10_OTTC_TESTABLE_HYPOTHESIS). Calling the architecture minimal before ablation would turn a hypothesis into a finding.

1) Constitutional conditioning and lawful responsiveness
Hypothesis: a qualified constitution reduces invalid proposals or unlawful commits under defined conditions compared with matched baselines.
Measure: illegal-alias incidence, canonical-address errors, write-surface violations, erroneous refusals of mandatory lawful transitions, valid-change completion, and correct recovery. Low mutation volume alone is not success.

2) Epistemic attribution and hypothesis testing
Hypothesis: typed proposal claims permit more reproducible diagnosis of misperception, inference failure, speculation, and unsupported fact promotion than otherwise comparable untyped architectures.
Measure: truthful EPISTEMIC_TAG assignment, evidence-linkage quality, attribution of prediction errors, valid refutation handling, and revisions following contradicting evidence. Epistemic status must not be reconstructed uncritically from source provenance.

3) Auditable governance and independent verification
Hypothesis: closed proposal dispositions and separate verification improve replayability and detection of improper commits, omissions, and pretextual suppression.
Measure: audit completeness, reason fidelity, verifier error detection, false rejections and false acceptances, trace-to-state agreement, and restoration after governed faults. Audit documents may be theatrical; their correctness is itself a test.

4) Ingress and long-horizon effects
Hypothesis: framing at the input boundary can alter evaluation before a claim is lawfully admitted, and some individually permitted transitions may accumulate into unjustified directional bias.
Measure: matched neutral/positive/negative/hostile-framing conditions; repeated-turn exposure; authorized versus unauthorized evidence uptake; longitudinal change conditioned on actual domain reconciliation duties; and lawful responsiveness. The proposed semantic-to-numeric drift instrument is not a confirmed thermodynamic entropy-production measurement.

5) Ontology translation and federation
Hypothesis: incomplete domain-law rendering or tacit completion produces consequential gaps not exposed by an apparently coherent transcript.
Test whether experts and uninformed reviewers can locate operative rules in the artifact itself. Separately qualify any proposed two-authority federation: source disclosure authority, negotiated mapping, receiving admissibility, independent commit authority, and audit across the handoff. Tabletop single-DM practice is a human precedent, not evidence that the sovereign-separation axis has been validated.

=== SECTION: DOMAIN_ONTOLOGY_AND_RECTANGLE_SQUARE ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Preserve the square-first origin and the distinction between expert authority and model assistance.

STRUCTURAL_CLAIM:
ChaOS was developed first as a narrative character square; nine generalized governance candidates were extracted after its responsibilities were separated into constitutional, kernel, scheme, cognition, dynamics, state, and volatile layers. The tenth was added later. Parallel subsequent refinement does not imply parallel origination. OTTC's rectangle states common functions, not the ontology or lawful transitions of a clinical, legal, scientific, or narrative square.

RESEARCH_IMPLICATION:
Domain experts must specify consequential surfaces, admissibility, exceptions, standing, and proper use. "Tacit completion" occurs when an experienced founder unconsciously supplies a missing rule while reading their own specification. A review must identify the operative rule *in the artifact*, not infer it from the founder's knowledge. An LLM can assist as a proposer without acquiring constitutional authority.

OPENNESS_CLAIM:
New domain squares and federations are prospective research objects. Cross-domain transfer additionally requires source-side permission and receiving-side epistemic admission. Their feasibility and safety are not established by the ChaOS narrative example.

COLLABORATION_SCAFFOLD_CLAIM:
Plural-model critique and human expert commits are a candidate method for discovering the domain's lawful structure. Model agreement is not qualification, and a prior built square is not retroactive evidence that a blind pre-build gate was passed.

=== SECTION: EXPERIMENTAL_SETUP ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Preserve assay chronology, distinct experimental purposes, and falsification conditions.

PHASE_0: Kernel precheck — confirm ten prescribed invariants without quietly treating epistemic typing as an optional enhancement.
PHASE_1: Blind, fresh-session, minimal-law, **pre-build** qualification of the candidate model/role/language surface, in order: Canonical Address Obedience (CAA), Write-Surface Discipline (WSD), Epistemic POV Integrity (EPI), Audit Non-Theater (ANT). Use the assay's own locally binding approve and reject cases. Record actual prompts, scores, failures, model identity, and operating language. "Blind" does not guarantee the model cannot recognize an evaluation.
PHASE_2: GO/NO-GO decision. If NO-GO, do not proceed to new ontology, governance, or COT machine design under the failed role assumptions.
PHASE_3: If GO, render the domain ontology and lawful schema, model-facing constitutional interface, authorities, execution order, and risk-appropriate external enforcement.
PHASE_4: Post-build conditioned regression testing (distinct from Phase 1, never relabeled as blind qualification); longer-horizon scenario evaluations when warranted.
PHASE_5: Stage 2/3 migration and retesting of both correct external rejection *and* acceptance of obligatory lawful work, including recovery paths.

INDEPENDENT_MINIMALITY_EXPERIMENT:
- For each of the ten invariants, remove only that invariant while preserving the other nine; compare matched mixed-epistemic, long-horizon tasks to the intact kernel.
- **Include deletion of epistemic-status typing**. Test whether the remaining architecture can still attribute, refute, and lawfully correct prediction errors without the tag.
- If a smaller set passes the necessary predictive-falsificationist requirements, the ten-invariant minimality hypothesis is refuted.
- An intact-kernel pass does not prove minimality. Separate this deletion battery from the model-coupling assay, and avoid post-hoc selection of only favorable runs.

Collect operation/epistemic violations, valid-transition completion, audit and evidence fidelity, unwanted refusals, drift under lawful change, and cross-condition reproducibility. Use appropriate baselines; do not substitute one short trace or an interpretability correlate for long-horizon outcomes.

=== SECTION: LIMITATIONS ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: This section states the live limits of OTTC, including the limits of instruments.

- The strongest coupling, reliability, drift, and minimality claims are unconfirmed; existing reviews do not count retroactively as blind qualification.
- In-model enforcement remains stochastic. Stage 3 can deterministically enforce *specified* state/operation boundaries but cannot certify unformalized semantics or automatically establish truth.
- Evidence tags describe asserted standing and must be checked; recorded provenance alone does not prove warrant.
- Blind minimal conditioning mitigates some evaluation confounds without proving lack of evaluation awareness.
- Interpretability may contribute mechanistic evidence, not privileged commit authority or guaranteed deception detection.
- Long-horizon tests can still miss failures; safety cannot be inferred from stability achieved by rejecting all changes.
- Domain law and permissions may vary by institution; cross-square sovereignty and ingress remain separate unqualified axes.
- Model/language dependence, context costs, maintenance, technical collaboration, and authority legitimacy are ongoing constraints.

=== SECTION: CONCLUSION ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Close with an accountable research claim rather than a promised outcome.

OTTC offers a falsifiable, architecture-level proposal for managing the passage from generative possibility into durable, consequential state. The first nine functions govern proposed changes, while the tenth preserves the declared epistemic standing of their supporting claims without merging status, origin, authorization, and warrant. The evidence program must determine whether this improves actual systems and whether any function can be removed without loss. A coherent constitution is a research object; it is not its own validation.
